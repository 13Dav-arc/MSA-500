# Third Term Terminal Examination: Agricultural Science

This comprehensive terminal examination rigorously evaluates student knowledge, analytical skills, and practical problem-solving across the Third Term curriculum:
- **Week 01:** Definition and Characteristics of Weeds (Plant out of place, survival adaptations, seed dormancy, and binomial nomenclature).
- **Week 02:** Classification and Dispersal of Weeds (Habitat, leaf morphology, life cycles, and dispersal agents: wind, water, animals, explosive).
- **Week 03:** Economic Importance, Harmful Effects, and Beneficial Uses of Weeds (Resource competition, critical period, yield loss, reservoir hosts, allelopathy, and positive utilities).
- **Week 04:** Methods of Weed Control (Cultural, mechanical, biological, and chemical methods; herbicide selectivity, environmental hazards, and Integrated Weed Management).
- **Midterm:** Midterm Diagnostic Synthesis of Weeks 01–04.
- **Week 06:** Meaning, Types, and Classification of Crop Pests (Insect vs. non-insect pests; mouthparts: biting/chewing, piercing/sucking, boring).
- **Week 07:** Damage Done by Pests and Their Effects on Crop Yield (Defoliation, dead hearts, root galls, frass, post-harvest losses, aflatoxins, and revenue collapse).
- **Week 08:** Methods of Pest Control (Physical, cultural, biological, and chemical methods; IPM, hermetic storage, PPE, and agrochemical safety).
- **Week 09:** Factors of Agricultural Production (Land, Labor, Capital, and Management; characteristics, fixed vs. working capital, and economic rewards).

## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: In agricultural science, what is the most concise definition of a weed?
A) Any plant with sweet edible fruits
B) A plant out of place (correct)
C) A tree grown for building construction
D) A crop cultivated in organized rows
Feedback: A weed is traditionally defined as "a plant out of place"—growing where it is not desired.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the botanical name of Spear grass, a notorious perennial weed with sharp underground rhizomes?
A) *Panicum maximum*
B) *Imperata cylindrica* (correct)
C) *Ageratum conyzoides*
D) *Talinum triangulare*
Feedback: *Imperata cylindrica* is the botanical name of Spear grass.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: Which anatomical feature distinguishes sedges like Nut grass (*Cyperus rotundus*) from true grasses?
A) Sedges possess round, hollow stems
B) Sedges possess solid, three-sided (triangular) stems (correct)
C) Sedges produce wooden fruits
D) Sedges have giant blue petals
Feedback: Sedges have solid, triangular (three-sided) stems, whereas grasses have hollow, round stems.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the botanical name of Goat weed, a common annual broad-leaved weed in Nigeria?
A) *Imperata cylindrica*
B) *Ageratum conyzoides* (correct)
C) *Chromolaena odorata*
D) *Amaranthus spinosus*
Feedback: *Ageratum conyzoides* is the scientific name of Goat weed.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: What biological term describes the phenomenon where weeds secrete biochemical toxins into the soil that inhibit the growth of neighboring crops?
A) Allelopathy (correct)
B) Osmosis
C) Parturition
D) Rumination
Feedback: Allelopathy is the release of inhibitory biochemical substances by one plant that harms another.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: The survival adaptation where weed seeds remain viable in the soil for years or decades without germinating until disturbed is:
A) Seed sterility
B) Seed dormancy (correct)
C) Respiration
D) Fallow cycle
Feedback: Seed dormancy allows weed seeds to survive adverse conditions in the soil seedbank.

### Q7 [single-choice]

A) Narrow-leaved grass weeds
B) Broad-leaved weeds (Dicots) (correct)

Feedback: Broad-leaved dicot weeds possess wide leaf blades, net venation, and taproot systems.

### Q8 [single-choice]

A) Waterleaf
B) Water hyacinth (*Eichhornia crassipes*) (correct)

### Q9 [single-choice]

A) Biennial weeds
B) Annual weeds (correct)
C) Perennial weeds

Feedback: Annual weeds complete their entire life cycle in a single growing season or under one year.

### Q10 [single-choice]

A) Hard cloven hooves
B) Pappus (parachute of fine hairs) (correct)

### Q11 [single-choice]

### Q12 [single-choice]

### Q24 [single-choice]

### Q36 [true-false]
Stem: Slicing off the surface foliage of Spear grass with a hand hoe permanently eradicates it because grasses have no underground structures.
A) True
B) False (correct)
Feedback: False. Spear grass regenerates rapidly from food-rich underground rhizomes buried beneath the soil surface.

### Q37 [true-false]
Stem: Hermetic storage in PICS bags kills stored grain weevils naturally by creating an airtight environment where oxygen is depleted and carbon dioxide rises.
A) True (correct)
B) False
Feedback: True. PICS bags seal out atmospheric oxygen, suffocating weevil eggs, larvae, and adults naturally.

### Q38 [true-false]
Stem: Dry cowpea beans with circular weevil holes can be planted with full confidence that 100% of the seeds will germinate into vigorous plants.
A) True
B) False (correct)
Feedback: False. Weevil larvae consume the vital seed embryo; perforated seeds lose viability and fail to germinate.

### Q39 [true-false]
Stem: Land can be manufactured and physically doubled in geographic size whenever human beings require more space for farming.
A) True
B) False (correct)
Feedback: False. Land is fixed in supply; humans can improve its fertility, but cannot expand total geographic territory.

### Q40 [true-false]
Stem: Integrated Weed Management (IWM) strategically combines cultural, mechanical, biological, and chemical methods to achieve sustainable weed suppression.
A) True (correct)
B) False
Feedback: True. IWM integrates diverse control practices to achieve long-term, eco-friendly weed suppression.
