# Second Term Midterm Continuous Assessment Review

This diagnostic review evaluates student mastery across the foundational livestock units covered during Weeks 01 through 04 of the Second Term:
- **Week 01:** Meaning, Types, and General Characteristics of Farm Animals (Work, dairy, meat, poultry, aquatic, and guard animals).
- **Week 02:** Characteristic Features of Major Livestock (Cattle, sheep, goats, pigs, rabbits, and poultry; breeds, gestation, and incubation).
- **Week 03:** Geographic Distribution of Livestock in Nigeria (Northern savanna dominance, tsetse fly belt, Trypanosomiasis/Nagana, and trypanotolerant breeds).
- **Week 04:** Classification of Farm Animals Based on Size and Habitat (Large stock vs. small stock; terrestrial vs. aquatic adaptations).

---

## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: What biological process describes the historical taming and captive breeding of wild animals by humans for food, work, and companionship?
A) Transhumance
B) Domestication (correct)
C) Deforestation
D) Afforestation
Feedback: Domestication is the progressive adaptation of wild animals to live, breed, and work under human management.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Which Nigerian cattle breed is known as *Bunaji* and serves as the nation's premier dual-purpose breed for both milk and beef?
A) Muturu
B) White Fulani (correct)
C) N'Dama
D) Keteku
Feedback: The White Fulani (*Bunaji*) is the most widespread dual-purpose cattle breed in Nigeria.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the gestation period of a female domestic pig (sow)?
A) 283 days
B) 150 days
C) 114 days (3 months, 3 weeks, and 3 days) (correct)
D) 30 days
Feedback: Pig gestation is exactly 114 days (3 months, 3 weeks, 3 days).

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following is a cloven-hoofed, humpless dwarf cattle breed indigenous to Southern Nigeria that possesses natural resistance to sleeping sickness?
A) Muturu (correct)
B) Sokoto Gudali
C) Red Bororo
D) Wadara
Feedback: Muturu is a dwarf, humpless taurine breed native to Southern Nigeria renowned for genetic trypanotolerance.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the primary difference in feeding behavior between domestic goats and sheep?
A) Goats eat fish, while sheep eat grain
B) Goats are agile browsers that feed on shrubs and leaves with tails upright, while sheep are grazers feeding on low grass with tails hanging down (correct)
C) Sheep climb trees, while goats dig underground tunnels
D) Sheep drink milk as adults, while goats only drink saltwater
Feedback: Goats browse shrubs and hold tails erect; sheep graze pasture grasses and hold tails downward.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: The duration required for fertile chicken eggs to hatch under proper incubation temperature is:
A) 14 days
B) 21 days (correct)
C) 28 days
D) 35 days
Feedback: Domestic fowl eggs have an incubation period of 21 days.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: Why are over 85% of Nigeria's cattle and small ruminants concentrated in the Northern Savanna belts?
A) Northern soil contains natural salt licks everywhere
B) Vast open grasslands provide abundant forage, and low humidity eliminates the humid breeding habitat of tsetse flies (correct)
C) Ruminants can only survive in sub-zero freezing climates
D) Commercial feed is illegal in Southern Nigeria
Feedback: Open savannas provide extensive grazing, and the dry climate prevents dense tsetse fly infestation.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: What insect vector transmits the protozoan parasite that causes Trypanosomiasis (Nagana) in Nigerian cattle?
A) Housefly
B) Mosquito
C) Tsetse fly (*Glossina*) (correct)
D) Black fly
Feedback: The tsetse fly (*Glossina*) is the biological vector transmitting *Trypanosoma* parasites to cattle.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following farm animals is categorized under large stock?
A) Landrace pig
B) Domestic rabbit
C) Bullock / Ox (correct)
D) Broiler chicken
Feedback: Bullocks (cattle) are large stock; pigs, rabbits, and chickens are classified as small stock.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: What respiratory organs allow aquatic animals like Nile tilapia and African catfish to extract dissolved oxygen from water?
A) Lungs
B) Gills (correct)
C) Trachea
D) Gizzard
Feedback: Aquatic fish absorb dissolved oxygen from water using gills.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: The gestation period of both sheep (ewes) and goats (does) is approximately:
A) 30 days
B) 114 days
C) 150 days (approx. 5 months) (correct)
D) 283 days
Feedback: Sheep and goats both have a gestation period of approximately 150 days (5 months).

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: Which sheep breed in Northern Nigeria is famous for its distinctive bi-coloration—front half dark and rear half white?
A) Yankasa
B) Ouda (correct)
C) Balami
D) West African Dwarf
Feedback: The Ouda sheep is easily identified by its contrasting two-tone coat (dark front, white rear).

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: What specialized grooming gland located near the tail base of ducks and geese secretes waterproofing oil for their plumage?
A) Preen gland (uropygial gland) (correct)
B) Mammary gland
C) Salivary gland
D) Pancreas
Feedback: The preen (uropygial) gland secretes oil that waterfowl use to waterproof their feathers.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: An entrepreneur with limited backyard space and modest startup capital would be best advised to invest in which category of livestock?
A) Large stock (e.g., dairy cattle ranching)
B) Small stock (e.g., rabbit or poultry farming) (correct)
C) Camels and pack horses
D) Forest wildlife hunting
Feedback: Small stock like rabbits and poultry require small space and modest initial capital, ideal for backyards.

### Q15 [true-false]
Stem: Zebu cattle breeds like White Fulani and Red Bororo possess humps and are highly resistant to trypanosomiasis in humid rainforests.
A) True
B) False (correct)
Feedback: False. Zebu cattle are humped but highly susceptible to trypanosomiasis in humid southern zones.

### Q16 [true-false]
Stem: In livestock taxonomy, domestic swine (pigs) are classified as small stock alongside sheep, goats, and rabbits.
A) True (correct)
B) False
Feedback: True. Swine are categorized as small stock in agricultural classification.

### Q17 [multi-select]
Stem: Which of the following breeds of livestock are known for natural trypanotolerance in Southern Nigeria? (Select all that apply)
A) Muturu cattle (correct)
B) West African Dwarf goat (correct)
C) N'Dama cattle (correct)
D) White Fulani cattle
Feedback: Muturu, N'Dama, and WAD goats are trypanotolerant. White Fulani cattle are susceptible.

### Q18 [multi-select]
Stem: Which of the following animals are classified as small stock? (Select all that apply)
A) Domestic rabbit (correct)
B) Layer chicken (correct)
C) West African Dwarf goat (correct)
D) Camel
Feedback: Rabbits, chickens, and goats are small stock. Camels are large stock.

### Q19 [matching]
Stem: Match each farm animal with its correct gestation or incubation period:
- Cow: Approximately 283 days (~9 months)
- Sow (Pig): Exactly 114 days (3m, 3w, 3d)
- Doe (Goat): Approximately 150 days (~5 months)
- Doe (Rabbit): Exactly 30 to 31 days
Feedback: Cow is ~283 days; pig is 114 days; goat is ~150 days; rabbit is 30–31 days.

### Q20 [matching]
Stem: Match each livestock type with its primary functional classification:
- Bullock: Work / Draught Animal
- White Fulani Cow: Dairy Animal
- African Catfish: Aquatic Farm Animal
- Broiler: Meat Poultry
Feedback: Bullocks are work animals; White Fulani cows produce dairy; catfish are aquatic; and broilers are meat poultry.
