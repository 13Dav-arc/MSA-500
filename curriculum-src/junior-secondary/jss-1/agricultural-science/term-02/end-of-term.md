# Second Term Terminal Examination: Agricultural Science

This comprehensive terminal examination rigorously evaluates student knowledge, analytical understanding, and practical competencies across the complete Second Term curriculum:
- **Week 01:** Meaning, Types, and General Characteristics of Farm Animals (Work, dairy, meat, poultry, aquatic, and guard animals).
- **Week 02:** Characteristic Features of Major Livestock (Bovine, ovine, caprine, porcine, cuniculture, and avian; breeds, gestation, and incubation).
- **Week 03:** Geographic Distribution of Livestock in Nigeria (Northern savanna dominance, tsetse fly belt, Trypanosomiasis/Nagana, and trypanotolerant breeds).
- **Week 04:** Classification of Farm Animals Based on Size and Habitat (Large stock vs. small stock; terrestrial vs. aquatic adaptations).
- **Midterm:** Midterm Diagnostic Synthesis of Weeks 01–04.
- **Week 06:** Classification Based on Mode of Reproduction (Viviparous mammals, oviparous birds/fish, parturition terminology, and colostrum).
- **Week 07:** Classification Based on Stomach Type and Digestion (Ruminants vs. monogastrics, 4 stomach chambers, avian digestion, coprophagy).
- **Week 08:** Uses and Economic Importance of Farm Animals (Food products, industrial by-products, draught power, soil fertility, and socio-cultural value).
- **Week 09:** General Care, Housing, Nutrition, and Health Management of Farm Animals (Management systems, roughages vs. concentrates, biosecurity, and health).

## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the primary biological definition of farm animals?
A) Any wild animals captured in the forest for wildlife tourism
B) Domesticated animals reared by humans for food, work, raw materials, and economic profit (correct)
C) Pests that destroy agricultural crops in the field
D) Microscopic soil bacteria that decompose dead leaves
Feedback: Farm animals are domesticated species reared by humans for economic, nutritional, and practical benefits.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Which Nigerian cattle breed is known as *Bunaji* and serves as the primary dual-purpose breed for both milk and beef production?
A) White Fulani (correct)
B) Muturu
C) N'Dama
D) Keteku
Feedback: The White Fulani (*Bunaji*) is the most widespread dual-purpose cattle breed in Nigeria.

### Q5 [single-choice]
