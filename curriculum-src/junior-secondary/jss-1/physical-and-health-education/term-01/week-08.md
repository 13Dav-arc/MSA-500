## Hook
Imagine leaping high into the air at the net, staring down an opposing defense, and blasting a volleyball into their court at eighty kilometers per hour with a ferocious spike! Now switch courts to a wooden hardwood floor where sneakers squeak in rhythm: you execute a lightning-fast crossover dribble, bounce-pass past two reaching defenders to a teammate cutting toward the basket, who catches the ball in mid-stride and glides upward for an acrobatic layup off the glass into the ten-foot-high rim! Think of Nigerian basketball greatness—from the iconic NBA legend **Hakeem Olajuwon** to the reigning African women's champions **D'Tigress** lighting up the world stage! Welcome to the electrifying world of court ball games: **Volleyball and Basketball**!

## Key Terms
- Term: Volleyball
- Definition: A non-contact team court game played by two teams of six players separated by a high net, whose objective is to hit the ball over the net and ground it within the opponent's court.
- Term: Basketball
- Definition: A team court game played between two teams of five players each, whose objective is to score points by shooting a spherical ball through an elevated hoop and net (basket) 3.05 meters high.
- Term: The Dig (Forearm Pass)
- Definition: A defensive volleyball pass executed by locking the wrists and elbows together and contacting the ball with the flat fleshy surface of the forearms.
- Term: The Set (Overhead Volley)
- Definition: An overhead passing technique in volleyball where the player uses the soft pads of the fingers to loft the ball high and accurately for a teammate to spike.
- Term: Spike (Smash)
- Definition: An explosive attacking volleyball play where a jumping player strikes the ball with an open hand at the peak of their leap, driving it sharply down into the opponent's court.
- Term: Chest Pass
- Definition: A direct, crisp two-handed basketball pass delivered from the passer's chest straight to the receiver's chest with thumbs flicking downward and outwards.
- Term: Bounce Pass
- Definition: A tactical basketball pass snapped downward so that the ball rebounds off the floor approximately two-thirds of the distance toward the receiver.
- Term: Lay-Up Shot
- Definition: A close-range basketball shot executed on the run where the player leaps off one foot near the basket and banks the ball gently off the backboard into the ring.
- Term: Double Dribble
- Definition: An illegal basketball violation committed when a player dribbles with both hands simultaneously or stops dribbling and resumes dribbling again.
- Term: Traveling
- Definition: An illegal basketball violation committed when a player holding the ball moves one or both feet irregularly without dribbling.

## Core Concept
### Origins & Inventors of Court Games
Both volleyball and basketball were invented in the state of Massachusetts, USA, within four years of each other:
1. **Basketball (1891):** Invented by Canadian physical education instructor **Dr. James Naismith** in Springfield, Massachusetts, using two peach baskets nailed to a gymnasium balcony and a soccer ball to keep students active indoors during harsh winters.
2. **Volleyball (1895):** Invented by **William G. Morgan** in Holyoke, Massachusetts, as a less strenuous, non-contact recreational game for businessmen, initially christened *"Mintonette"*.

### Volleyball: Court, Rules & Fundamentals
- **Court Dimensions:** Rectangular, measuring **$18\text{ meters}$ long by $9\text{ meters}$ wide**, divided into two $9\text{m} \times 9\text{m}$ halves by a centerline and a net. An **Attack Line** is drawn $3\text{ meters}$ from the net on each side.
- **Net Height:** Standard regulation net height is $2.43\text{ meters}$ for men and $2.24\text{ meters}$ for women (calibrated slightly lower for junior secondary classes).
- **Team Size & Rotation:** 6 players per team on the court. When a receiving team wins a rally, its players rotate **one position clockwise** before serving!

```
                  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │           VOLLEYBALL COURT ROTATION           │
                  │   Position 4          Position 3   Position 2 │ (Front Row)
                  │   (Left-Front)        (Mid-Front)  (Right-Front)
                  │ ─────────────────── NET ───────────────────── │
                  │   Position 5          Position 6   Position 1 │ (Back Row)
                  │   (Left-Back)         (Mid-Back)   (SERVER)   │
                  │              ── Clockwise Rotation ──         │
                  └───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Core Volleyball Skills:
1. **The Service:** Putting the ball into play from behind the end line. Techniques include the Underhand Serve (safe for beginners), Overhand/Tennis Serve (aggressive spin), and Jump Serve.
2. **The Forearm Pass (Dig):** Used to receive hard serves and spikes. Hands are clasped, thumbs aligned side-by-side, elbows locked rigid, and knees bent to absorb and cushion the ball.
3. **The Overhead Pass (Set):** Shaping hands like a triangle above the forehead, contacting the ball gently with the finger pads to float it up for an attacker.
4. **The Spike & Block:** The spike drives the ball down violently; the defensive block features front-row players leaping with upright outstretched hands at the net to deflect the spike.
- **Rally Point Scoring:** The first team to score **25 points** (with at least a 2-point lead) wins a set. Matches are played to best of 3 or best of 5 sets. A team is allowed a maximum of **three touches** before returning the ball over the net.

### Basketball: Court, Rules & Fundamentals
- **Court Dimensions:** Standard FIBA court measures **$28\text{ meters}$ long by $15\text{ meters}$ wide**.
- **Ring Height:** The rim is suspended horizontally at exactly **$3.05\text{ meters}$ ($10\text{ feet}$)** above the floor.
- **Players on Court:** 5 active players per team (Point guard, Shooting guard, Small forward, Power forward, Center).

#### Core Basketball Skills:
1. **Dribbling:** Bouncing the ball continuously against the floor using the finger pads (not slapping with the palm!), keeping the head up and body low.
2. **Passing:**
   - *Chest Pass:* Quickest pass, snapping from chest to chest.
   - *Bounce Pass:* Snapped to hit the floor two-thirds of the distance to the teammate, ideal for slipping under the outstretched arms of defenders.
   - *Overhead Pass:* Held above the head with both hands, used for skip passes over a zone defense.
3. **Shooting:**
   - *Lay-up:* Approaching the rim, taking a legal two-step cadence, leaping high off one foot, and softly kissing the ball off the backboard.
   - *Jump Shot:* Leaping vertically into the air and releasing the ball at the highest point with high arch and backward wrist rotation.
   - *Free Throw:* An unguarded shot from the 4.60-meter foul line awarded after fouls, worth 1 point.
- **Scoring Breakdown:**
  - Field goal scored from inside the three-point arc = **2 points**.
  - Field goal scored from behind the three-point arc = **3 points**.
  - Free throw = **1 point**.
- **Key Violations:** Traveling, Double Dribble, Backcourt Violation (returning ball to backcourt once established in frontcourt), 24-second shot clock expiration.

## Common Mistakes

| Sport | Common Error | Why It Happens | Rule / Mechanical Correction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Volleyball** | **Catching or scooping the ball (Lift/Carry)** | Novices try to catch the ball to regain control. | Illegal violation; the ball must be cleanly rebounded or popped off the finger pads or forearms without resting. |
| **Volleyball** | **Touching the net with the body or arms** | Jumpers fail to control their forward momentum during spikes. | Net foul; touching any part of the net while playing the ball concedes an immediate point to the opponent. |
| **Basketball** | **Slapping the ball with the open palm when dribbling** | Lack of finger pad sensitivity. | Loss of control; push down rhythmically using the sensitive pads of the fingers with a flexible wrist. |
| **Basketball** | **Double Dribble (Catch and dribble again)** | Novices stop moving when trapped, then panic and bounce again. | Rule infraction; once a player picks up their dribble with two hands, they must pass or shoot. |
| **Basketball** | **Taking three or four steps without bouncing (Traveling)** | Running like soccer players while holding the ball. | Violation; a player may only take two rhythmic gathering steps when completing a layup or stop. |

## Worked Examples

### Scenario 1: Deciding a Volleyball Net Touch
- **Context:** During an intense rally in the inter-school tournament, Chinedu executes an explosive spike. The ball slams into the opponent's court for a clean winner. However, on Chinedu's descent, his jersey brushes against the top tape of the net.
- **Problem:** Is the point awarded to Chinedu's team?
- **Step 1:** Check the official FIVB rule on net contact: Touching the net between the antennae while playing the ball is an explicit net fault.
- **Step 2:** Chinedu was directly involved in the action of playing the ball when his clothing contacted the net.
- **Step 3:** A net fault cancels the attacking winner.
- **Verdict:** The referee blows the whistle, signals a net violation on Chinedu, and awards the point and service to the opposing team!

### Scenario 2: Calculating Basketball Points in a Clutch Play
- **Context:** With five seconds left on the game clock, team captain Amina launches a shot from two feet behind the curved three-point arc. She is fouled in mid-air by a defender. The ball swishes through the net as the buzzer sounds! The referee signals the foul and awards Amina one bonus free throw, which she sinks cleanly.
- **Problem:** How many total points did Amina score on this single play?
- **Step 1:** The original shot was taken from behind the 3-point line and was successful = **3 points**.
- **Step 2:** The foul occurred during the successful shooting act, granting one free throw.
- **Step 3:** The free throw was successfully made = **1 point**.
- **Step 4:** Calculate sum: $3 + 1 = 4\text{ points}$.
- **Verdict:** Amina completed a spectacular **4-point play**!

## Guardian Sync
- **Wall Set & Volley Drill:** Stand facing an exterior wall about 1 meter away. Practice continuous overhead finger-pad tapping (setting) against the wall to build finger strength and coordination.
- **Basketball Low-Dribble Drill:** Practice bouncing a tennis or basketball at knee height 50 times with the right hand and 50 times with the left hand without looking down at the ball.
- **Discussion:** Discuss with the student how team rotation and communication in court games develop social responsibility, trust, and cooperative leadership.
