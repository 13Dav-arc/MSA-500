## Key Terms
- Term: Physical Education
- Definition: An integral phase of general education that develops physical competence, health-related fitness, emotional stability, and social values through structured physical activities.
- Term: Health-Related Fitness
- Definition: Organic bodily attributes that promote longevity and disease prevention, including cardiorespiratory endurance, muscular strength, muscular endurance, flexibility, and body composition.
- Term: Skill-Related Fitness
- Definition: Neuromuscular performance components that optimize athletic execution, including agility, balance, coordination, power, reaction time, and speed.
- Term: Crouch Start
- Definition: The mandatory four-point sprint starting stance utilizing starting blocks executed under three commands: "On your marks", "Set", and the starter's signal.
- Term: Non-Visual Baton Exchange
- Definition: The blind high-speed transfer technique used in 4x100m relays where the outgoing runner does not look backward at the baton.
- Term: Fosbury Flop
- Definition: The universal high jump technique where the athlete executes a curved approach and arches backwards over the crossbar onto a foam crash mat.
- Term: Stop Board (Toe Board)
- Definition: The curved wooden obstacle at the front of the 2.135m shot put circle designed to help throwers absorb forward momentum without fouling.
- Term: Association Football
- Definition: An 11-a-side team sport played on a rectangular pitch where players maneuver a ball into the opponent's goal without using their hands or arms.
- Term: Forearm Pass (Dig)
- Definition: A foundational defensive volleyball skill executed by joining hands and rebounding the ball off the locked flat forearms.
- Term: Traveling
- Definition: An illegal basketball violation committed when a player moves their pivot foot or runs without continuously bouncing the ball.
- Term: Active Recreation
- Definition: Leisure pursuits demanding vigorous bodily exertion and cardiovascular energy expenditure that refresh both body and mind.
- Term: Atilogwu Dance
- Definition: An athletic, highly acrobatic traditional Igbo dance from southeastern Nigeria renowned for high jumps, aerial somersaults, and human pyramids.

### Key Examination Review Pillars
1. **Physical Education Foundations (Weeks 01–02):**
   - General education integration: educating the body and educating through the body.
   - Traditional physical culture in Nigeria: Wrestling (*Kokawa*), canoeing, hunting, moonlight games, traditional acrobatics (*Ege*).
   - Health-Related Fitness (5 components: cardiorespiratory endurance, muscular strength, muscular endurance, flexibility, body composition).
   - Skill-Related Fitness (6 components: agility, balance, coordination, power, reaction time, speed).
2. **Track Athletics & Sprints (Week 03):**
   - 400m oval track structure: straights and curves.
   - Crouch start progression: "On your marks" (feet in blocks, rear knee down, bridge fingers behind line) $\rightarrow$ "Set" (hips rise higher than shoulders, back knee lifts, motionless freeze) $\rightarrow$ "Gun" (explosive simultaneous block push).
   - Relay mechanics: 4x100m blind non-visual pass; 4x400m visual pass; baton position inside the 20m/30m changeover zone dictates legality.
3. **Field Athletics: Jumps & Throws (Weeks 04 & 06):**
   - Universal 4 phases: Approach $\rightarrow$ Take-off $\rightarrow$ Flight $\rightarrow$ Landing.
   - Take-off rule: strictly **single foot** for High Jump and Long Jump.
   - Long jump flight styles: Sail, Hang, Hitch-kick. Measured perpendicularly to nearest sand depression.
   - High jump clearance: Fosbury Flop requires curved J-run and certified foam crash mats (never sand!).
   - Throwing events: Shot Put circle ($2.135\text{ m}$), stop board ($10\text{ cm}$ high), put from neck, glide technique. Discus circle ($2.50\text{ m}$), release off index finger. Landing sector $34.92^\circ$. Exit through rear half only after implement lands.
4. **Ball Games (Weeks 07–08):**
   - Football (Soccer): 11 players, 90 minutes ($2 \times 45\text{m}$), pitch $105\text{m} \times 68\text{m}$, goal $7.32\text{m} \times 2.44\text{m}$, penalty spot at 11m, direct vs indirect free kicks, offside rules, referee, linesmen, and 4th official.
   - Volleyball: 6 players, court $18\text{m} \times 9\text{m}$, net $2.43\text{m}$ (men) / $2.24\text{m}$ (women), clockwise rotation, maximum 3 touches, 25 points per set (2-point lead).
   - Basketball: 5 players, court $28\text{m} \times 15\text{m}$, hoop $3.05\text{m}$ ($10\text{ ft}$), 2 points (inside arc), 3 points (outside arc), 1 point (free throw), traveling and double dribble violations.
5. **Recreation & Traditional Dance (Week 09):**
   - Leisure is discretionary uncommitted free time; recreation is constructive activity engaged in during leisure for renewal.
   - Active recreation (high exertion: swimming, football) vs. Passive recreation (low exertion: reading, *Ayo Olopon*).
   - Nigerian folk dances: *Bata* (Yoruba, Bata drums, rapid shoulder/waist movements), *Atilogwu* (Igbo, acrobatics, *Ogene*, flutes), *Koroso* (Hausa/Fulani, ankle rattles, rhythmic stamping), *Swange* (Tiv, serpentine undulating torso movements).

## Common Mistakes

| Category | Common Student Trap | Correct Examination Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fitness Testing** | Thinking the Sit-and-Reach test evaluates abdominal muscle strength. | The Sit-and-Reach test strictly assesses lower back and hamstring **flexibility**. |
| **Relay Handoff** | Believing a relay pass is legal as long as the runner's feet are in the zone. | Legality is governed 100% by the position of the **baton itself** inside the changeover zone lines. |
| **Long Jump Scoring**| Assuming distance is measured to where the athlete's feet landed even if they fall back. | Measurement is taken to the **nearest mark in the sand** made by any body part towards the board. |
| **Throwing Exit** | Believing a thrower can step out the front of the circle once the shot hits the ground. | Stepping out of the front half of the circle at any point is an automatic **foul throw**. Must exit rear half! |
| **Offside in Football**| Calling offside when a player receives a ball directly from a throw-in or corner kick. | Official IFAB Law 11 explicitly exempts throw-ins, corner kicks, and goal kicks from the offside rule. |
| **Volleyball Rotation**| Rotating counter-clockwise when regaining the serve. | Volleyball players must strictly rotate **one position clockwise**. |

## Worked Examples

### Scenario 1: Comprehensive Multi-Sport Rules Dispute
- **Context:** An annual inter-house sports festival witnesses three controversial calls:
  1. In the long jump, Bola takes off using a powerful two-footed jump from the wooden board.
  2. In the shot put, Segun's shot lands inside the sector, and he exits through the back half of the circle before the metal ball touches the grass.
  3. In football, Kunle scores directly into the opponent's net from an indirect free kick without any other player touching the ball.
- **Problem:** Analyze and deliver official rulings for all three incidents.
- **Analysis 1:** Athletics rule mandates single-foot take-off for long jump. A two-footed take-off is an immediate **foul**.
- **Analysis 2:** The athlete must remain inside the throwing circle until the implement has struck the ground. Exiting before impact is a **foul throw**.
- **Analysis 3:** An indirect free kick cannot score directly. A goal kick is awarded to the opposing team.
- **Verdict:** All three plays represent illegal actions / invalid scores according to official rulebooks!

### Scenario 2: Selecting Proper Fitness Diagnostics for an Academic Battery
- **Context:** The PHE department is preparing the end-of-term physical fitness report cards. They must evaluate:
  1. Muscular Endurance
  2. Agility
  3. Cardiorespiratory Endurance
- **Problem:** Which combination of standardized fitness tests must be administered?
- **Step 1:** Muscular endurance is measured by timed repetitive contractions (e.g., 1-minute bent-knee sit-ups or push-ups).
- **Step 2:** Agility is measured by rapid directional changes (e.g., 10m x 4 Shuttle Run test).
- **Step 3:** Cardiorespiratory endurance is measured by distance covered over prolonged aerobic time (e.g., 12-Minute Cooper Run/Walk test).
- **Verdict:** Sit-up test (Endurance), Shuttle Run (Agility), and 12-Minute Cooper Run (Cardiorespiratory).
