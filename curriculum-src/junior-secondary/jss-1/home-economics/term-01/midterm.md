# First Term Midterm Continuous Assessment Review

This diagnostic continuous assessment evaluates student mastery across the foundational Home Economics units covered during Weeks 01 through 04 of the First Term:
- **Week 01:** Meaning, Scope, and Importance of Home Economics (Core branches, resources, and career pathways).
- **Week 02:** The Human Body: Grooming, Personal Hygiene, and Posture (Care of skin, teeth, hair, hands, feet, and posture).
- **Week 03:** Puberty and Adolescence (Hormonal triggers, secondary sexual traits, menstruation, menarche, and menstrual hygiene).
- **Week 04:** Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs) and HIV/AIDS (Causative agents, symptoms, modes of transmission, myths, abstinence, and PMTCT).

---

## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which branch of Home Economics focuses on nutrient intake, balanced meal planning, culinary skills, and food preservation?
A) Clothing and Textiles
B) Food and Nutrition (correct)
C) Home Management
D) Consumer Education
Feedback: Food and Nutrition deals with nutrient chemistry, balanced diets, cooking, and food preservation.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: In Home Management, which of the following is categorized as a human resource?
A) A 3-bedroom bungalow
B) Personal skills, intelligence, and time (correct)
C) ₦50,000 in a bank account
D) A gas cooking cylinder
Feedback: Personal abilities, knowledge, time, and physical energy are human resources; tangible physical assets are material resources.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the tough, outermost protective layer of the human skin called?
A) Dermis
B) Epidermis (correct)
C) Pulp cavity
D) Subcutaneous fat
Feedback: The epidermis is the outer protective layer of epithelial cells shielding the body.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: What sticky biofilm of bacteria, food residues, and saliva forms on teeth and causes tooth decay if not brushed away?
A) Sebum
B) Dental plaque (correct)
C) Tartar
D) Enamel
Feedback: Dental plaque is the bacterial film that ferments sugars and produces tooth-decaying acids.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: What fungal infection causes severe itching, redness, and peeling skin between the toes when feet are left damp inside shoes?
A) Ringworm of the scalp
B) Athlete's Foot (*Tinea pedis*) (correct)
C) Scurvy
D) Rickets
Feedback: Athlete's Foot (*Tinea pedis*) is a fungal infection that thrives in warm, damp spaces between toes.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the primary male sex hormone produced by the testes that drives secondary sexual traits like a deep voice and facial hair?
A) Estrogen
B) Testosterone (correct)
C) Progesterone
D) Insulin
Feedback: Testosterone is the male sex hormone responsible for male pubertal changes and sperm production.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the term for the very first menstrual period experienced by an adolescent girl?
A) Menopause
B) Menarche (correct)
C) Ovulation
D) Gestation
Feedback: Menarche is the scientific term for the first occurrence of menstruation in a female's life.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the correct direction for washing or wiping the external female genitalia to prevent bacterial urinary tract infections?
A) From the anus forward toward the vagina
B) From the front (vagina) backward toward the anus (correct)
C) In rapid circular motions across both openings simultaneously
D) Backwards and forwards ten times
Feedback: Cleaning from front to back prevents the transfer of fecal bacteria from the anus into the vagina or urethra.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: What specific immune cells in the human body does HIV target, infect, and progressively destroy?
A) Red blood cells
B) CD4 helper T-lymphocytes (correct)
C) Blood platelets
D) Enamel cells
Feedback: HIV selectively infects and destroys CD4 helper T-lymphocytes, paralyzing immune defenses.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: Which bacterial STI is caused by *Neisseria gonorrhoeae* and produces a thick, yellowish-green pus discharge and burning urination?
A) Syphilis
B) Gonorrhea (correct)
C) Genital herpes
D) Trichomoniasis
Feedback: Gonorrhea causes thick purulent discharge and painful urination.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the single, painless round ulcer that appears at the infection site during the primary stage of Syphilis called?
A) Gall
B) Chancre (correct)
C) Acne
D) Tartar
Feedback: The primary lesion of Syphilis is a painless, firm ulcer called a chancre.

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following is the only 100% effective, zero-risk method for adolescents and unmarried youth to avoid sexually acquired STIs and HIV?
A) Drinking warm tea after exercise
B) Complete sexual abstinence (correct)
C) Washing with herbal soap
D) Eating large amounts of raw onions
Feedback: Sexual abstinence is the only 100% guaranteed way to avoid sexually transmitted infections.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: Why should used disposable sanitary pads NEVER be flushed down a flush toilet?
A) They turn the water pure black
B) They do not dissolve in water and will swell, causing severe blockage of plumbing pipes and sewers (correct)
C) They cause the toilet to explode into flames
D) They release oxygen into the air
Feedback: Sanitary pads do not break down in water; flushing them blocks pipes and septic systems.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following is a medically verified route of HIV transmission?
A) Mosquito or bedbug bites
B) Transfusion of contaminated, unscreened blood or sharing unsterilized needles (correct)
C) Shaking hands or hugging a friend
D) Sharing toilet seats in public schools
Feedback: HIV is transmitted through contaminated blood, unsterilized needles, sexual contact, and mother-to-child transmission.

### Q15 [true-false]
Stem: Home Economics is strictly a domestic subject designed solely for females and has no relevance to boys or modern professional careers.
A) True
B) False (correct)
Feedback: False. Home Economics is a scientific, co-educational discipline providing essential life skills and diverse careers for both sexes.

### Q16 [true-false]
Stem: An individual can contract HIV by sharing a drinking cup, hugging, or sitting next to an infected person in a classroom.
A) True
B) False (correct)
Feedback: False. HIV is not transmitted through saliva, sweat, tears, hugging, or casual contact.

### Q17 [multi-select]
Stem: Which of the following are core branches of Home Economics? (Select all that apply)
A) Food and Nutrition (correct)
B) Clothing and Textiles (correct)
C) Home Management (Family Resource Management) (correct)
D) Petroleum Geology
Feedback: Food & Nutrition, Clothing & Textiles, and Home Management are core branches. Petroleum geology is an earth science.

### Q18 [multi-select]
Stem: Which of the following are essential daily personal hygiene practices? (Select all that apply)
A) Bathing at least once or twice daily with clean water and soap (correct)
B) Brushing teeth twice daily (morning and before bedtime) with fluoride paste (correct)
C) Washing hands with soap under running water before eating (correct)
D) Sharing wet bath towels with all neighbors in the compound
Feedback: Daily bathing, twice-daily tooth brushing, and handwashing are essential hygiene habits. Sharing towels spreads infections.

### Q19 [matching]
Stem: Match each branch of Home Economics with its primary area of focus:
- Food and Nutrition: Study of nutrient values, culinary arts, and balanced diets
- Clothing and Textiles: Study of natural/synthetic fibers, garment sewing, and fashion
- Home Management: Planning, budgeting, and controlling family resources
- Child Development: Physical, cognitive, and social milestones of children
Feedback: Food & Nutrition is diet/cooking; Clothing & Textiles is fibers/sewing; Home Management is resources/budgeting; Child Development is pediatric growth.

### Q20 [matching]
Stem: Match each sexually transmitted infection with its characteristic clinical feature:
- Gonorrhea: Thick yellowish-green pus discharge and painful burning urination
- Syphilis: Primary painless ulcer (chancre) that later progresses to a body rash
- Genital Herpes: Recurrent clusters of painful, fluid-filled blister sores
- HIV / AIDS: Progressive destruction of CD4 immune cells leading to opportunistic infections
Feedback: Gonorrhea causes discharge; Syphilis causes chancres; Herpes causes painful blisters; HIV destroys CD4 cells.
