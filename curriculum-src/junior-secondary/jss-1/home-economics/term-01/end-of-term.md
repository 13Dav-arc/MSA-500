# First Term Terminal Examination: Home Economics

This comprehensive terminal examination rigorously evaluates student knowledge, practical understanding, and analytical problem-solving across the complete First Term curriculum:
- **Week 01:** Meaning, Scope, and Importance of Home Economics (Core branches, human vs. material resources, and professional vocational careers).
- **Week 02:** The Human Body: Grooming, Personal Hygiene, and Posture (Anatomy and care of skin, teeth, hair, hands, feet, and posture).
- **Week 03:** Puberty and Adolescence (Hormonal triggers, secondary sexual characteristics, menstrual cycle, menarche, and menstrual hygiene).
- **Week 04:** Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs) and HIV/AIDS (Bacterial and viral STIs, CD4 cells, transmission routes vs. myths, abstinence, PMTCT, and destigmatization).
- **Midterm:** Midterm Diagnostic Synthesis of Weeks 01–04.
- **Week 06:** Rights and Responsibilities of the Child (Child's Rights Act 2003, survival, development, protection, and corresponding domestic and civic duties).
- **Week 07:** The Family: Meaning, Functions, and Relationship with the Home (House vs. home, family structures: nuclear, extended, single-parent, blended; socialization and family values).
- **Week 08:** Housing: Types of Houses and Care of the Home (Traditional vs. modern houses, functional rooms, and daily, weekly, and periodic cleaning routines).
- **Week 09:** Cleaning Equipment, Materials, and Home Cleaning Agents (Tools, care and storage protocols, commercial and homemade abrasives, detergents, and disinfectants).

## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following is the most comprehensive definition of Home Economics?
A) The study of wild animals living in nature reserves
B) An interdisciplinary field of study centered on family resources, home management, and improving the quality of human life (correct)
C) An engineering course focused on repairing heavy tractor engines
D) The study of ancient languages and civilizations
Feedback: Home Economics integrates practical sciences and arts to manage family resources and enhance life in the home and community.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Which branch of Home Economics focuses on nutrient intake, balanced meal planning, culinary arts, and food preservation?
A) Clothing and Textiles
B) Food and Nutrition (correct)
C) Home Management
D) Consumer Education
Feedback: Food and Nutrition deals with nutrient chemistry, balanced diets, cooking, and food safety.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: In Home Management, which of the following is categorized as a human resource?
A) A four-bedroom residential flat
B) Personal skills, intelligence, time, and physical energy (correct)
C) A brand new gas cooker
D) ₦100,000 cash in a savings bank
Feedback: Personal attributes like skills, intelligence, time, and physical energy are human resources; tangible physical assets are material resources.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the tough, outermost protective layer of the human skin called?
A) Dermis
B) Epidermis (correct)

D) Pulp cavity
Feedback: The epidermis is the outer protective layer of epithelial cells shielding the body.

### Q9 [single-choice]

A) True (correct)
B) False

### Q39 [true-false]
Stem: A physical house made of expensive Italian marble automatically becomes a happy home, even if family members hate and fight each other daily.
A) True
B) False (correct)
Feedback: False. A physical building is merely a house; a home requires mutual love, warmth, and peace among family members.

### Q40 [true-false]
Stem: Storing a broom resting upright on its bristles protects the fibers and makes the broom last three times longer.
A) True
B) False (correct)
Feedback: False. Resting a broom on its bristles crushes and deforms the fibers; it should be hung by its handle.
