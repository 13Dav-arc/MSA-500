# Second Term Terminal Examination: Home Economics

This comprehensive terminal examination rigorously evaluates student mastery, technical analysis, and practical problem-solving across the complete Second Term curriculum:
- **Week 01:** Family Needs, Goals, and Standards I (Needs vs. wants, hierarchy of primary and secondary needs, scale of preference, and opportunity cost).
- **Week 02:** Family Needs, Goals, and Standards II (Goal time horizons: short, intermediate, long-term; rigid vs. flexible standards; the Home Management Cycle: planning, organizing, implementing, evaluating).
- **Week 03:** Sewing Tools and Equipment (Classification into measuring, marking, cutting, stitching, and pressing tools; maintenance and safety protocols).
- **Week 04:** Practical Production of Household Articles & Basic Stitches (Temporary, permanent joining, neatening, and decorative embroidery stitches; construction of pillowcases and kitchen aprons).
- **Midterm:** Midterm Diagnostic Synthesis of Weeks 01–04.
- **Week 06:** Production of Income-Yielding Crafts (Tie-and-dye/*Adire* textile design; vat dyes, caustic soda, sodium hydrosulphite, fabric preparation, folding/tying techniques, and chemical safety).
- **Week 07:** Production of Home Cleaning Agents (Abrasives and surfactants; homemade scouring powders from eggshells, wood ash potash, river sand, sandpaper leaves, and citrus acids).
- **Week 08:** Production of Cosmetics and Deodorants (Skin pomades, lotions, biological difference between deodorants and anti-perspirants, indigenous botanical emollients: shea butter, coconut oil, double-boiler method, patch testing).
- **Week 09:** Regulatory Control of Cosmetic Products in Nigeria (Statutory mandates of NAFDAC, SON, FCCPC; hazards of hydroquinone, mercury, and corticosteroids; mandatory product labeling and consumer rights).

## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following best defines a primary family need?
A) An expensive electronic entertainment gadget
B) An indispensable biological necessity required for human survival and physical health (correct)
C) A luxury vacation trip to a foreign country
D) A designer sports shoe worn for fashion
Feedback: Primary needs (food, clothing, shelter, basic healthcare) are vital biological necessities required to sustain life.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: When a family organizes their desires in a ranked order of biological urgency and economic importance, they are constructing a:
A) Scale of preference (correct)
B) Family coat of arms
C) Cash book ledger
D) School timetable
Feedback: A scale of preference is an ordered list of unsatisfied wants ranked from most essential to least pressing.

### Q3 [single-choice]

C) The ornamental wall clock that was forgone (correct)
D) The supermarket shopping cart
Feedback: Opportunity cost represents the next best alternative forgone or sacrificed when making an economic choice.
