## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following consonant sounds is an alveolar lateral liquid articulated with air flowing past the sides of the tongue?
A) /l/ (correct)
B) /r/
C) /m/
D) /n/
Feedback: Consonant /l/ is an alveolar lateral liquid produced when the tongue tip contacts the teeth ridge while air escapes around its sides.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: How is the consonant sound /r/ articulated in standard English speech?
A) The tip of the tongue approaches the alveolar ridge closely without touching it (correct)
B) The tongue tip makes firm, unbroken contact with the teeth ridge
C) Both lips are pressed together to block all escaping air
D) Air is directed exclusively through the nasal cavity[cite: 1]
Feedback: Consonant /r/ is a post-alveolar continuant where the tongue curls near the alveolar ridge without making physical contact.
