## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following consonant sounds is a voiceless labio-dental fricative?
A) /f/ (correct)
B) /v/
C) /s/
D) /z/
Feedback: Consonant /f/ is articulated by the upper teeth and lower lip without vocal cord vibration, making it a voiceless labio-dental fricative.
