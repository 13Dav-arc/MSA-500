## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following words contains the short close front unrounded vowel sound /ɪ/?
A) Ship (correct)
B) Sheep
C) Shoot
D) Shape
Feedback: *Ship* (/ʃɪp/) contains the short pure vowel /ɪ/, while *sheep* (/ʃiːp/) contains long /iː/.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Which word pair represents a minimal pair contrasting /ɪ/ and /iː/?
A) Fit and Feet (correct)
B) Pot and Port
C) Cup and Cap
D) Bad and Bed
Feedback: *Fit* (/fɪt/) and *Feet* (/fiːt/) differ solely by the contrast between short /ɪ/ and long /iː/.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following words contains the short open front vowel sound /æ/?
A) Pan (correct)
B) Pen
C) Pin
D) Pun
Feedback: *Pan* (/pæn/) contains the open front vowel /æ/.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: Which word pair represents a minimal pair contrasting /e/ and /æ/?
A) Men and Man (correct)
B) Seat and Sit
C) Full and Fool
D) Cot and Court
Feedback: *Men* (/men/) and *Man* (/mæn/) contrast the mid vowel /e/ with the open vowel /æ/.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following words contains the short open back rounded vowel sound /ɒ/?
A) Cot (correct)
B) Court
C) Cart
D) Curt
Feedback: *Cot* (/kɒt/) contains the short back rounded vowel /ɒ/.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following words contains the long back rounded vowel sound /ɔː/?
A) Port (correct)
B) Pot
C) Pat
D) Pit
Feedback: *Port* (/pɔːt/) contains the long back rounded vowel /ɔː/.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following words contains the short close back rounded vowel sound /ʊ/?
A) Full (correct)
B) Fool
C) Fall
D) Feel
Feedback: *Full* (/fʊl/) contains the short vowel /ʊ/, while *fool* (/fuːl/) contains long /uː/.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following words contains the long mid-central vowel sound /ɜː/?
A) Girl (correct)
B) Gull
C) Gill
D) Goal
Feedback: *Girl* (/gɜːl/) contains the long mid-central vowel /ɜː/.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: What special phonetic name is given to the neutral vowel /ə/ occurring exclusively in unstressed syllables?
A) The Schwa (correct)
B) The Monophthong
C) The Diphthong
D) The Plosive
Feedback: /ə/ is internationally termed the *Schwa*.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following consonant sounds is a VOICELESS bilabial plosive?
A) /p/ (correct)
B) /b/
C) /d/
D) /g/
Feedback: /p/ is articulated by closing both lips without vocal cord vibration.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: In which of the following words is the letter "b" SILENT?
A) Doubt (correct)
B) Basket
C) Table
D) Timber
Feedback: In *doubt* (/daʊt/), the letter "b" is completely silent.

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: Which word pair represents a minimal pair contrasting voiceless /t/ and voiced /d/?
A) Ten and Den (correct)
B) Pin and Bin
C) Cap and Gap
D) Cot and Caught
Feedback: *Ten* (/ten/) and *Den* (/den/) contrast voiceless /t/ with voiced /d/.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: In which of the following words is the initial letter "k" SILENT?
A) Know (correct)
B) Keep
C) Kettle
D) King
Feedback: Initial "k" before "n" is silent in *know* (/nəʊ/).

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following words begins with the VOICED velar plosive sound /g/?
A) Gate (correct)
B) Kate
C) Date
D) Plate
Feedback: *Gate* begins with the voiced velar plosive /g/.

### Q15 [single-choice]
Stem: What type of noun is "Kaduna" in the sentence: "Chidi lives in Kaduna"?
A) Proper noun (correct)
B) Common noun
C) Abstract noun
D) Collective noun
Feedback: *Kaduna* is the specific capitalized name of a city, making it a proper noun.

### Q16 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following nouns is UNCOUNTABLE?
A) Furniture (correct)
B) Chair
C) Table
D) Stool
Feedback: *Furniture* is an uncountable mass noun that cannot take the plural "-s".

### Q17 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the correct irregular plural form of "ox"?
A) Oxen (correct)
B) Oxes
C) Oxens
D) Oxies
Feedback: *Ox* takes the archaic plural suffix `-en` to form *oxen*.

### Q18 [single-choice]
Stem: Identify the personal pronoun in the OBJECT case:
A) Us (correct)
B) We
C) They
D) She
Feedback: *Us* is the objective case of the first-person plural pronoun.

### Q19 [single-choice]
Stem: Which pronoun correctly completes the sentence: "The girl ______ won the debate received a trophy"?
A) who (correct)
B) which
C) whose
D) whom
Feedback: *Who* is the relative subject pronoun referring to a human antecedent (*The girl*).

### Q20 [single-choice]
Stem: In the sentence *"The principal praised the disciplined students"*, what is the direct object of the transitive verb "praised"?
A) the disciplined students (correct)
B) The principal
C) praised
D) yesterday
Feedback: *The disciplined students* receives the direct action of the transitive verb *praised*.

### Q21 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the comparative degree of the irregular adjective "bad"?
A) Worse (correct)
B) Worst
C) Badder
D) More bad
Feedback: *Bad* compares irregularly: *bad*, *worse*, *worst*.

### Q22 [single-choice]
Stem: Choose the grammatically correct sentence comparing two siblings:
A) Tolu is older than Ngozi (correct)
B) Tolu is more older than Ngozi
C) Tolu is oldest than Ngozi
D) Tolu is most old than Ngozi
Feedback: When comparing two individuals, use the simple comparative *older* without *more*.

### Q23 [single-choice]
Stem: Which preposition correctly completes the sentence: "The football match will kick off ______ 4:00 PM"?
A) at (correct)
B) on
C) in
D) to
Feedback: Clock times are always introduced by the preposition *at*.

### Q24 [single-choice]
Stem: Complete the sentence with the correct preposition: "Divide the oranges ______ the two boys."
A) between (correct)
B) among
C) besides
D) across
Feedback: *Between* is used when sharing between exactly two persons.

### Q25 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following is a COORDINATING conjunction from the FANBOYS acronym?
A) Yet (correct)
B) Because
C) Although
D) Unless
Feedback: *Yet* belongs to the seven FANBOYS coordinating conjunctions.

### Q26 [single-choice]
Stem: Complete the sentence with the appropriate subordinating conjunction: "She could not play football ______ she had a fractured ankle."
A) because (correct)
B) but
C) and
D) so
Feedback: *Because* introduces the cause or reason for an action.

### Q27 [single-choice]
Stem: According to the rules of concord, which verb correctly completes the sentence: "The news ______ broadcast at nine o'clock"?
A) was (correct)
B) were
C) are
D) have been
Feedback: *News* is singular in meaning and takes the singular verb *was*.

### Q28 [single-choice]
Stem: Apply the proximity rule: "Neither the captain nor the players ______ on the pitch."
A) were (correct)
B) was
C) is
D) has been
Feedback: With *neither...nor*, the verb agrees with the closer plural subject *players*, requiring *were*.

### Q29 [single-choice]
Stem: In reading comprehension, what does the word "potable" mean when describing water?
A) Safe and fit for human drinking (correct)
B) Stored in clay pots
C) Boiling hot
D) Heavily silted
Feedback: *Potable* means purified water safe for human consumption.

### Q30 [single-choice]
Stem: What does the term "subsistence farming" mean?
A) Cultivating food crops primarily to feed one's household with little surplus for trade (correct)
B) Commercial export agriculture
C) Mechanized cattle ranching
D) Greenhouse floral cultivation
Feedback: Subsistence farming focuses on family survival rather than commercial profit.

### Q31 [single-choice]
Stem: In the phrase *"unscrupulous syndicates"*, what part of speech is "unscrupulous"?
A) Adjective (correct)
B) Preposition
C) Conjunction
D) Adverb
Feedback: *Unscrupulous* describes the noun *syndicates*, functioning as a descriptive adjective.

### Q32 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the central sentence that expresses the main idea of a paragraph called?
A) Topic sentence (correct)
B) Concluding sentence
C) Transitional phrase
D) Dependent clause
Feedback: The topic sentence declares the central controlling idea of a paragraph.

### Q33 [single-choice]

### Q34 [single-choice]

A) Soliloquy (correct)

### Q41 [single-choice]
Stem: "The athlete ran like a cheetah." What figure of speech is used in this sentence?
A) Simile (correct)
B) Metaphor
C) Personification
D) Hyperbole
Feedback: The comparison uses "like" to link the athlete's speed to a cheetah, making it a simile.

### Q42 [single-choice]
Stem: "The classroom was a boiling furnace during the hot afternoon." What figure of speech is present?
A) Metaphor (correct)
B) Simile
C) Personification
D) Onomatopoeia
Feedback: The direct identification of the classroom *as* a furnace without "like" or "as" is a metaphor.

### Q43 [true-false]
Stem: In phonetic transcription, the colon-like length mark (ː) indicates a prolonged, long vowel duration.
Answer: true
Feedback: The mark (ː) denotes long vowel articulation, as in /iː/, /ɔː/, /uː/, and /ɜː/.

### Q44 [true-false]
Stem: Uncountable mass nouns like "information" and "furniture" can be made plural by adding "-s" when there are many items.
Answer: false
Feedback: Uncountable nouns never take "-s"; use "pieces of information" or "items of furniture".

### Q45 [true-false]
Stem: The schwa sound /ə/ occurs exclusively in unstressed syllables in English words.
Answer: true
Feedback: The schwa /ə/ is a neutral vowel restricted strictly to unstressed syllables.

### Q46 [true-false]
Stem: An intransitive verb requires a direct object to complete its grammatical meaning.
Answer: false
Feedback: Intransitive verbs make complete sense on their own without requiring a direct object.

### Q47 [true-false]
Stem: A quatrain is a poetic stanza containing exactly four lines of verse.
Answer: true
Feedback: A four-line poetic stanza is called a quatrain.

### Q48 [true-false]
Stem: An informal letter to a friend should conclude with "Yours faithfully" followed by your full name and signature.
Answer: false
Feedback: "Yours faithfully" is reserved for formal letters. Informal letters conclude with "Yours sincerely" or "Yours affectionately" followed by first name only.

### Q60 [matching]
