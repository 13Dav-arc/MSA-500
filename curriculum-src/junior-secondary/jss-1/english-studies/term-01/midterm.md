## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following words contains the short close front unrounded vowel sound /ɪ/?
A) Sit (correct)
B) Seat
C) Seed
D) Seen
Feedback: *Sit* contains the short vowel sound /ɪ/, whereas *seat*, *seed*, and *seen* contain long /iː/.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Which word pair is a minimal pair contrasting /e/ and /æ/?
A) Bet and Bat (correct)
B) Pot and Port
C) Full and Fool
D) Ship and Sheep
Feedback: *Bet* (/bet/) and *bat* (/bæt/) differ solely by the contrast between /e/ and /æ/.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: In which of the following words is the long back rounded vowel sound /ɔː/ heard?
A) Law (correct)
B) Lot
C) Lock
D) Log
Feedback: *Law* (/lɔː/) contains the long vowel sound /ɔː/, whereas *lot*, *lock*, and *log* contain short /ɒ/.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following words contains the short close back rounded vowel sound /ʊ/?
A) Book (correct)
B) Boot
C) Moon
D) Pool
Feedback: The digraph `oo` in *book* realizes the short vowel /ʊ/ (/bʊk/).

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: What type of noun is "integrity" in the sentence: "Integrity is the foundation of good leadership"?
A) Abstract noun (correct)
B) Proper noun
C) Collective noun
D) Concrete unit noun
Feedback: *Integrity* denotes an intangible moral virtue or concept, making it an abstract noun.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following nouns is UNCOUNTABLE and cannot take the plural suffix "-s"?
A) Luggage (correct)
B) Basket
C) Uniform
D) Classroom
Feedback: *Luggage* is an uncountable mass noun; pluralization with "-s" is ungrammatical in standard English.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the correct irregular plural form of "mouse"?
A) Mice (correct)
B) Mouses
C) Mices
D) Mousen
Feedback: *Mouse* undergoes internal vowel change to form *mice*.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: Identify the personal pronoun in the OBJECT case:
A) Them (correct)
B) They
C) She
D) We
Feedback: *Them* is the objective case of the third-person plural pronoun; *they*, *she*, and *we* are nominative (subject) case.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: In the sentence *"The principal delivered an inspiring speech"*, what is the direct object of the transitive verb "delivered"?
A) an inspiring speech (correct)
B) The principal
C) delivered
D) yesterday
Feedback: *An inspiring speech* receives the action of the transitive verb *delivered*.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: In reading comprehension, what is the meaning of the word "potable" when applied to water supplies?
A) Safe and fit for human drinking (correct)
B) Stored in decorative pottery
C) Extremely hot and boiling
D) Heavily contaminated with silt
Feedback: *Potable* means clean and safe to drink without danger of waterborne illness.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: What reading comprehension skill involves identifying the main idea of a passage rather than focusing on minor details?
A) Skimming for central themes (correct)
B) Proofreading for typographical errors
C) Reciting phonetic vowel charts
D) Checking alphabetical spelling in a dictionary
Feedback: Skimming allows readers to quickly grasp the overarching main idea of an informational text.

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: In the sentence *"The youth committee demonstrated remarkable solidarity during the crisis"*, what does "solidarity" mean?
A) Mutual unity and collective support (correct)
B) Physical hardness like solid stone
C) Financial bankruptcy
D) Violent conflict between leaders
Feedback: *Solidarity* refers to unified support and mutual cooperation among people pursuing a common goal.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: In the phrase *"indispensable prerequisite"*, what part of speech is "indispensable"?
A) Adjective (correct)
B) Verb
C) Adverb
D) Pronoun
Feedback: *Indispensable* modifies the noun *prerequisite*, functioning as an adjective.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the term for the central organizing sentence that states the main idea of a paragraph?
A) Topic sentence (correct)
B) Concluding sentence
C) Transitional phrase
D) Dependent clause
Feedback: The topic sentence declares the central controlling theme of a paragraph.

### Q15 [single-choice]
Stem: Which grammatical tense is predominantly employed when writing a narrative essay recounting past personal events?
A) Past simple tense (correct)
B) Present continuous tense
C) Future simple tense
D) Present perfect subjunctive
Feedback: Past simple tense is the standard tense for recounting completed events in a narrative.

### Q16 [single-choice]
Stem: How many addresses are written on the page in a standard INFORMAL letter to a close friend?
A) One address (writer's address only) (correct)
B) Two addresses
C) Three addresses
D) Zero addresses
Feedback: Informal letters contain only one address—the writer's address in the top right corner.

### Q17 [single-choice]
Stem: In descriptive writing, which sensory technique creates the most vivid impression of a noisy marketplace?
A) Incorporating auditory sensory details like shouting traders and clattering metal pots (correct)
B) Providing numerical census population statistics of the state
C) Listing legal commercial trade regulations
D) Writing entirely in passive past perfect verbs
Feedback: Auditory sensory language brings the bustling sounds of a market directly to life.

### Q18 [single-choice]
Stem: What are the three primary genres of literature?
A) Prose, Drama, and Poetry (correct)
B) Biography, History, and Geography
C) Grammar, Speech, and Composition
D) Novel, Short story, and Flash fiction
Feedback: The three overarching genres of literature are Prose, Drama, and Poetry.

### Q19 [single-choice]
Stem: In a prose fiction story, what is the central leading character facing the main conflict called?
A) Protagonist (correct)
B) Antagonist
C) Narrator
D) Stage manager
Feedback: The protagonist is the principal character driving the main dramatic action.

### Q20 [single-choice]
Stem: Which animal character is most famous in West African folklore as the cunning, shrewd trickster?
A) Tortoise (correct)
B) Lion
C) Elephant
D) Hyena
Feedback: The Tortoise (*Ijapa* in Yoruba, *Mbe* in Igbo) is celebrated as the cunning oral trickster.

### Q21 [single-choice]
Stem: What type of traditional narrative explains the sacred origin of the world, natural forces, and deities?
A) Myth (correct)
B) Novel
C) Farce
D) Editorial
Feedback: Myths are sacred traditional accounts of cosmic origins and supernatural forces.

### Q22 [true-false]
Stem: In phonetic transcription, the colon-like length mark (ː) indicates that a vowel sound has a prolonged duration.
Answer: true
Feedback: The length mark (ː) denotes long vowel articulation, as in /iː/, /ɔː/, and /uː/.

### Q23 [true-false]
Stem: An intransitive verb requires a direct object to complete its grammatical meaning.
Answer: false
Feedback: An intransitive verb does not require a direct object; transitive verbs require direct objects.

### Q24 [true-false]
Stem: An informal letter to a classmate should conclude with "Yours faithfully" followed by your full surname and signature.
Answer: false
Feedback: "Yours faithfully" is reserved for formal letters where the recipient's name is unknown. Informal letters use "Yours sincerely" or "Yours affectionately" followed by first name only.

### Q25 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO words that contain the long vowel sound /iː/:
Correct: Sheep, Meat
Incorrect: Ship, Mit
Feedback: *Sheep* (/ʃiːp/) and *meat* (/miːt/) contain /iː/; *ship* and *mit* contain /ɪ/.

### Q26 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO uncountable nouns that cannot be pluralized with "-s":
Correct: Furniture, Information
Incorrect: Chair, Message
Feedback: *Furniture* and *information* are uncountable; *chair* and *message* are countable.

### Q27 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO elements of prose fiction:
Correct: Plot, Setting
Incorrect: Stanza, Stage directions
Feedback: *Plot* and *setting* are elements of prose fiction; *stanza* belongs to poetry and *stage directions* belong to drama.

### Q28 [matching]
Stem: Match each word with its corresponding pure vowel sound:
Pairs:
- Fit -> /ɪ/
- Feet -> /iː/
- Pot -> /ɒ/
Feedback: *Fit* contains /ɪ/, *feet* contains /iː/, and *pot* contains /ɒ/.

### Q29 [matching]
Stem: Match each pronoun with its grammatical case:
Pairs:
- We -> Subject case
- Us -> Object case
- Ours -> Possessive case
Feedback: *We* is subject case, *us* is object case, and *ours* is possessive case.

### Q30 [matching]
Stem: Match each literary genre with its primary form of expression:
Pairs:
- Prose -> Continuous sentences organized in paragraphs and chapters
- Drama -> Spoken dialogue and stage directions for theatrical performance
- Poetry -> Metrical lines arranged in rhythmic stanzas
Feedback: Prose uses paragraphs, drama uses dialogue for performance, and poetry uses stanzas.
