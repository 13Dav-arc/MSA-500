## Hook
Picture this scenario: you walk through the glass entrance doors of a multi-national corporate office in Lagos or Abuja. The reception foyer is spotless and cool, soft music plays in the background, and behind a polished wooden desk sits a smartly dressed professional who immediately looks up, offers a warm smile, and greets you: *"Good morning! Welcome to First Horizon Capital. My name is Aisha. How may I assist you today?"* Within two minutes, she checks your appointment, offers you a seat, and notifies the executive of your arrival. Contrast this with another office where the receptionist is chewing gum with her mouth open, scrolling through social media videos with the volume turned up, ignores you for ten minutes, and snaps: *"Who are you looking for? He is not on seat!"* Which of these two companies would you trust with your money or business contract? The receptionist is the **public face, the voice, and the frontline ambassador** of every organization! In this lesson, you will master the vital duties of an office receptionist, explore professional telephone etiquette, and learn how courtesy and diplomacy build powerful corporate goodwill!

## Key Terms
- Term: Receptionist
- Definition: A frontline administrative clerical officer stationed in the reception office or foyer who serves as the primary contact point for receiving visitors, answering telephone inquiries, and directing clients.
- Term: Reception Office (Front Desk)
- Definition: The dedicated entrance hall, waiting lounge, or welcoming foyer of an organization designed to receive, screen, and assist visitors before they enter internal departments.
- Term: Visitors' Book (Visitor's Register)
- Definition: An official administrative logbook kept at the reception desk to record vital information about external visitors (date, arrival time, visitor's full name, address, person to see, purpose of visit, departure time).
- Term: Appointment Register (Executive Diary)
- Definition: An official schedule or digital calendar maintained by the receptionist to record confirmed upcoming dates and times when clients are scheduled to meet with executives.
- Term: Telephone Message Pad
- Definition: A pre-printed standardized stationery slip used by a receptionist to record details of incoming phone calls when the intended recipient is unavailable.
- Term: Tact
- Definition: The skill, sensitivity, and diplomacy required to handle delicate, difficult, or uncomfortable human interactions with courtesy without offending others.
- Term: Diction
- Definition: The clarity, pronunciation, and distinct vocal articulation of spoken words during face-to-face and telephone communication.
- Term: Switchboard (PBX / PABX)
- Definition: A central telephone control console operated by a receptionist to route incoming external telephone calls to specific internal departmental extension lines.
- Term: Corporate Ambassador
- Definition: An employee whose public demeanor, professionalism, and courteous conduct directly represent and reflect the values and prestige of the organization.
- Term: Visitor's Badge (Pass)
- Definition: An identification card issued temporarily to an authorized visitor at the reception desk for security monitoring within the facility.

### 2. Detailed Duties and Responsibilities of a Receptionist

#### A. Receiving and Welcoming Visitors
- Welcome every visitor promptly the moment they approach the desk.
- Inquire politely about their mission: *"Please, who would you like to see?"*
- Request the visitor to complete the **Visitors' Book** or record the details on their behalf.
- Contact the relevant officer via telephone intercom to verify if they are ready to receive the guest.
- Direct the visitor courteously or arrange for an office assistant to escort them to the correct office.

#### B. Managing Official Registers

##### 1. The Visitors' Book (Visitor's Register)
An essential security and administrative record containing standardized columns:


##### 2. The Appointment Register
Tracks scheduled meetings. If a visitor arrives without an appointment, the receptionist politely explains the executive's schedule and books a future appointment in the register.

#### C. Operating the Telephone Switchboard
- Answers external incoming calls, screens callers, and transfers them to internal departmental extensions.
- When an executive is in a meeting or unavailable, takes comprehensive notes on a **Telephone Message Pad**.

#### D. Maintaining Reception Ambience
- Ensures the waiting area is clean, tidy, well-ventilated, and quiet.
- Stocks the magazine rack with current company newsletters, annual reports, and brochures so waiting guests are informed and entertained.

### 4. Professional Telephone Etiquette
Communicating over the telephone requires exceptional vocal discipline because the caller cannot see your facial expressions or body language:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   THE GOLDEN RULES OF TELEPHONE ETIQUETTE              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. PROMPT ANSWERING: Answer within two to three rings.                 │
│ 2. PROFESSIONAL GREETING: State Time + Company Name + Your Identity:   │
│    "Good morning, MindStormer Global Academy, Emeka speaking.          │
│     How may I help you?"                                               │
│ 3. ENUNCIATE CLEARLY: Speak at a moderate pace with pleasant diction;  │
│    never mumble or shout into the mouthpiece.                          │
│ 4. STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Never chew gum, eat food, or yawn on the phone!│
│ 5. ACTIVE LISTENING: Take notes; confirm spelling of names & numbers.  │
│ 6. COURTEOUS CLOSING: Thank the caller warmly; allow them to hang up   │
│    first to avoid accidentally cutting them off!                       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Taking a Telephone Message: The Message Slip
When an executive is unavailable, a complete message slip must contain:
1. Date and exact time of the call.
2. Name of the caller and their organization.
3. Telephone number of the caller.
4. Summary of the message.
5. Action required (e.g., *"Please call back," "Will call again"*).
6. Name and initials of the receptionist who took the message.

## Common Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Answering an office telephone with a casual *"Hello, who is calling?"*
  *Correction:* An office phone must be answered professionally by identifying the company and the speaker: *"Good morning, Zenith Logistics, Ngozi speaking. How may I direct your call?"*
- **Mistake 2:** Leaving the reception desk completely empty to run personal errands or buy snacks.
  *Correction:* The reception desk must never be abandoned unattended. If the receptionist must take a break, an appointed relief clerk must cover the desk.
- **Mistake 3:** Disclosing sensitive executive movements to casual visitors (e.g., *"The Director has traveled to London with his family"*).
  *Correction:* A receptionist must exercise strict confidentiality, stating simply: *"The Director is currently out of the office on official assignment. May I take a message?"*
- **Mistake 4:** Slamming down the telephone receiver immediately after finishing speaking.
  *Correction:* Always allow the caller to hang up first, or replace the receiver gently onto its cradle to avoid an insulting, loud crash in the caller's ear.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Scripting a Professional Telephone Interaction
*Scenario:* An external client, Chief Adebayo, calls the Managing Director of Horizon Construction Ltd at 10:15 AM. The Managing Director is currently chairing a closed board meeting and cannot take calls.
- Script the exact telephone dialogue conducted by the receptionist (Kemi).

*Dialogue Script:*
- **Receptionist:** *"Good morning, Horizon Construction Limited, Kemi speaking. How may I help you?"*
- **Chief Adebayo:** *"Hello, I need to speak with your Managing Director, Alhaji Garba, immediately!"*
- **Receptionist:** *"Thank you, sir. May I know who is calling, please?"*
- **Chief Adebayo:** *"This is Chief Adebayo from Apex Steel Mills."*
- **Receptionist:** *"Thank you, Chief Adebayo. The Managing Director is currently chairing an executive board meeting that will conclude by 12:30 PM. He is unavailable to take calls right now. May I take a detailed message for him, or would you prefer him to return your call as soon as the meeting ends?"*
- **Chief Adebayo:** *"Please tell him that the steel shipment has arrived at Apapa port and I need his confirmation. My phone number is 0802-345-6789."*
- **Receptionist:** *"Thank you, Chief Adebayo. Let me confirm: the steel shipment has arrived at Apapa port, you require his confirmation, and your number is 0802-345-6789. I will place this message on his desk immediately. Thank you for calling Horizon Construction, sir. Have a wonderful day."*
