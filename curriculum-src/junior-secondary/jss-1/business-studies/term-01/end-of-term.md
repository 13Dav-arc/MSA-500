# First Term Terminal Examination: Business Studies

This comprehensive terminal examination rigorously evaluates student mastery, commercial reasoning, and administrative problem-solving across the complete First Term curriculum:
- **Week 01:** Introduction to Business Studies (Meaning, the five core components: Commerce, Bookkeeping, Office Practice, Keyboarding, Marketing; profit calculation formula; vocational and corporate career opportunities).
- **Week 02:** The Office (Meaning as organizational nerve center; the six core functions: receiving, recording, processing, storing, giving out information, and safeguarding assets; small vs. large offices; comparative analysis of open-plan vs. closed/private office layouts).
- **Week 03:** Office Staff and Departments (Administrative structure and organograms; functions of HR, Accounts, Purchasing, Marketing, and Transport departments; staff hierarchy: top, middle, and junior clerical staff; types of clerks; essential qualities of an outstanding clerk: confidentiality, tact, integrity, and punctuality).
- **Week 04:** Right Attitude to Work (Punctuality, regularity, diligence, devotion to duty, and team spirit; personal, organizational, and national rewards; manifestations and consequences of negative work attitudes: tardiness, absenteeism, and eye-service).
- **Midterm:** Midterm Diagnostic Synthesis of Weeks 01–04.
- **Week 06:** Introduction to Commerce and Trade (Commerce equation: Trade + Aids to Trade; divisions of home trade: wholesale and retail, breaking bulk; divisions of foreign trade: import, export, entrepot; the six essential aids to trade: banking, insurance, transport, warehousing, advertising, and communication).
- **Week 07:** Production in Business (Meaning of production as creation of utility; form, place, time, and possession utility; the three sequential stages: primary/extractive, secondary/manufacturing, tertiary/services; the four factors of production and their rewards: Land [Rent], Labor [Wages], Capital [Interest], Entrepreneur [Profit/Loss]).
- **Week 08:** Forms of Business Ownership (Sole Proprietorship vs. Partnership; the legal doctrine of unlimited liability; the Partnership Deed; categories of partners: active, dormant/sleeping, nominal/quasi, and limited partners).
- **Week 09:** Consumer, Market, and Society (Consumer sovereignty; definition of a market; the eight universal consumer rights; responsibilities of an enlightened consumer; statutory mandates of FCCPC, NAFDAC, and SON).
