## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: In the Hindu-Arabic numeral system, what is the place value of the digit 4 in the number 245,671,890?
A) Ten Millions (correct)
B) Millions
C) Hundred Millions
D) Hundred Thousands
Feedback: Counting from the right: Units, Tens, Hundreds, Thousands, Ten Thousands, Hundred Thousands, Millions, Ten Millions (4).

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the Hindu-Arabic equivalent of the Roman numeral MCMLXXXIV?
A) 1984 (correct)
B) 1964
C) 2084
D) 1884
Feedback: $\text{M} = 1000$; $\text{CM} = 900$; $\text{LXXX} = 80$; $\text{IV} = 4$. Total = $1000 + 900 + 80 + 4 = 1984$.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: Write the number "Eight billion, twenty-four million, five hundred thousand and twelve" in figures:
A) 8,024,500,012 (correct)
B) 8,240,500,012
C) 8,024,050,012
D) 8,240,050,120
Feedback: 8 Billions ($8$), 024 Millions ($024$), 500 Thousands ($500$), 012 Units ($012$) $\rightarrow 8,024,500,012$.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: Express 458,206 in expanded notation using powers of 10:
A) (4 × 10⁵) + (5 × 10⁴) + (8 × 10³) + (2 × 10²) + (6 × 10⁰) (correct)
B) (4 × 10⁶) + (5 × 10⁵) + (8 × 10⁴) + (2 × 10²) + (6 × 10¹)
C) (4 × 10⁵) + (5 × 10⁴) + (8 × 10³) + (2 × 10¹) + (6 × 10⁰)
D) (4 × 10⁴) + (5 × 10³) + (8 × 10²) + (2 × 10¹) + (6 × 10⁰)
Feedback: The place values are $4 \times 10^5$, $5 \times 10^4$, $8 \times 10^3$, $2 \times 10^2$, $0 \times 10^1$, and $6 \times 10^0$.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the difference between the total value of the digit 7 and the digit 3 in the number 75,340?
A) 69,700 (correct)
B) 67,000
C) 72,000
D) 40,000
Feedback: Value of 7 is $70,000$; value of 3 is $300$. Difference = $70,000 - 300 = 69,700$.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the Roman numeral for 450?
A) CDL (correct)
B) LD
C) CCCCL
D) MCD
Feedback: $450 = 400 + 50$. $400 = \text{CD}$, $50 = \text{L}$, giving $\text{CDL}$.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following numbers is a prime number?
A) 43 (correct)
B) 49
C) 51
D) 57
Feedback: 43 is prime; $49 = 7 \times 7$, $51 = 3 \times 17$, and $57 = 3 \times 19$ are composite numbers.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the prime factorization of 144 in index form?
A) 2⁴ × 3² (correct)
B) 2³ × 3³
C) 2⁵ × 3
D) 12²
Feedback: $144 = 16 \times 9 = 2^4 \times 3^2$.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 36, 54, and 90?
A) 18 (correct)
B) 9
C) 6
D) 27
Feedback: $36 = 2^2 \times 3^2$; $54 = 2 \times 3^3$; $90 = 2 \times 3^2 \times 5$. Common primes with lowest powers: $2^1 \times 3^2 = 18$.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of 15, 20, and 30?
A) 60 (correct)
B) 120
C) 90
D) 300
Feedback: $15 = 3 \times 5$; $20 = 2^2 \times 5$; $30 = 2 \times 3 \times 5$. $\text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3 \times 5 = 4 \times 15 = 60$.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: How many factors does the number 36 possess in total?
A) 9 factors (correct)
B) 8 factors
C) 6 factors
D) 10 factors
Feedback: Factors of 36 are {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36} (exactly 9 factors).

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: Two traffic lights flash at intervals of 14 seconds and 21 seconds respectively. If they flash together, after how many seconds will they next flash together?
A) 42 seconds (correct)
B) 84 seconds
C) 35 seconds
D) 294 seconds
Feedback: $\text{LCM}(14, 21)$: $14 = 2 \times 7$, $21 = 3 \times 7$. $\text{LCM} = 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$ seconds.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: Which number between 1 and 100 has exactly three factors?
A) 25 (correct)
B) 15
C) 27
D) 30
Feedback: Squares of prime numbers have exactly three factors. $25 = 5^2$, with factors {1, 5, 25}.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the smallest non-zero whole number divisible by 6, 9, and 15?
A) 90 (correct)
B) 45
C) 180
D) 60
Feedback: $\text{LCM}(6, 9, 15)$: $6 = 2 \times 3$, $9 = 3^2$, $15 = 3 \times 5$. $\text{LCM} = 2 \times 3^2 \times 5 = 90$.

### Q15 [single-choice]
Stem: Convert the improper fraction 47/9 into a mixed number:
A) 5 2/9 (correct)
B) 5 4/9
C) 4 7/9
D) 5 1/9
Feedback: $47 \div 9 = 5$ with remainder 2, giving $5\frac{2}{9}$.

### Q16 [single-choice]
Stem: Convert the mixed number 7 3/8 into an improper fraction:
A) 59/8 (correct)
B) 56/8
C) 29/8
D) 61/8
Feedback: $\frac{(7 \times 8) + 3}{8} = \frac{56 + 3}{8} = \frac{59}{8}$.

### Q17 [single-choice]
Stem: Reduce the fraction 48/64 to its simplest lowest terms:
A) 3/4 (correct)
B) 6/8
C) 12/16
D) 5/8
Feedback: $\text{HCF}(48, 64) = 16$. $\frac{48 \div 16}{64 \div 16} = \frac{3}{4}$.

### Q18 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: 3/5 + 2/7
A) 31/35 (correct)
B) 5/12
C) 29/35
D) 1 1/35
Feedback: $\text{LCM}(5, 7) = 35$. $\frac{21}{35} + \frac{10}{35} = \frac{31}{35}$.

### Q19 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: 4 1/3 - 2 5/6
A) 1 1/2 (correct)
B) 1 2/3
C) 2 1/6
D) 1 1/6
Feedback: $\frac{13}{3} - \frac{17}{6} = \frac{26 - 17}{6} = \frac{9}{6} = \frac{3}{2} = 1\frac{1}{2}$.

### Q20 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the product of 5/12 and 8/15 in simplest form?

B) 40/180
C) 1/3
D) 4/15
Feedback: $\frac{5}{12} \times \frac{8}{15} = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2}{9}$.

B) ₦18,000

### Q22 [single-choice]

### Q36 [true-false]
Statement: The number 1 is a prime number because it can only be divided by 1.
Answer: false
Feedback: A prime number must possess exactly two distinct factors; 1 has only one factor, so it is neither prime nor composite.

### Q37 [true-false]
Statement: The product of any non-zero fraction and its reciprocal is always equal to 1.
Answer: true
Feedback: $\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{b}{a} = 1$.

### Q38 [true-false]
Statement: The common fraction 3/8 produces a terminating decimal when converted into decimal form.
Answer: true
Feedback: $\frac{3}{8} = 0.375$, which terminates because the denominator has only prime factors of 2.

### Q39 [true-false]
Statement: On the integer number line, -12 is greater in value than -5.
Answer: false
Feedback: On the number line, $-5$ lies to the right of $-12$, making $-5 > -12$.

### Q40 [true-false]
Statement: When multiplying or dividing two negative integers, the resulting answer is always a positive number.
Answer: true
Feedback: Operating on identical negative signs produces a positive result: $(-) \times (-) = (+)$ and $(-) \div (-) = (+)$.

### Q41 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO numbers that are prime numbers between 20 and 40:
Correct: 29, 37
Incorrect: 27, 39
Feedback: 29 and 37 are prime numbers; $27 = 3 \times 9$ and $39 = 3 \times 13$ are composite numbers.

### Q42 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO fractions that are equivalent to 3/4:
Correct: 15/20, 18/24
Incorrect: 9/15, 12/20
Feedback: $\frac{3 \times 5}{4 \times 5} = \frac{15}{20}$ and $\frac{3 \times 6}{4 \times 6} = \frac{18}{24}$. $\frac{9}{15} = \frac{3}{5}$ and $\frac{12}{20} = \frac{3}{5}$.

### Q43 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO decimals that are greater than 0.35:
Correct: 0.4, 0.38
Incorrect: 0.305, 0.09
Feedback: $0.4 = 0.40$ and $0.38$ are greater than $0.35$; $0.305$ and $0.09$ are smaller.

### Q44 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO expressions that evaluate to a positive integer:
Correct: (-8) × (-3), (-32) ÷ (-4)
Incorrect: (-6) + (-2), (+14) × (-2)
Feedback: $(-8) \times (-3) = +24$ and $(-32) \div (-4) = +8$. $(-6) + (-2) = -8$ and $14 \times (-2) = -28$.

### Q45 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO mathematical values that represent 75%:
Correct: 3/4, 0.75
Incorrect: 7.5, 7/5
Feedback: $75\% = \frac{75}{100} = \frac{3}{4} = 0.75$. 7.5 is 750% and 7/5 is 140%.

### Q46 [matching]

### Q47 [matching]
Stem: Match each pair of numbers with their Highest Common Factor (HCF):
Pairs:
- 24 and 32 -> 8
- 30 and 45 -> 15

Feedback: $\text{HCF}(24, 32) = 8$; $\text{HCF}(30, 45) = 15$; $\text{HCF}(42, 56) = 14$.

### Q48 [matching]
Stem: Match each fraction operation with its evaluated result:
Pairs:
- 2/3 + 1/4 -> 11/12
- 2/3 - 1/4 -> 5/12
- 2/3 × 1/4 -> 1/6
Feedback: $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{11}{12}$; $\frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{5}{12}$; $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{12} = \frac{1}{6}$.

### Q49 [matching]
Stem: Match each percentage with its equivalent reduced fraction:
Pairs:
- 25% -> 1/4
- 50% -> 1/2
- 75% -> 3/4
Feedback: $25\% = \frac{1}{4}$; $50\% = \frac{1}{2}$; $75\% = \frac{3}{4}$.

### Q50 [matching]
Stem: Match each directed arithmetic problem with its correct answer:
Pairs:
- -8 + 12 -> 4
- -8 - 4 -> -12
- -8 × (-2) -> 16
Feedback: $-8 + 12 = 4$; $-8 - 4 = -12$; $(-8) \times (-2) = 16$.
