## Hook
If a commercial market trader earns a profit of ₦5,000 on Monday but suffers a business loss of ₦8,000 on Tuesday, how is her financial balance represented mathematically? Or suppose an aircraft cruises at 10,000 metres above sea level while a marine submarine explores an oceanic trench 500 metres beneath the water surface. Ordinary positive numbers cannot distinguish between gaining money and owing debts, or rising above the clouds and plunging beneath the waves. Directed numbers, known formally as integers, introduce negative numbers into mathematics, providing a symmetrical number line that accounts for gains, losses, debts, depths, and freezing temperatures.

## Key Terms
- Term: Integers (Directed Numbers)
  Definition: The complete set of whole numbers comprising positive integers, negative integers, and zero: $\{\dots, -3, -2, -1, 0, +1, +2, +3, \dots\}$.
- Term: Positive Integers
  Definition: Whole numbers located strictly to the right of zero on the number line ($+1, +2, +3, \dots$), representing values greater than zero.
- Term: Negative Integers
  Definition: Whole numbers located strictly to the left of zero on the number line ($-1, -2, -3, \dots$), representing values less than zero and marked with an obligatory minus sign ($-$).
- Term: Number Line
  Definition: A continuous horizontal straight line on which points correspond to real numbers, with zero as the neutral origin, positive numbers increasing to the right, and negative numbers decreasing to the left.
- Term: Absolute Value (Modulus)
  Definition: The non-negative numerical distance of an integer from zero on the number line regardless of its directional sign, symbolized by vertical bars (e.g., $|-7| = 7$ and $|+7| = 7$).

## Core Concept
### 1. Meaning and Real-World Applications of Directed Numbers
In basic science and mathematics, quantities have both magnitude (size) and direction:
- **Financial Balance:**
  - Depositing money, receiving salary, or making a profit is **positive** ($+₦10,000$).
  - Withdrawing money, incurring debt, or recording a loss is **negative** ($-₦4,500$).
- **Temperature:**
  - Temperatures above the freezing point of water ($0^\circ\text{C}$) are **positive** ($+28^\circ\text{C}$).
  - Temperatures below freezing (such as polar ice or a laboratory deep freezer) are **negative** ($-15^\circ\text{C}$).
- **Elevation and Altitude:**
  - Mountain peaks above sea level are **positive** ($+1,200\text{ m}$).
  - Sea level is the neutral origin ($0\text{ m}$).
  - Submarine trenches or underground mines below sea level are **negative** ($-350\text{ m}$).
- **The Role of Zero ($0$):**
  Zero is the central reference point. It is **neither positive nor negative**.

### 2. The Integer Number Line
On a horizontal number line:
$$\longleftarrow \text{ Decreasing (Smaller)} \quad\quad\quad\quad\quad \text{Increasing (Larger)} \longrightarrow$$
$$\dots, -5, -4, -3, -2, -1, \quad 0, \quad +1, +2, +3, +4, +5, \dots$$
- Any integer situated to the **right** of another integer has a **greater value**:
  - $+4 > +1$
  - $0 > -3$ ($0$ is greater than every negative integer)
  - $-1 > -6$ ($-1$ is to the right of $-6$, so owing $₦1$ is better than owing $₦6$)
  - $-2 > -100$

### 3. Addition and Subtraction of Directed Numbers
- **Rules on the Number Line:**
  1. *Adding a positive number:* Move to the **right** ($+5 + 3 = +8$).
  2. *Adding a negative number:* Adding a debt means moving to the **left**:
     $$a + (-b) = a - b \quad \rightarrow \quad 7 + (-4) = 7 - 4 = 3$$
  3. *Subtracting a positive number:* Taking away a gain means moving to the **left**:
     $$a - (+b) = a - b \quad \rightarrow \quad 2 - (+5) = 2 - 5 = -3$$
  4. *Subtracting a negative number:* Taking away a debt creates a gain; the two minus signs combine into a plus sign, moving to the **right**:
     $$a - (-b) = a + b \quad \rightarrow \quad 4 - (-3) = 4 + 3 = 7$$
     $$-6 - (-8) = -6 + 8 = 2$$

### 4. Multiplication and Division of Directed Numbers
The rules for signs in multiplication and division depend on whether the signs are identical or different:
- **Identical Signs (Like Signs) Produce a Positive Result ($+$):**
  $$\begin{aligned}
  (+) \times (+) &= (+) \quad\rightarrow\quad (+4) \times (+3) = +12 \\
  (-) \times (-) &= (+) \quad\rightarrow\quad (-4) \times (-3) = +12 \\
  (+) \div (+) &= (+) \quad\rightarrow\quad (+20) \div (+4) = +5 \\
  (-) \div (-) &= (+) \quad\rightarrow\quad (-20) \div (-4) = +5
  \end{aligned}$$
- **Opposite Signs (Unlike Signs) Produce a Negative Result ($-$):**
  $$\begin{aligned}
  (+) \times (-) &= (-) \quad\rightarrow\quad (+5) \times (-3) = -15 \\
  (-) \times (+) &= (-) \quad\rightarrow\quad (-5) \times (+3) = -15 \\
  (+) \div (-) &= (-) \quad\rightarrow\quad (+24) \div (-6) = -4 \\
  (-) \div (+) &= (-) \quad\rightarrow\quad (-24) \div (+6) = -4
  \end{aligned}$$

## Comparative Matrix
Heading: Comparing Directed Numbers and Operational Rules
- Label: Positive vs Negative
  Title: Greater than Zero vs Less than Zero
  Body: Positive integers lie to the right of zero and represent surplus, elevations, and gains; negative integers lie to the left of zero, requiring minus signs, and represent debts and sub-zero values.
- Label: Addition of Negative vs Subtraction of Negative
  Title: Subtracting Quantity vs Adding Opposite
  Body: Adding a negative integer reduces value ($7 + (-3) = 4$); subtracting a negative integer eliminates debt, effectively increasing value ($7 - (-3) = 10$).
- Label: Like Signs vs Unlike Signs
  Title: Positive Product/Quotient vs Negative Product/Quotient
  Body: Multiplying or dividing two numbers with identical signs always yields a positive result; operating on two numbers with different signs always yields a negative result.
- Label: Greater Negative vs Greater Absolute Value
  Title: Spatial Position vs Magnitude Distance
  Body: As negative numbers move further left, their absolute magnitude increases ($|-50| = 50$), but their actual numerical value decreases ($-50 < -5$).

## Common Mistakes
- Mistake: Thinking that $-8$ is greater than $-2$ because $8$ is larger than $2$.
  Fact: On the negative side of the number line, numbers decrease in value as they move left. $-2$ is greater than $-8$ ($-2 > -8$).
- Mistake: Forgetting that subtracting a negative number produces an addition (e.g., evaluating $5 - (-3)$ as $2$ instead of $8$).
  Fact: Double negative signs invert direction: $5 - (-3) = 5 + 3 = 8$.
- Mistake: Claiming that zero is a positive integer.
  Fact: Zero ($0$) is the neutral origin; it is neither positive nor negative.

## Worked Examples
### Example 1: Solving an Everyday Thermal Temperature Differential
Context: In a cold storage laboratory, a deep freezer maintains a temperature of $-18^\circ\text{C}$, while the ambient laboratory room temperature is $+24^\circ\text{C}$.
Problem: What is the total temperature difference between the room and the interior of the deep freezer?
Fix:
1. Difference = $\text{Higher Temperature} - \text{Lower Temperature}$.
2. Calculation: $+24 - (-18)$.
3. Subtracting a negative yields addition: $24 + 18 = 42^\circ\text{C}$.
The temperature difference between the room and the freezer is $42^\circ\text{C}$.

### Example 2: Evaluating a Multi-Operation Integer Expression
Context: A student evaluates signed algebraic terms during an arithmetic test.
Problem: Evaluate: $(-4) \times (-6) + (-36) \div (+9) - (-7)$.
Fix:
1. Multiplication first: $(-4) \times (-6) = +24$ (like signs produce positive).
2. Division next: $(-36) \div (+9) = -4$ (unlike signs produce negative).
3. Expression becomes: $24 + (-4) - (-7)$.
4. Addition of negative: $24 - 4 = 20$.
5. Subtraction of negative: $20 - (-7) = 20 + 7 = 27$.
The final evaluated answer is $27$.

### Example 3: Solving a Bank Overdraft Financial Word Problem
Context: A trader's bank account has an opening balance of $₦12,000$. She issues a business cheque for $₦20,000$, and the bank honors the payment by granting an overdraft. Later that afternoon, she deposits $₦5,000$ cash into the account.
Problem: Express the trader's final account status as a directed number and explain its meaning.
Fix:
1. Initial balance = $+12,000$.
2. Cheque deduction = $-20,000 \rightarrow 12,000 - 20,000 = -8,000$ (an overdraft debt of $₦8,000$).
3. Cash deposit = $+5,000 \rightarrow -8,000 + 5,000 = -3,000$.
The account balance is $-₦3,000$, meaning she currently owes the bank $₦3,000$.

## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following numbers is an integer located to the left of zero on the number line?
A) -7 (correct)
B) +5
C) 0
D) 1/2
Feedback: Negative integers like -7 are located strictly to the left of zero on the horizontal number line.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Which comparison statement between negative integers is mathematically correct?
A) -3 > -9 (correct)
B) -10 > -2
C) -15 > 0
D) -8 > +1
Feedback: On the number line, -3 is located to the right of -9, which means -3 is greater than -9 ($-3 > -9$).

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: 8 + (-12)
A) -4 (correct)
B) 4
C) 20
D) -20
Feedback: Adding a negative number is equivalent to subtraction: $8 - 12 = -4$.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: -15 - (-7)
A) -8 (correct)
B) -22
C) 8
D) 22
Feedback: Subtracting a negative creates an addition: $-15 + 7 = -8$.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the product of (-6) and (-8)?
A) +48 (correct)
B) -48
C) -14
D) +14
Feedback: Multiplying two numbers with identical negative signs yields a positive product: $(-) \times (-) = (+)$, so $(-6) \times (-8) = +48$.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: (-45) ÷ (+9)
A) -5 (correct)
B) +5
C) -9
D) +9
Feedback: Dividing numbers with unlike signs yields a negative quotient: $(-) \div (+) = (-)$, so $(-45) \div 9 = -5$.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the neutral integer that is neither positive nor negative?
A) 0 (correct)
B) 1
C) -1
D) Infinity
Feedback: Zero is the origin of the number line; it is neither positive nor negative.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: A submarine cruises at an elevation of -250 metres relative to sea level. It descends a further 120 metres. What is its new depth?
A) -370 metres (correct)
B) -130 metres
C) +130 metres
D) +370 metres
Feedback: $-250 - 120 = -370$ metres.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: (-3) × (+4) × (-2)
A) +24 (correct)
B) -24
C) -12
D) +12
Feedback: $(-3) \times (+4) = -12$. Then $(-12) \times (-2) = +24$.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the value of the expression: -10 + 15 - 8?
A) -3 (correct)
B) +3
C) +17
D) -13
Feedback: $-10 + 15 = 5$. Then $5 - 8 = -3$.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: The temperature in a northern city at midnight is -4°C. By midday, the temperature has risen by 11°C. What is the midday temperature?
A) +7°C (correct)
B) -15°C
C) +15°C
D) -7°C
Feedback: $-4^\circ\text{C} + 11^\circ\text{C} = +7^\circ\text{C}$.

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: (-72) ÷ (-8)
A) +9 (correct)
B) -9
C) -64
D) +80
Feedback: Dividing two numbers with like signs gives a positive result: $(-) \div (-) = (+)$, so $(-72) \div (-8) = +9$.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: Arrange the integers -4, +3, 0, -8, +1 in ascending order (from smallest to largest):
A) -8, -4, 0, +1, +3 (correct)
B) +3, +1, 0, -4, -8
C) -4, -8, 0, +1, +3
D) 0, -4, -8, +1, +3
Feedback: Numbers furthest to the left on the number line are smallest: $-8 < -4 < 0 < +1 < +3$.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the absolute value (modulus) of -25?
A) 25 (correct)
B) -25
C) 0
D) 1/25
Feedback: The absolute value $|-25|$ represents distance from zero, which is always non-negative: $|-25| = 25$.

### Q15 [true-false]
Statement: Multiplying two negative integers together always produces a negative product.
Answer: false
Feedback: Multiplying two negative numbers (identical signs) always produces a positive product: $(-) \times (-) = (+)$.

### Q16 [true-false]
Statement: Zero is greater in value than any negative integer on the number line.
Answer: true
Feedback: Because zero is situated to the right of every negative integer, it is greater than any negative number ($0 > -n$).

### Q17 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO calculations that result in a positive integer (+):
Correct: (-5) × (-4), (-16) ÷ (-2)
Incorrect: (-8) + (+3), (+12) × (-2)
Feedback: $(-5) \times (-4) = +20$ and $(-16) \div (-2) = +8$. $(-8) + 3 = -5$ and $(+12) \times (-2) = -24$.

### Q18 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO integers that are smaller than -5:
Correct: -6, -10
Incorrect: -4, 0
Feedback: $-6$ and $-10$ lie to the left of $-5$ and are smaller; $-4$ and $0$ lie to the right and are greater.

### Q19 [matching]
Stem: Match each directed arithmetic problem with its correct evaluated answer:
Pairs:
- 5 - 9 -> -4
- -5 - 4 -> -9
- 5 - (-4) -> 9
Feedback: $5 - 9 = -4$; $-5 - 4 = -9$; $5 - (-4) = 5 + 4 = 9$.

### Q20 [matching]
Stem: Match each real-world scenario with its corresponding directed number:
Pairs:
- A bank deposit of ₦10,000 -> +10000
- A debt overdraft of ₦5,000 -> -5000
- Sea level elevation -> 0
Feedback: Deposits are $+10000$, debts are $-5000$, and sea level is $0$.
