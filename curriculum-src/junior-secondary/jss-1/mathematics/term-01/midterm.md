## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the place value of the digit 8 in the number 28,451,903?
A) Millions (correct)
B) Hundred Thousands
C) Ten Millions
D) Thousands
Feedback: From the right: Units(3), Tens(0), Hundreds(9), Thousands(1), Ten Thousands(5), Hundred Thousands(4), Millions(8).

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Express the Roman numeral MCMXLVIII in Hindu-Arabic figures:
A) 1948 (correct)
B) 1968
C) 2148
D) 1848
Feedback: $\text{M} = 1000$; $\text{CM} = 900$; $\text{XL} = 40$; $\text{VIII} = 8$. Sum = $1000 + 900 + 40 + 8 = 1948$.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following numbers is a prime number?
A) 37 (correct)
B) 39
C) 49
D) 51
Feedback: 37 has only two factors (1 and 37). $39 = 3 \times 13$, $49 = 7 \times 7$, and $51 = 3 \times 17$ are composite numbers.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the prime factorization of 90 in index notation?
A) 2 × 3² × 5 (correct)
B) 2² × 3 × 5
C) 2 × 3 × 5²
D) 9 × 10
Feedback: $90 = 2 \times 45 = 2 \times 3 \times 15 = 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 = 2 \times 3^2 \times 5$.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: Find the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 36 and 48:
A) 12 (correct)
B) 6
C) 4
D) 18
Feedback: $36 = 2^2 \times 3^2$; $48 = 2^4 \times 3$. $\text{HCF} = 2^2 \times 3^1 = 4 \times 3 = 12$.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: Find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of 12 and 18:
A) 36 (correct)
B) 72
C) 54
D) 24
Feedback: $12 = 2^2 \times 3$; $18 = 2 \times 3^2$. $\text{LCM} = 2^2 \times 3^2 = 4 \times 9 = 36$.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: Convert 31/7 into a mixed number:
A) 4 3/7 (correct)
B) 4 2/7
C) 5 1/7
D) 3 6/7
Feedback: $31 \div 7 = 4$ with a remainder of 3. Therefore, $\frac{31}{7} = 4\frac{3}{7}$.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: Convert the mixed number 6 2/5 into an improper fraction:
A) 32/5 (correct)
B) 30/5
C) 17/5
D) 62/5
Feedback: $\frac{(6 \times 5) + 2}{5} = \frac{30 + 2}{5} = \frac{32}{5}$.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: Reduce the fraction 45/60 to its lowest terms:
A) 3/4 (correct)
B) 9/12
C) 5/6
D) 15/20
Feedback: $\text{HCF}(45, 60) = 15$. $\frac{45 \div 15}{60 \div 15} = \frac{3}{4}$.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: 2/3 + 3/5
A) 1 4/15 (correct)
B) 5/8
C) 1 1/15
D) 13/15
Feedback: $\text{LCM}(3, 5) = 15$. $\frac{10}{15} + \frac{9}{15} = \frac{19}{15} = 1\frac{4}{15}$.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: 4 1/2 - 1 3/4
A) 2 3/4 (correct)
B) 3 1/4
C) 2 1/4
D) 3 3/4
Feedback: $\frac{9}{2} - \frac{7}{4} = \frac{18 - 7}{4} = \frac{11}{4} = 2\frac{3}{4}$.

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: What is 3/7 of ₦42,000?
A) ₦18,000 (correct)
B) ₦14,000
C) ₦21,000
D) ₦6,000
Feedback: $\frac{3}{7} \times ₦42,000 = 3 \times ₦6,000 = ₦18,000$.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: 5/6 ÷ 2/3
A) 1 1/4 (correct)
B) 5/9
C) 10/18
D) 1/2
Feedback: $\frac{5}{6} \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{5 \times 1}{2 \times 2} = \frac{5}{4} = 1\frac{1}{4}$.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate using BODMAS: 3/4 - 1/2 × 2/3
A) 5/12 (correct)
B) 1/6
C) 1/3
D) 7/12
Feedback: Multiply first: $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{1}{3}$. Then subtract: $\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{9 - 4}{12} = \frac{5}{12}$.

### Q15 [true-false]
Statement: The number 2 is both an even number and a prime number.
Answer: true
Feedback: 2 is divisible only by 1 and 2, making it the only even prime number.

### Q16 [true-false]
Statement: The reciprocal of 1/5 is 5.
Answer: true
Feedback: Inverting $\frac{1}{5}$ gives $\frac{5}{1} = 5$, and $\frac{1}{5} \times 5 = 1$.

### Q17 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO fractions that are equivalent to 2/3:
Correct: 8/12, 10/15
Incorrect: 4/9, 6/12
Feedback: $\frac{2 \times 4}{3 \times 4} = \frac{8}{12}$ and $\frac{2 \times 5}{3 \times 5} = \frac{10}{15}$. $\frac{4}{9} \neq \frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$.

### Q18 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO prime numbers between 40 and 50:
Correct: 41, 47
Incorrect: 45, 49
Feedback: 41 and 47 are prime numbers; $45 = 5 \times 9$ and $49 = 7 \times 7$ are composite numbers.

### Q19 [matching]
Stem: Match each numeral expression with its correct value:
Pairs:
- Roman Numeral XCIV -> 94
- Prime Factors of 12 in Index Form -> 2² × 3
- Reciprocal of 4/5 -> 5/4
Feedback: $\text{XCIV} = 94$; $12 = 2^2 \times 3$; reciprocal of $\frac{4}{5}$ is $\frac{5}{4}$.

### Q20 [matching]
Stem: Match each fraction problem with its correct evaluated answer:
Pairs:
- 1/3 + 1/4 -> 7/12
- 1/3 × 1/4 -> 1/12
- 1/3 ÷ 1/4 -> 4/3
Feedback: $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{7}{12}$; $\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{12}$; $\frac{1}{3} \div \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{3} \times 4 = \frac{4}{3}$.
