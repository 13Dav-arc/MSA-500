## Hook
Over the past eight weeks of this first academic term, you have established the core building blocks of mathematical fluency. You have decoded place values extending into billions, unraveled ancient Roman numeral systems, and factored numbers into their prime components. You have manipulated fractional pieces through common denominators and reciprocals, linked fractions seamlessly with terminating and recurring decimals, negotiated real-world financial percentages, and navigated positive and negative signed integers on the number line. This 80% Mastery Gate assessment is your diagnostic platform to synthesize these foundational skills, resolve lingering conceptual errors, and demonstrate your absolute readiness for the First Term Terminal Examination!

## Key Terms
- Term: Mastery Gate
  Definition: A diagnostic review and assessment block designed to confirm that learners have achieved at least 80% mastery of core mathematical concepts before terminal examination grading.
- Term: Place Value
  Definition: The positional worth assigned to a digit based on powers of ten in the decimal Hindu-Arabic numeral system.
- Term: Prime Factorization
  Definition: Decomposing a composite number into a unique product of prime numbers, commonly expressed using index notation.
- Term: Reciprocal
  Definition: The multiplicative inverse of a fraction or whole number ($\frac{a}{b} \rightarrow \frac{b}{a}$) whose product with the original value equals 1.
- Term: Directed Numbers (Integers)
  Definition: Numbers possessing both magnitude and directional sign ($+$, $-$, or neutral $0$), ordered symmetrically along the number line.

## Core Concept
### 1. Number Systems and Place Value (Week 01)
- **Decimal System (Base 10):** Standard positional place value periods: Units ($10^0$), Thousands ($10^3$), Millions ($10^6$), Billions ($10^9$).
- **Roman Numerals:** Seven core symbols: $\text{I}(1), \text{V}(5), \text{X}(10), \text{L}(50), \text{C}(100), \text{D}(500), \text{M}(1000)$.
  - Additive order: $\text{VI} = 6, \text{LX} = 60, \text{MC} = 1100$.
  - Subtractive order: $\text{IV} = 4, \text{IX} = 9, \text{XL} = 40, \text{XC} = 90, \text{CD} = 400, \text{CM} = 900$.
  - Note: Roman numerals contain **no symbol for zero**.

### 2. Factors, Multiples, HCF, and LCM (Week 02)
- **Primes vs. Composites:** A prime number has exactly two factors ($1$ and itself); composite numbers have more than two factors. $2$ is the only even prime; $1$ is neither prime nor composite.
- **Prime Factorization:** Expressing numbers in powers of primes (e.g., $180 = 2^2 \times 3^2 \times 5^1$).
- **Highest Common Factor (HCF):** Product of common prime factors with their **lowest powers**.
- **Lowest Common Multiple (LCM):** Product of all prime factors with their **highest powers**.

### 3. Fractions and Operational Rules (Weeks 03 & 04)
- **Fraction Anatomy:** $\frac{\text{Numerator}}{\text{Denominator}}$ (Denominator $b \neq 0$).
  - Proper: $\frac{a}{b} < 1$ (e.g., $\frac{3}{5}$).
  - Improper: $\frac{a}{b} \geq 1$ (e.g., $\frac{7}{4} = 1\frac{3}{4}$).
  - Equivalent Fractions: Multiply or divide top and bottom by identical non-zero integers ($\frac{a \times k}{b \times k}$).
  - Simplest Form: Divide top and bottom by their HCF.
- **Fraction Arithmetic:**
  - Addition/Subtraction: Convert to a common denominator using the LCM.
  - Multiplication: Multiply numerators together and denominators together; cancel common factors.
  - Division: Invert the divisor into its **reciprocal** and multiply ($\frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a}{b} \times \frac{d}{c}$).
  - **BODMAS:** Brackets $\rightarrow$ Of $\rightarrow$ Division $\rightarrow$ Multiplication $\rightarrow$ Addition $\rightarrow$ Subtraction.

### 4. Decimals and Percentage Mathematics (Weeks 06 & 07)
- **Decimal Positions:** Tenths ($0.1$), Hundredths ($0.01$), Thousandths ($0.001$).
- **Decimals to Fractions:** Write over power of 10 and reduce ($0.375 = \frac{375}{1000} = \frac{3}{8}$).
- **Fractions to Decimals:**
  - *Terminating:* Denominator in lowest terms has prime factors of only $2$ and/or $5$ (e.g., $\frac{1}{4} = 0.25$).
  - *Recurring:* Denominator contains other prime factors like $3$ or $7$ (e.g., $\frac{1}{3} = 0.\dot{3}$).
- **Decimal Operations:**
  - Addition/Subtraction: Align decimal points vertically.
  - Multiplication: Multiply as whole numbers, then insert the decimal point by counting total decimal places.
  - Powers of 10: Multiply $\rightarrow$ move point right; Divide $\rightarrow$ move point left.
- **Percentages ($x\% = \frac{x}{100} = 0.0x$):**
  - Finding percentage of a quantity: $\frac{\%}{100} \times \text{Total}$.
  - Expressing part of whole as percentage: $\frac{\text{Part}}{\text{Whole}} \times 100\%$.
  - Discounts: Deduct discount amount from marked price.

### 5. Directed Numbers and Integer Operations (Week 08)
- **The Number Line:** Positive numbers ($+n$) right of zero; negative numbers ($-n$) left of zero; zero is neutral. Numbers to the right are always greater ($-2 > -10$).
- **Signed Addition and Subtraction:**
  - Adding a negative is subtraction: $a + (-b) = a - b$.
  - Subtracting a negative is addition: $a - (-b) = a + b$.
- **Signed Multiplication and Division Rules:**
  - Like signs yield positive ($+$): $(-) \times (-) = (+)$, $(+) \times (+) = (+)$.
  - Unlike signs yield negative ($-$): $(+) \times (-) = (-)$, $(-) \times (+) = (-)$.

  Title: Directional Sign and Spatial Position
  Body: Positive integers lie right of zero and represent gains/surplus; negative integers lie left of zero and represent debts/freezing points; zero is the neutral boundary.

## Common Mistakes
- Mistake: Treating the number 1 as a prime number.
  Fact: A prime number must possess exactly two distinct factors. The number 1 has only one factor (itself), making it neither prime nor composite.
- Mistake: Adding denominators when adding fractions (e.g., $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{7}$).
  Fact: Denominators cannot be added directly. Convert both fractions to equivalent fractions with a common denominator (LCM) first: $\frac{4}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = \frac{7}{12}$.
- Mistake: Evaluating $-5 - (-8)$ as $-13$.
  Fact: Subtracting a negative creates a positive addition: $-5 - (-8) = -5 + 8 = +3$.
- Mistake: Thinking that $0.09$ is greater than $0.2$ because 9 is greater than 2.
  Fact: Decimals are compared from the tenths column. $0.2 = 0.20$ ($2$ tenths), which is greater than $0.09$ ($0$ tenths).

## Worked Examples
### Example 1: Comprehensive Multi-Topic Diagnostic Problem
Context: A secondary school student takes a term diagnostic revision exercise.
Problem: Evaluate the following expression using BODMAS:
$$\left(3\frac{1}{2} - 1\frac{1}{4}\right) \div \left(\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{4}{5}\right)$$
Fix:
1. First Bracket (Subtraction):
   - $3\frac{1}{2} - 1\frac{1}{4} = \frac{7}{2} - \frac{5}{4} = \frac{14 - 5}{4} = \frac{9}{4}$.
2. Second Bracket (Multiplication):
   - $\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{3 \times 1}{2 \times 5} = \frac{3}{10}$.
3. Division between results:
   - $\frac{9}{4} \div \frac{3}{10} = \frac{9}{4} \times \frac{10}{3}$.
4. Cross-cancel:
   - $9 \div 3 = 3$; $10$ and $4$ divide by $2$ to give $5$ and $2$.
   - $\frac{3 \times 5}{2 \times 1} = \frac{15}{2} = 7\frac{1}{2}$.
The evaluated result is $7\frac{1}{2}$ (or $7.5$).

### Example 2: Financial Percentage Calculation with Discounts
Context: A school bookstore purchases 50 mathematics workbooks at $₦1,200$ each. The publisher gives a $10\%$ bulk discount, but charges $₦2,500$ for transportation.
Problem: Calculate the total final cost incurred by the school bookstore.
Fix:
1. Marked total price = $50 \times ₦1,200 = ₦60,000$.
2. Bulk discount ($10\%$) = $\frac{10}{100} \times ₦60,000 = ₦6,000$.
3. Discounted book price = $₦60,000 - ₦6,000 = ₦54,000$.
4. Add transportation = $₦54,000 + ₦2,500 = ₦56,500$.
The bookstore pays a total of $₦56,500$.

### Example 3: Signed Integer Temperature Problem
Context: At 6:00 AM, the temperature recorded on a mountain peak is $-7^\circ\text{C}$. By 2:00 PM, the temperature rises by $15^\circ\text{C}$. By 10:00 PM, the temperature falls by $9^\circ\text{C}$.
Problem: What is the final temperature on the mountain peak at 10:00 PM?
Fix:
1. Starting temperature = $-7^\circ\text{C}$.
2. Afternoon rise (+): $-7 + 15 = +8^\circ\text{C}$.
3. Night fall (-): $+8 - 9 = -1^\circ\text{C}$.
The final temperature at 10:00 PM is $-1^\circ\text{C}$.

## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the place value of the digit 5 in the number 352,408,196?
A) Ten Millions (correct)
B) Millions
C) Hundred Millions
D) Hundred Thousands
Feedback: Counting positions from the right, the 5 occupies the Ten Millions column ($50,000,000$).

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Convert the Roman numeral MCMLXIX into standard Hindu-Arabic figures:
A) 1969 (correct)
B) 1949
C) 2069
D) 1869
Feedback: $\text{M} = 1000$; $\text{CM} = 900$; $\text{LX} = 60$; $\text{IX} = 9$. Sum = $1000 + 900 + 60 + 9 = 1969$.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the prime factorization of 120 expressed in index notation?
A) 2³ × 3 × 5 (correct)
B) 2² × 3² × 5
C) 2⁴ × 3 × 5
D) 8 × 15
Feedback: $120 = 2 \times 60 = 2 \times 2 \times 30 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 15 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 5$.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of 48 and 72?
A) 24 (correct)
B) 12
C) 8
D) 144
Feedback: $48 = 2^4 \times 3$; $72 = 2^3 \times 3^2$. $\text{HCF} = 2^3 \times 3 = 8 \times 3 = 24$.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of 8, 12, and 16?
A) 48 (correct)
B) 96
C) 24
D) 32
Feedback: $8 = 2^3$; $12 = 2^2 \times 3$; $16 = 2^4$. $\text{LCM} = 2^4 \times 3 = 16 \times 3 = 48$.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: Convert the improper fraction 37/8 into a mixed number:
A) 4 5/8 (correct)
B) 4 3/8
C) 5 3/8
D) 4 7/8
Feedback: $37 \div 8 = 4$ with remainder 5 $\rightarrow 4\frac{5}{8}$.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: 2/5 + 1/3
A) 11/15 (correct)
B) 3/8
C) 1/2
D) 7/15
Feedback: $\text{LCM}(5, 3) = 15$. $\frac{6}{15} + \frac{5}{15} = \frac{11}{15}$.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: 3 1/3 - 1 3/4
A) 1 7/12 (correct)
B) 2 1/12
C) 1 5/12
D) 2 7/12
Feedback: $\frac{10}{3} - \frac{7}{4} = \frac{40 - 21}{12} = \frac{19}{12} = 1\frac{7}{12}$.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: 3/8 ÷ 9/16
A) 2/3 (correct)
B) 3/4
C) 27/128
D) 1 1/2
Feedback: $\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{16}{9} = \frac{1 \times 2}{1 \times 3} = \frac{2}{3}$.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: Convert 0.08 into a common fraction in its lowest terms:
A) 2/25 (correct)
B) 8/10
C) 4/25
D) 1/12
Feedback: $0.08 = \frac{8}{100} = \frac{8 \div 4}{100 \div 4} = \frac{2}{25}$.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following fractions produces a terminating decimal?
A) 7/20 (correct)
B) 2/7
C) 5/9
D) 1/6
Feedback: $20 = 2^2 \times 5$ (only prime factors of 2 and 5). $\frac{7}{20} = 0.35$ (terminating).

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: Calculate 25% of ₦24,000:
A) ₦6,000 (correct)
B) ₦4,800
C) ₦8,000
D) ₦5,000
Feedback: $25\% = \frac{1}{4}$. $\frac{1}{4} \times ₦24,000 = ₦6,000$.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: (-18) ÷ (-3)
A) +6 (correct)
B) -6
C) +15
D) -21
Feedback: Dividing like negative signs yields positive: $(-) \div (-) = (+)$, so $(-18) \div (-3) = +6$.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: Evaluate: -12 + (-8) - (-15)
A) -5 (correct)
B) -35
C) +5
D) -11
Feedback: $-12 - 8 = -20$. Then $-20 - (-15) = -20 + 15 = -5$.

### Q15 [true-false]
Statement: A fraction whose denominator has a prime factor of 3 in its reduced form will always produce a recurring decimal.
Answer: true
Feedback: In base-10, any irreducible fraction containing prime factors other than 2 or 5 in its denominator yields a recurring decimal.

### Q16 [true-false]
Statement: On the integer number line, -15 is greater in value than -4 because 15 is larger than 4.
Answer: false
Feedback: Numbers further to the left have smaller values; $-4$ is to the right of $-15$, so $-4 > -15$.

### Q17 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO numbers that are prime numbers:
Correct: 17, 31
Incorrect: 27, 49
Feedback: 17 and 31 are prime; $27 = 3^3$ and $49 = 7^2$ are composite numbers.

### Q18 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO operations that yield a negative integer result:
Correct: (-4) × (+6), (+15) ÷ (-3)
Incorrect: (-5) × (-5), (-20) ÷ (-4)
Feedback: Unlike signs yield negative results: $(-4) \times (+6) = -24$ and $(+15) \div (-3) = -5$. Like signs yield positive ($+25$ and $+5$).

### Q19 [matching]
Stem: Match each percentage with its equivalent reduced fraction:
Pairs:
- 40% -> 2/5
- 60% -> 3/5
- 80% -> 4/5
Feedback: $40\% = \frac{2}{5}$; $60\% = \frac{3}{5}$; $80\% = \frac{4}{5}$.

### Q20 [matching]
Stem: Match each directed number problem with its correct evaluated answer:
Pairs:
- -6 + 10 -> 4
- -6 - 4 -> -10
- -6 × (-4) -> 24
Feedback: $-6 + 10 = 4$; $-6 - 4 = -10$; $(-6) \times (-4) = 24$.
