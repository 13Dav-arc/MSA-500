## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the straight line connecting the centre of a circle to any point on its circumference called?
A) Radius (correct)[cite: 8]
B) Diameter
C) Chord
D) Arc
Feedback: The radius is the straight line joining the centre of a circle to any point on its outer boundary (circumference)[cite: 8].

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the sum of the interior angles of any triangle?
A) $180^\circ$ (correct)[cite: 9]
B) $90^\circ$
C) $360^\circ$
D) $270^\circ$
Feedback: The angle-sum property of a triangle states that the interior angles always add up to $180^\circ$[cite: 9].

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: How many lines of symmetry does a regular rectangle have?
A) 2 (correct)[cite: 12]
B) 4
C) 1
D) 0
Feedback: A rectangle has 2 lines of symmetry passing through the midpoints of its opposite sides[cite: 12].

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the name of a polygon with six sides?
A) Hexagon (correct)[cite: 15]
B) Pentagon
C) Heptagon
D) Octagon
Feedback: A polygon with 6 straight sides is called a hexagon[cite: 15].

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the circumference of a circle whose radius is $7\text{ cm}$? (Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$)
A) $44\text{ cm}$ (correct)[cite: 18]
B) $22\text{ cm}$
C) $88\text{ cm}$
D) $154\text{ cm}$
Feedback: $C = 2\pi r = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 = 44\text{ cm}$[cite: 17, 18].

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: Find the perimeter of an equilateral triangle with each side measuring $5\text{ cm}$:
A) $15\text{ cm}$ (correct)[cite: 18, 19]
B) $10\text{ cm}$
C) $20\text{ cm}$
D) $25\text{ cm}$
Feedback: An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides: $3 \times 5\text{ cm} = 15\text{ cm}$[cite: 18, 19].

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: A rectangular lawn has a length of $10\text{ m}$ and a width of $6\text{ m}$. What is its perimeter?
A) $32\text{ m}$ (correct)[cite: 24]
B) $26\text{ m}$
C) $60\text{ m}$
D) $16\text{ m}$
Feedback: $P = 2(l + b) = 2(10 + 6) = 2(16) = 32\text{ m}$[cite: 21, 24].

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: The perimeter of a parallelogram is $48\text{ cm}$. If one side is $15\text{ cm}$, what is the length of the adjacent side?
A) $9\text{ cm}$ (correct)[cite: 23]
B) $12\text{ cm}$
C) $18\text{ cm}$
D) $33\text{ cm}$
Feedback: $P = 2(x + y) \implies 48 = 2(15 + y) \implies 24 = 15 + y \implies y = 9\text{ cm}$[cite: 23].

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: Calculate the area of a circle with a radius of $7\text{ cm}$ (Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$):
A) $154\text{ cm}^2$ (correct)[cite: 35, 37]
B) $44\text{ cm}^2$
C) $49\text{ cm}^2$
D) $308\text{ cm}^2$
Feedback: $\text{Area} = \pi r^2 = \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 7 = 154\text{ cm}^2$[cite: 29, 30].

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the area of a right-angled triangle with base $12\text{ cm}$ and perpendicular height $9\text{ cm}$?
A) $54\text{ cm}^2$ (correct)[cite: 30]
B) $108\text{ cm}^2$
C) $21\text{ cm}^2$
D) $48\text{ cm}^2$
Feedback: $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 9 = 54\text{ cm}^2$[cite: 30].

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: The area of a square playground is $100\text{ m}^2$. What is the length of one side?
A) $10\text{ m}$ (correct)[cite: 31, 32]
B) $25\text{ m}$
C) $50\text{ m}$
D) $20\text{ m}$
Feedback: For a square, $\text{length} = \sqrt{\text{Area}} = \sqrt{100} = 10\text{ m}$[cite: 31, 32].

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: Calculate the area of a trapezium with parallel bases of $8\text{ cm}$ and $12\text{ cm}$, and a perpendicular height of $5\text{ cm}$:
A) $50\text{ cm}^2$ (correct)[cite: 33]
B) $100\text{ cm}^2$
C) $40\text{ cm}^2$
D) $60\text{ cm}^2$
Feedback: $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h = \frac{1}{2}(8 + 12) \times 5 = 10 \times 5 = 50\text{ cm}^2$[cite: 33].

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: How many faces, edges, and vertices does a standard cube have?
A) 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices (correct)[cite: 42, 53]
B) 6 faces, 8 edges, 12 vertices
C) 8 faces, 12 edges, 6 vertices
D) 4 faces, 6 edges, 4 vertices
Feedback: A cube has 6 square faces, 12 edges, and 8 vertices[cite: 42, 53].

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: Calculate the volume of a cuboid measuring $12\text{ cm}$ long, $8\text{ cm}$ wide, and $6.5\text{ cm}$ high:
A) $624\text{ cm}^3$ (correct)[cite: 51]
B) $312\text{ cm}^3$
C) $192\text{ cm}^3$
D) $576\text{ cm}^3$
Feedback: $\text{Volume} = l \times b \times h = 12 \times 8 \times 6.5 = 624\text{ cm}^3$[cite: 50, 51].

### Q15 [true-false]
Statement: A general parallelogram has rotational symmetry of order 2, but has zero lines of symmetry.
Answer: true[cite: 13]
Feedback: A general parallelogram has rotational symmetry of order 2, but no line of symmetry[cite: 13].

### Q16 [true-false]
Statement: A cylinder has two circular edges and two vertices.
Answer: false[cite: 43]
Feedback: A cylinder has two circular edges, but zero vertices[cite: 43].

### Q17 [multi-select]

Correct: Rhombus[cite: 13], Kite[cite: 13]

### Q18 [multi-select]

Incorrect: Cone[cite: 41, 44], Sphere[cite: 41, 45]
