## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the straight line connecting the centre of a circle to any point on its circumference called?
A) Radius (correct)[cite: 8]
B) Diameter
C) Chord
D) Arc
Feedback: The radius is the straight line joining the centre of a circle to any point on its outer boundary[cite: 8].

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the line that divides a circle into two equal semi-circles called?
A) Diameter (correct)[cite: 8]
B) Radius
C) Chord
D) Tangent
Feedback: The diameter passes directly through the centre point and divides the circle into two equal semi-circles[cite: 8].

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: The region inside a circle bounded by two radii and an arc is known as:
A) A sector (correct)[cite: 8]
B) A segment
C) A semi-circle
D) A chord
Feedback: A sector is the region enclosed between two radii and the intercepted arc of a circle[cite: 8].

### Q14 [single-choice]

### Q15 [single-choice]

### Q16 [single-choice]

### Q17 [single-choice]

### Q20 [single-choice]
