## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which statement best defines environmental sanitation in basic science?
A) The active maintenance of clean, hygienic surroundings and safe waste disposal to prevent disease (correct)
B) The total preservation of natural forests without human agricultural intervention
C) The biological study of plant taxonomy and animal cell reproduction
D) The chemical synthesis of synthetic fertilizers from crude petroleum derivatives
Feedback: Environmental sanitation encompasses waste management, sewage isolation, water protection, and general hygiene to stop disease transmission.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following represents the three core pillars of environmental sanitation?
A) Safe waste disposal, water source protection, and clean shared living spaces (correct)
B) Heavy industrial manufacturing, road construction, and chemical weed extermination
C) Intensive daily athletic drills, vocal training, and visual art production
D) Mineral prospecting, commercial fishing, and offshore petroleum drilling
Feedback: The three interconnected pillars are safe disposal of refuse/sewage, safeguarding water bodies, and maintaining sanitary community spaces.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: Which dangerous waterborne bacterial disease causes severe watery diarrhea and rapid dehydration when domestic water is contaminated with sewage?
A) Cholera (correct)
B) Scurvy
C) Kwashiorkor
D) Night blindness
Feedback: Vibrio cholerae spreads through sewage-contaminated water and food, leading to profuse watery diarrhea and lethal dehydration if untreated.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: Female Anopheles mosquitoes require which environmental condition to deposit eggs and rear larvae?
A) Stagnant, unmoving bodies of water such as blocked drainage ditches (correct)
B) Dry, fast-flowing mountain rapids with rocky gravel beds
C) High-temperature desert sand dunes devoid of vegetation
D) Completely enclosed, dark household storage trunks
Feedback: Mosquitoes breed in standing, still water; clearing silt from compound gutters eliminates their necessary reproductive habitat.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: When siting a new pit latrine in a residential compound, what is the most critical scientific safety guideline?
A) It must be sited at a safe distance away and down-gradient from any water well or borehole (correct)
B) It must be positioned directly beside the main kitchen food prep table
C) It must share an immediate wall with the subterranean wellhead casing
D) It must be elevated directly above the community rainwater catchment roof
Feedback: Latrines must be separated by a safe distance down-slope from wells to prevent fecal microbes from percolating into the drinking aquifer.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: What hazardous liquid is produced when rainwater percolates through an open, decomposing refuse dumpsite?
A) Toxic leachate containing dissolved chemicals and pathogenic microbes (correct)
B) Purified potable spring water enriched with essential minerals
C) Concentrated hydrocarbon fuel suitable for domestic cooking stoves
D) Crystalline calcium carbonate solution that purifies topsoil
Feedback: Rainwater dissolving decomposing refuse generates toxic leachate, which seeps into soil layers and contaminates adjacent groundwater aquifers.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: Why is visual clarity alone an unreliable indicator of water safety for drinking?
A) Many lethal pathogens, bacteria, and dissolved toxins are microscopic and transparent (correct)
B) Pure water always possesses a deep blue or vibrant green coloration
C) Bacteria can only survive in dark brown water containing visible organic silt
D) Clear water lacks the dietary carbohydrates necessary for human metabolism
Feedback: Microorganisms such as Salmonella typhi and Vibrio cholerae are invisible without magnification; transparent water can still carry deadly pathogen loads.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the primary purpose of storing treated drinking water in containers fitted with tight lids?
A) To physically prevent airborne dust, insects, and contaminated hands from entering (correct)
B) To stop the water from evaporating into vapor during cool morning hours
C) To allow anaerobic bacteria to convert dissolved minerals into glucose
D) To prevent the plastic drum from collapsing under ambient atmospheric pressure
Feedback: Tightly sealed lids shield the reserve from external vectors like houseflies, floating dust particles, and direct hand contact.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: How should domestic drinking water be drawn from a wide storage drum to prevent re-contamination?
A) Using a dedicated long-handled cup or clean ladle without immersing fingers (correct)
B) Dipping bare hands and unwashed drinking tumblers into the drum
C) Tilting the drum until water spills across the unwashed compound floor
D) Siphoning the water through an untreated, dirty garden hose
Feedback: A dedicated long-handled ladle enables drawing water while keeping fingers and exterior contaminants outside the water body.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: Which simple household procedure is most universally reliable for destroying biological pathogens in raw drinking water?
A) Bringing the water to a rolling boil for several minutes (correct)
B) Leaving the water uncovered under direct midday sunlight for 10 minutes
C) Straining the water through a porous cotton handkerchief
D) Stirring the water vigorously with a clean wooden spoon
Feedback: Vigorous boiling raises water to 100°C, denaturing proteins and effectively killing pathogenic bacteria, viruses, and parasites.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: How do unsanitary classroom conditions directly affect academic achievement in secondary schools?
A) They cause gastrointestinal and vector-borne illnesses that drive up student absenteeism (correct)
B) They cause examination question papers to dissolve chemically
C) They prevent students from understanding mathematical formulas in textbooks
D) They change the chemical composition of ballpoint pen ink
Feedback: Bad hygiene leads to infections like typhoid, diarrhea, and malaria, causing missed class days and poor academic outcomes.

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the most effective container for accumulating household solid refuse before municipal collection?
A) A durable, covered dustbin that excludes vermin and prevents rainwater entry (correct)
B) An open, shallow wicker basket left outside under the eaves
C) A loose heap scattered across the shared compound walkway
D) An unlined earthen ditch dug immediately beside the kitchen window
Feedback: Covered bins stop disease vectors such as flies and rodents from contacting waste and prevent rain from washing trash into storm drains.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: Leaving solid refuse in open, unmanaged heaps in the community leads to all the following hazards EXCEPT:
A) Rapid natural purification of regional groundwater reserves (correct)
B) Proliferation of rodents, houseflies, and biting insects
C) Blockage of drainage channels resulting in severe street flooding
D) Release of pungent, offensive odours that pollute local air
Feedback: Open dumping severely pollutes soil and water; it does not purify groundwater.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: When compound drainage ditches become choked with silt and plastic debris, what is the immediate ecological result?
A) Water flow stops, creating stagnant pools ideal for mosquito reproduction (correct)
B) The water accelerates and spontaneously purifies itself of bacteria
C) Earthworms convert the plastic waste into organic agricultural manure
D) The temperature of the compound drops significantly below freezing
Feedback: Silt blockages prevent drainage, forming the still, warm water pools required by mosquito larvae to mature.

### Q15 [single-choice]
Stem: In biological science, "nutrition" is formally described as:
A) The biochemical process of acquiring and assimilating nutrients for energy, growth, and tissue repair (correct)
B) The physical removal of metabolic excretory products from the renal system
C) The physiological regulation of body temperature via perspiration
D) The transmission of nerve impulses between the central nervous system and muscles
Feedback: Nutrition is the uptake, digestion, and assimilation of dietary nutrients necessary for metabolic maintenance, growth, and repair.

### Q16 [single-choice]
Stem: A balanced diet must supply:
A) All six classes of food in appropriate proportions corresponding to metabolic requirements (correct)
B) A single food class in massive quantities to ensure maximum stomach distension
C) Exclusively animal proteins and lipids to accelerate muscular development
D) Equal gram weights of every known food item regardless of caloric density
Feedback: A balanced diet incorporates carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, and water in balanced ratios to meet metabolic needs.

### Q17 [single-choice]
Stem: During cellular respiration, human tissue cells oxidize glucose obtained primarily from:
A) Dietary carbohydrates (correct)
B) Mineral salts
C) Dietary water
D) Vitamin supplements
Feedback: Carbohydrates are enzymatically converted into glucose, the primary substrate oxidized during cellular respiration to synthesize ATP.

### Q18 [single-choice]
Stem: Which Nigerian agricultural staple is characterized by a high concentration of complex dietary carbohydrates?
A) White yam tubers (correct)
B) Boneless beef fillet
C) Pure palm kernel oil
D) Fresh garden egg fruit
Feedback: Yam tubers are nutrient-dense root crops loaded with complex starches that provide sustained carbohydrate energy.

### Q19 [single-choice]
Stem: What fundamental role do dietary proteins play inside the human body?
A) Providing amino acid building blocks for cell synthesis, muscle building, and tissue repair (correct)
B) Acting as the universal fluid solvent for blood plasma circulation
C) Insulating subcutaneous tissue against environmental temperature fluctuations
D) Serving as the primary quick-release fuel for emergency anaerobic bursts
Feedback: Proteins are broken down into amino acids, which cells reassemble to synthesize cellular membranes, enzymes, hormones, and muscle tissue.

### Q20 [single-choice]
Stem: Which local Nigerian legume serves as an exceptional, cost-effective source of vegetable protein?
A) Cowpeas (beans) (correct)
B) Cassava tubers
C) Sweet potato roots
D) Ripe plantain fingers
Feedback: Cowpeas (Vigna unguiculata) provide high concentrations of vegetable protein, making them an accessible staple for healthy growth.

### Q21 [single-choice]
Stem: Gram for gram, dietary fats and oils provide approximately how much caloric energy compared to carbohydrates?
A) Roughly twice the energy per gram (correct)
B) Exactly half the energy per gram
C) The identical amount of energy per gram
D) Zero energy per gram
Feedback: Lipids yield approximately 9 kcal/gram, whereas carbohydrates and proteins yield roughly 4 kcal/gram, making fats twice as energy-dense.

### Q22 [single-choice]
Stem: Why is unadulterated Nigerian red palm oil nutritionally valuable beyond its fat content?
A) It is exceptionally rich in beta-carotene, a biochemical precursor to Vitamin A (correct)
B) It contains high levels of soluble animal protein fibers
C) It replaces the biological necessity for drinking water
D) It acts as an oral antibiotic that cures systemic bacterial infections
Feedback: Red palm oil gets its bright color from carotenoids, particularly beta-carotene, which the human body converts into active Vitamin A.

### Q23 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the primary biological characteristic of dietary vitamins?
A) Organic micronutrients required in tiny amounts to regulate metabolic enzymes and maintain health (correct)
B) Inorganic mineral rocks that provide rigid structural density to skeleton bones
C) High-calorie macronutrients consumed in kilogram quantities for mechanical power
D) Indigestible plant fibers that solely provide bowel movement bulk
Feedback: Vitamins are organic compounds required in minute quantities that function as coenzymes and regulators of vital biochemical pathways.

### Q24 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following lists contains ONLY fat-soluble vitamins that require dietary lipids for efficient absorption?
A) Vitamins A, D, E, and K (correct)
B) Vitamins B1, B2, B6, and B12
C) Vitamin C, Folic acid, and Iron
D) Calcium, Phosphorus, and Vitamin C
Feedback: Vitamins A, D, E, and K dissolve in lipids and are absorbed alongside fats; Vitamin C and the B-complex group are water-soluble.

### Q25 [single-choice]
Stem: Dietary iron is a critical physiological mineral because it forms the prosthetic core of:
A) Hemoglobin, the oxygen-carrying protein in red blood cells (correct)
B) Insulin, the hormone regulating blood glucose concentration
C) Keratin, the structural protein in human hair and fingernails
D) Salivary amylase, the enzyme that hydrolyzes dietary starch
Feedback: Iron is the central functional atom in the heme group of hemoglobin, which binds molecular oxygen in lungs and delivers it to cells.

### Q26 [single-choice]
Stem: Which mineral pair is indispensable for establishing high compressive strength and bone density in the human skeleton?
A) Calcium and Phosphorus (correct)
B) Sodium and Chlorine
C) Potassium and Iodine
D) Iron and Zinc
Feedback: Calcium and phosphorus form hydroxyapatite crystals, the mineralized structural matrix that gives bones and teeth their strength.

### Q27 [single-choice]
Stem: What severe clinical condition results from an insufficient dietary intake of iodine?
A) Endemic Goitre (enlargement of the thyroid gland) (correct)
B) Scurvy (fragile capillary walls and loose teeth)
C) Kwashiorkor (edema and hair depigmentation)
D) Anemia (diminished red blood cell hemoglobin)
Feedback: The thyroid gland requires iodine to synthesize thyroxine; deficiency causes the gland to hypertrophy into a swelling known as goitre.

### Q28 [single-choice]
Stem: Human blood plasma is composed of approximately what percentage of water?
A) 90% to 92% (correct)
B) 20% to 25%
C) 50% to 55%
D) 10% to 15%
Feedback: Blood plasma is over 90% water, serving as the fluid medium that transports blood cells, dissolved nutrients, hormones, and wastes.

### Q29 [single-choice]
Stem: A toddler whose diet consists almost entirely of cassava starch without protein develops edema, a swollen belly, and peeling skin. This diagnosis is:
A) Kwashiorkor (correct)
B) Marasmus
C) Night blindness
D) Rickets
Feedback: Kwashiorkor is characterized by hypoproteinemia, resulting in fluid retention in tissues (edema), skin lesions, and growth arrest.

### Q30 [single-choice]
Stem: How does Marasmus clinically distinguish itself from Kwashiorkor in pediatric patients?
A) Marasmus manifests as severe total emaciation (skin and bones) due to combined calorie and protein starvation (correct)
B) Marasmus causes massive fluid retention and a swollen abdomen
C) Marasmus affects only hair pigmentation without causing body weight loss
D) Marasmus is caused by an excess of animal saturated fats
Feedback: Marasmus results from comprehensive starvation of both energy and protein, causing gross wasting of fat and muscle tissue without edema.

### Q31 [single-choice]
Stem: What biochemical failure in the body causes the loose teeth, bleeding gums, and opening wounds seen in Scurvy?
A) Impaired synthesis of collagen connective tissue due to lack of Vitamin C (correct)
B) Inability to oxidize glucose during cellular respiration
C) Rapid demineralization of bone calcium into the urinary tract
D) Total failure of red blood cells to divide inside bone marrow
Feedback: Ascorbic acid (Vitamin C) is an obligatory cofactor for prolyl hydroxylase in collagen synthesis; deficiency causes connective tissue collapse.

### Q32 [single-choice]
Stem: Softening, bowing of the weight-bearing leg bones, and cranial bossing in young children are diagnostic signs of:
A) Rickets, caused by deficiency of Vitamin D or calcium (correct)
B) Scurvy, caused by deficiency of Vitamin C
C) Goitre, caused by deficiency of dietary iodine
D) Anemia, caused by deficiency of dietary iron
Feedback: Rickets is defective skeletal bone mineralization in growing children caused by lack of Vitamin D (or calcium), causing bones to bend under weight.

### Q33 [single-choice]
Stem: Why does a prolonged deficiency of Vitamin A lead directly to night blindness (nyctalopia)?
A) The retina cannot synthesize rhodopsin, the pigment essential for low-light photoreception (correct)
B) The optic nerve loses its myelin sheath and stops conducting electrical impulses
C) The lens of the eye becomes clouded with insoluble calcium crystals
D) The eye muscles lose muscle tone and cannot focus incoming light rays
Feedback: Vitamin A (retinal) combines with opsin to form rhodopsin in rod cells; without Vitamin A, night vision fails.

### Q34 [single-choice]
Stem: Which affordable, traditional Nigerian food item can be introduced into meals to effectively combat nutritional iron-deficiency anemia?
A) Ground dried crayfish and dark green leafy vegetables (ugwu) (correct)
B) Refined cassava flour pap without additions
C) Bleached white granulated sugar cubes
D) Bottled carbonated sweet beverages
Feedback: Crayfish and green leafy vegetables (ugwu) are packed with bioavailable iron, facilitating hemoglobin synthesis.

### Q35 [single-choice]
Stem: Which daily meal combination represents the most nutritionally balanced plate using affordable Nigerian foods?
A) Steamed cowpea beans, boiled yam, vegetable ugwu stew with crayfish, and clean drinking water (correct)
B) A large bowl of white garri soaked in cold water with sugar and no other food
C) Fried yam slices dipped in refined vegetable shortening without soup or vegetables
D) Instant wheat noodles prepared with chili powder alone
Feedback: Beans (protein), yam (carbohydrate), ugwu (vitamins/minerals), crayfish (protein/minerals), and water provide all six essential nutrient classes.

### Q36 [true-false]
Statement: Pathogenic bacteria such as Vibrio cholerae are large enough to be easily seen with the naked eye floating in clear drinking water.
Answer: false
Feedback: Bacterial pathogens are microscopic single-celled organisms measuring a few micrometres; they are completely invisible to unaided human sight.

### Q37 [true-false]
Statement: Because Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) is water-soluble, any excess consumed is readily excreted in urine rather than stored in body tissues.
Answer: true
Feedback: Water-soluble vitamins cannot be stored in adipose tissue reserves and must be supplied continually through the regular diet.

### Q38 [true-false]
Statement: Experiencing physical fullness (satiety) after eating a massive portion of starchy cassava proves that all essential nutrient classes have been absorbed.
Answer: false
Feedback: Satiety is triggered by stomach distension from bulky starches; it does not ensure adequate intake of proteins, vitamins, or essential minerals.

### Q39 [true-false]
Statement: Siting a pit latrine on ground uphill from and 2 metres away from a shallow domestic well poses no risk of pathogen contamination to the well.
Answer: false
Feedback: Siting latrines uphill and near water sources allows fecal microbes to leach rapidly through soil and contaminate drinking groundwater.

### Q40 [true-false]
Statement: Unrefined Nigerian red palm oil contains beta-carotene, which the human liver can convert into active Vitamin A.
Answer: true
Feedback: Unbleached red palm oil is among the richest natural sources of provitamin A carotenoids, supporting vision and immune health.

### Q41 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO serious infectious diseases that are directly transmitted through unsanitary waste handling and contaminated drinking water:
Correct: Cholera, Typhoid fever
Incorrect: Sickle cell anemia, Hypertension
Feedback: Cholera and typhoid fever are caused by bacteria spread through fecally contaminated water; sickle cell is genetic and hypertension is cardiovascular.

### Q42 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO core public health methods recognized in basic environmental sanitation:
Correct: Safe sewage disposal, Proper waste (refuse) disposal
Incorrect: Dumping untreated plastic garbage into city storm drains, Constructing open latrines adjacent to drinking boreholes
Feedback: The established core sanitation methods are proper refuse disposal, safe sewage disposal, clean shared spaces, and safe water handling.

### Q43 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO nutrient classes that primarily provide caloric metabolic energy to power physical work and cellular functions:
Correct: Carbohydrates, Fats and oils
Incorrect: Mineral salts, Dietary water
Feedback: Carbohydrates and lipids yield caloric energy during cellular metabolism; minerals and water are non-caloric regulators.

### Q44 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO accessible Nigerian food staples that provide high concentrations of dietary protein:
Correct: Cowpea beans, Dried ground crayfish
Incorrect: Cassava fufu, Polished white rice
Feedback: Cowpea beans and crayfish are rich in amino acids for tissue synthesis; fufu and polished rice are predominantly carbohydrates.

### Q45 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO hallmark clinical symptoms exhibited by young children suffering from Kwashiorkor:
Correct: Fluid-filled abdominal swelling (edema), Thinning discolored reddish-brown hair
Incorrect: Rapid enlargement of skeletal arm bones, Hyperactive athletic vigor
Feedback: Kwashiorkor causes systemic fluid retention (edema in the belly and legs) and melanin loss resulting in characteristic reddish hair.

### Q48 [matching]

- Iron -> Synthesizing hemoglobin for oxygen transport in the bloodstream
- Vitamin A -> Maintaining retinal photopigments for low-light vision
- Iodine -> Supporting thyroid hormone synthesis and metabolic regulation
Feedback: Iron is the core of hemoglobin, Vitamin A supports retinal rods for night vision, and iodine enables thyroxine production.

### Q49 [matching]
Stem: Match each clinical deficiency condition with its primary nutritional cause:
Pairs:
- Kwashiorkor -> Severe dietary protein deficiency
- Scurvy -> Prolonged lack of Vitamin C (ascorbic acid)
- Rickets -> Insufficient Vitamin D and calcium during bone growth
Feedback: Kwashiorkor is missing protein, scurvy is missing Vitamin C, and rickets is missing Vitamin D or calcium needed for calcification.

### Q50 [matching]
Stem: Match each environmental hazard with its correct scientific remedy:
Pairs:
- Stagnant water in school gutters -> Clear sediment blockage to restore continuous water flow
- Dipping bare hands into drinking drums -> Use a dedicated long-handled ladle to draw water

Feedback: Unblocking gutters stops mosquito breeding, ladles prevent fecal re-contamination of water, and proper siting shields aquifers.
