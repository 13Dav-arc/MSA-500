## Quiz
### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: In the memory word MR NIGERCLAD used to remember the ten signs of life, what does the letter 'C' stand for?
A) Competition among living things for limited resources (correct)
B) Condensation of water vapour into rain clouds
C) Circulation of blood through arteries and veins
D) Combustion of dry firewood during home cooking
Feedback: 'C' stands for Competition, which is the struggle between living organisms for essential resources like food, water, sunlight, and living space.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Which biological kingdom contains microscopic, single-celled organisms that do not have a true cell nucleus, such as bacteria?
A) Kingdom Monera (correct)
B) Kingdom Fungi
C) Kingdom Plantae
D) Kingdom Animalia
Feedback: Kingdom Monera is made up of simple, single-celled organisms (prokaryotes) that lack a membrane-bound nucleus.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: Why can green plants manufacture their own food through photosynthesis while animals cannot?
A) Plants have chloroplasts containing chlorophyll to trap sunlight (correct)
B) Plants absorb ready-made cooked food through their roots
C) Plants have soft cell membranes instead of tough cell walls
D) Plants breathe using lungs and gills
Feedback: Green plants contain chloroplasts packed with chlorophyll, which absorbs sunlight to make food from water and carbon dioxide.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: When a soaked bean seed is peeled, it easily separates into two equal halves. This shows that the bean plant is a:
A) Dicotyledon (correct)
B) Monocotyledon
C) Non-flowering plant
D) Spore-bearing fern
Feedback: Dicotyledonous plants (dicots) have seeds containing two cotyledons (seed-leaves) that easily split into two halves.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: Which pair of features is typically found on a monocotyledonous plant such as maize or guinea corn?
A) Parallel leaf veins and a fibrous root system (correct)
B) Net-like leaf veins and a deep central taproot
C) Tough woody bark and reproductive spores
D) Broad leaves with branching veins and seed halves
Feedback: Monocots have narrow leaves with parallel venation and a cluster of fibrous roots branching from the stem base.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the main structural difference between a vertebrate animal and an invertebrate animal?
A) Vertebrates have an internal backbone, while invertebrates do not (correct)
B) Vertebrates make their own food, while invertebrates eat plants
C) Vertebrates live only on land, while invertebrates live only in rivers
D) Vertebrates have soft shells, while invertebrates have warm blood
Feedback: Vertebrates are animals with an internal bony skeleton and backbone; invertebrates do not possess a backbone.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: Even though a fruit bat can fly through the air with wings, it is classified as a mammal because:
A) It has fur, gives birth to live young, and nurses them with milk (correct)
B) It lays hard-shelled eggs in high tree branches
C) It breathes underwater through moist gill slits
D) It has a toothless beak and hollow feathers
Feedback: Bats are true mammals because they have hair or fur, give birth to live babies, and possess mammary glands to produce milk.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: In which state of matter are particles packed tightly in neat rows, vibrating only in their fixed spots?
A) Solid (correct)
B) Liquid
C) Gas
D) Vapour
Feedback: In solids, strong attractive forces keep particles locked in regular patterns where they can only vibrate in fixed positions.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: Why can air inside a bicycle pump or syringe be easily squeezed (compressed) into a smaller space?
A) Gas particles are separated by large empty spaces (correct)
B) Gas particles melt into a liquid when pushed
C) Gas particles have no mass and no weight
D) Gas particles stop moving when pressure is applied
Feedback: Gases compress easily because their particles are widely scattered with large empty spaces between them.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: When solid camphor mothballs placed inside a clothes cupboard slowly disappear without leaving any liquid stain, what process has occurred?
A) Sublimation (correct)
B) Evaporation
C) Freezing
D) Condensation
Feedback: Sublimation is the direct change of state where a solid turns into a gas without melting into a liquid first.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: Why are electrical wires made of copper metal covered on the outside with rubber or plastic?
A) Copper is a good conductor of electricity, while rubber is an insulator that prevents shock (correct)
B) Copper melts at room temperature, while rubber keeps it cool
C) Rubber conducts electricity faster, while copper blocks the current
D) Rubber is a heavy metal, while copper is a light non-metal
Feedback: Copper conducts electricity easily, while rubber and plastic are non-metal insulators that protect users from electric shocks.

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: In science, the ability or capacity of a body to do work is called energy, and it is measured in:
A) Joules (J) (correct)
B) Newtons (N)
C) Kilograms (kg)
D) Litres (L)
Feedback: Both energy and work done are measured using the standard scientific unit called the Joule (J).

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: A ripe mango resting high up on a tree branch has stored energy because of its height above the ground. This stored energy is called:
A) Gravitational potential energy (correct)
B) Sound energy
C) Kinetic energy
D) Light energy
Feedback: Potential energy is stored energy possessed by an object due to its position, height, or stretched state.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following is a major advantage of using solar energy from the Sun instead of a petrol generator?
A) Solar energy is renewable and does not pollute the air with toxic smoke (correct)
B) Solar panels work only during heavy rainstorms at night
C) Solar panels burn large amounts of firewood and coal
D) Solar energy runs out completely after two weeks
Feedback: Solar energy is clean and renewable; it never runs out and does not release dangerous smoke or greenhouse gases.

### Q15 [true-false]
Statement: Non-living things like rocks and motor cars carry out biological respiration, nutrition, and cellular excretion.
Answer: false
Feedback: Only living organisms carry out biological life processes; non-living things do not respire, feed, excrete cellular waste, or reproduce.

### Q16 [true-false]
Statement: Tilapia fish and garden lizards are cold-blooded vertebrates whose body temperatures change with the temperature of their surroundings.
Answer: true
Feedback: Fish, amphibians, and reptiles are cold-blooded (poikilothermic) animals that rely on external surroundings to regulate their body heat.

### Q17 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO animals that belong to the invertebrate group:
Correct: Land snail, Housefly
Incorrect: Domestic goat, Catfish
Feedback: Land snails (molluscs) and houseflies (insects) lack backbones and are invertebrates; goats and catfish are vertebrates.

### Q18 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO sources of energy that are classified as renewable:
Correct: Flowing river water (hydroelectric), Sunlight (solar)
Incorrect: Coal rock, Crude oil
Feedback: Flowing water and sunlight replenish naturally and will never run out; coal and crude oil are non-renewable fossil fuels.

### Q19 [matching]
Stem: Match each scientific term with its correct definition:
Pairs:
- Adaptation -> Special features that help an organism survive in its habitat
- Sublimation -> A solid changing directly into a gas without melting
- Condensation -> A gas cooling down and changing into a liquid
Feedback: Adaptation helps living things survive, sublimation turns solid directly into gas, and condensation turns gas into liquid.

### Q20 [matching]
Stem: Match each item with its correct scientific category:
Pairs:
- Cowpea (Bean) -> Dicotyledonous flowering plant
- Tilapia -> Vertebrate class Pisces
- Crude Oil -> Non-renewable fossil fuel source
Feedback: Beans have two cotyledons (dicot), tilapia is a fish with gills and fins (Pisces), and crude oil is a non-renewable fossil fuel.
