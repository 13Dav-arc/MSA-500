## Quiz
### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following is the best scientific definition of a living thing?
A) An organism that has life and carries out fundamental life processes like feeding, breathing, and reproducing (correct)
B) Any solid material that has a fixed shape and cannot be compressed
C) An object that moves whenever an engine or mechanical motor pushes it
D) A substance that dissolves quickly in water and changes colour
Feedback: Living things possess life and perform vital biological functions such as nutrition, respiration, movement, growth, and reproduction.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: In the memory word MR NIGERCLAD used to remember the signs of life, what does the letter 'C' stand for?
A) Competition (correct)
B) Circulation
C) Combustion
D) Condensation
Feedback: 'C' stands for Competition, which is the struggle between living organisms for essential environmental resources such as food, water, sunlight, and living space.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: What biological process breaks down digested food in body cells using oxygen to release energy?
A) Respiration (correct)
B) Photosynthesis
C) Transpiration
D) Germination
Feedback: Respiration is the biochemical breakdown of food substances within cells to liberate usable energy.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: The ability of a living organism to detect changes in its surroundings and react to them is called:
A) Irritability (correct)
B) Excretion
C) Locomotion
D) Sublimation
Feedback: Irritability (or sensitivity) is the capacity to detect external environmental stimuli (like touch, light, or temperature) and respond appropriately.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: How is excretion fundamentally different from passing out solid faeces (egestion)?
A) Excretion removes chemical waste made inside body cells, while egestion removes undigested food (correct)
B) Excretion occurs only in animals, while egestion occurs only in green plants
C) Excretion takes place only at night, while egestion happens during the day
D) Excretion builds new body tissues, while egestion breaks down fats
Feedback: Excretion is the removal of metabolic cellular waste (such as urine, sweat, and carbon dioxide), whereas egestion expels undigested food remains through the anus.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: The special physical features or behaviors that help an organism survive in its natural habitat are called:
A) Adaptation (correct)
B) Reproduction
C) Photosynthesis
D) Competition
Feedback: Adaptation refers to structural or behavioral traits that enable an organism to survive and thrive in its specific environment.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: Inside which green cell structures do plants manufacture food using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide?
A) Chloroplasts (correct)
B) Cell walls
C) Sap vacuoles
D) Lenticels
Feedback: Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll, which captures sunlight to manufacture sugars during photosynthesis.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: Microscopic, single-celled organisms that do not have a defined cell nucleus, such as bacteria, belong to Kingdom:
A) Monera (correct)
B) Plantae
C) Protista
D) Fungi
Feedback: Kingdom Monera consists of unicellular prokaryotes, which are organisms that lack a membrane-bound nucleus.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: Which biological kingdom includes mushrooms, toadstools, and moulds that absorb nutrients from decaying organic matter?
A) Fungi (correct)
B) Plantae
C) Animalia
D) Monera
Feedback: Organisms in Kingdom Fungi lack chlorophyll and feed saprophytically by absorbing dissolved nutrients from dead or decaying materials.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: Through which tiny pores on their leaves do terrestrial green plants take in and release air?
A) Stomata (correct)
B) Gills
C) Nostrils
D) Spiracles
Feedback: Stomata are microscopic pores located on leaves that allow plants to exchange gases with the surrounding air.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: A flowering plant whose seed contains only one seed-leaf is known as a:
A) Monocotyledon (correct)
B) Dicotyledon
C) Gymnosperm
D) Bryophyte
Feedback: Monocotyledons (monocots) are flowering plants that produce seeds with a single cotyledon (seed-leaf).

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following Nigerian staple food crops is an example of a monocotyledon?
A) Maize (correct)
B) Cowpea (beans)
C) Groundnut
D) Soya bean
Feedback: Maize produces seeds with a single cotyledon, parallel leaf veins, and fibrous roots, making it a monocotyledon.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: Which type of root system is typical of dicotyledonous plants like beans and mango trees?
A) Taproot system (correct)
B) Fibrous root system
C) Stilt root system
D) Prop root system
Feedback: Dicotyledonous plants develop a main central taproot that grows deep down, with smaller branching roots spreading sideways.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: What type of leaf vein pattern (venation) is observed on the broad leaves of a bean plant?
A) Net-like (reticulate) venation (correct)
B) Parallel venation
C) Circular venation
D) Spiral venation
Feedback: Dicots like beans have net-like or reticulate leaf venation, where veins branch out like a network.

### Q15 [single-choice]
Stem: Animals that completely lack an internal backbone are scientifically classified as:
A) Invertebrates (correct)
B) Mammals
C) Vertebrates
D) Amphibians
Feedback: Invertebrates are organisms that do not develop an internal spinal column or bony backbone.

### Q16 [single-choice]
Stem: To which vertebrate class does a tilapia fish belong?
A) Pisces (correct)
B) Amphibia
C) Reptilia
D) Aves
Feedback: Tilapia belongs to the class Pisces (Fish), characterized by fins, scales, and breathing with gills.

### Q17 [single-choice]
Stem: An animal that has moist, scaleless skin and lays jelly-covered eggs in water is classified as an:
A) Amphibian (correct)
B) Reptile
C) Bird
D) Arthropod
Feedback: Amphibians (such as toads and frogs) have soft, moist skin without scales and depend on water bodies to lay eggs.

### Q18 [single-choice]
Stem: Why is a fruit bat classified as a mammal rather than a bird, even though it flies in the air?
A) It has fur, gives birth to live young, and nurses them with milk (correct)
B) It has a toothless beak and hollow feathers
C) It lays hard-shelled eggs in high trees
D) It breathes underwater using internal gills
Feedback: Bats are true mammals because they are covered in fur, bear live young, and possess mammary glands to produce milk.

### Q19 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following is the best scientific definition of matter?
A) Anything that has mass and occupies physical space (correct)
B) Any substance that can produce electricity when heated
C) Any object that can be seen clearly with the naked eye
D) A solid material that cannot be broken or changed
Feedback: Matter is scientifically defined as anything that possesses mass (weight) and takes up space (volume).

### Q20 [single-choice]
Stem: All matter is made up of billions of tiny, microscopic building blocks called:
A) Atoms and molecules (correct)
B) Cells and tissues
C) Stomata and lenticels
D) Chloroplasts and bones
Feedback: The particulate theory of matter states that all matter is composed of tiny fundamental particles called atoms or molecules.

### Q21 [single-choice]
Stem: Which state of matter has a definite shape, a fixed volume, and particles that only vibrate in fixed spots?
A) Solid (correct)
B) Liquid
C) Gas
D) Plasma
Feedback: In a solid, strong forces hold particles tightly in place in regular rows, giving the object a definite shape and fixed volume.

### Q22 [single-choice]
Stem: Why can a gas be easily squeezed (compressed) into a much smaller container while a solid cannot?
A) Gas particles are separated by large empty spaces (correct)
B) Gas particles melt into a liquid when pushed
C) Gas particles have no mass and no weight
D) Gas particles stop moving when pressure is applied
Feedback: Gas particles are separated by wide empty spaces, which allows them to be pushed closer together when pressure is applied.

### Q23 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the direct transformation of a solid into a gas without melting into a liquid called?
A) Sublimation (correct)
B) Evaporation
C) Condensation
D) Melting
Feedback: Sublimation occurs when a solid turns directly into vapour without melting into a liquid state first (e.g., camphor).

### Q24 [single-choice]
Stem: Steam rising from a boiling pot cools down on the underside of a metal lid and turns back into liquid water. This change of state is called:
A) Condensation (correct)
B) Freezing
C) Boiling
D) Sublimation
Feedback: Condensation is the physical phase change where a gas cools down and turns back into a liquid.

### Q25 [single-choice]
Stem: What property allows metals like copper and aluminium to be hammered into thin flat sheets without breaking?
A) Malleability (correct)
B) Ductility
C) Brittleness
D) Sublimation
Feedback: Malleability is the physical property of a metal that allows it to be beaten or hammered into thin sheets.

### Q26 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following is a non-metal commonly used as an electrical insulator to cover flexible wires?
A) Rubber (correct)
B) Copper
C) Iron
D) Aluminium
Feedback: Rubber and plastics are non-metals and poor conductors of electricity, making them excellent insulators to wrap around wires.

### Q27 [single-choice]
Stem: In science, the ability or capacity of a body to do work is called energy, and its standard scientific unit of measurement is the:
A) Joule (J) (correct)
B) Newton (N)
C) Kilogram (kg)
D) Metre (m)
Feedback: Both energy and work done are measured using the standard scientific unit called the Joule (J).

### Q28 [single-choice]
Stem: What form of mechanical energy is possessed by an object because of its physical motion, such as a running cheetah or rolling ball?
A) Kinetic energy (correct)
B) Potential energy
C) Chemical energy
D) Nuclear energy
Feedback: Kinetic energy is the energy possessed by an object because of its physical motion.

### Q29 [single-choice]
Stem: What form of energy is stored inside food substances, dry-cell torch batteries, and petrol?
A) Chemical energy (correct)
B) Light energy
C) Sound energy
D) Mechanical energy
Feedback: Chemical energy is stored in the molecular bonds of food, batteries, and fuels, and is released during chemical reactions.

### Q30 [single-choice]
Stem: Which major hydroelectric dam in Nigeria produces electricity using the power of flowing river water?
A) Kainji Dam (correct)
B) Aso Rock Dam
C) Niger Bridge Dam
D) Zuma Dam
Feedback: Kainji Dam (along with Shiroro Dam) uses flowing river water to turn huge turbines that generate renewable electricity in Nigeria.

### Q31 [single-choice]
Stem: Energy obtained from natural sources that are constantly replenished and can never run out is called:
A) Renewable energy (correct)
B) Non-renewable energy
C) Exhaustible energy
D) Synthetic energy
Feedback: Renewable energy sources, such as sunlight, wind, and flowing water, replenish naturally and will never run out.

### Q32 [single-choice]
Stem: The Law of Conservation of Energy states that:
A) Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, but can only change from one form to another (correct)
B) Energy can easily be destroyed whenever a machine is switched off
C) Energy is only found inside hot objects and cannot exist in cold objects
D) Energy increases in quantity every time an engine moves faster
Feedback: The Law of Conservation of Energy states that the total amount of energy remains constant; it cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed.

### Q33 [single-choice]
Stem: A brick with a mass of $2\text{ kg}$ is lifted to a height of $4\text{ metres}$. If gravity $g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$, what is its potential energy ($PE = mgh$)?
A) $80\text{ Joules}$ (correct)
B) $20\text{ Joules}$
C) $40\text{ Joules}$
D) $16\text{ Joules}$
Feedback: $PE = m \times g \times h = 2\text{ kg} \times 10\text{ m/s}^2 \times 4\text{ m} = 80\text{ Joules (J)}$.

### Q34 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the correct chemical symbol for the element Potassium?
A) K (correct)
B) P
C) Po
D) Pt
Feedback: The chemical symbol for potassium is $\text{K}$, which comes from its Latin name *Kalium*.

### Q35 [single-choice]
Stem: In a filtration experiment, what is the clear liquid that passes through the filter paper called?
A) Filtrate (correct)
B) Residue
C) Distillate
D) Solute
Feedback: The clear liquid that successfully passes through the pores of the filter paper into the container below is called the filtrate.

### Q36 [true-false]
Statement: Animal cells possess a tough outer cell wall made of cellulose just like plant cells.
Answer: false
Feedback: Animal cells do not have a cellulose cell wall; they are enclosed only by a soft, flexible cell membrane.

### Q37 [true-false]
Statement: The Law of Conservation of Energy states that energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed from one form to another.
Answer: true
Feedback: Energy cannot be created from nothing or destroyed into nothing; it only changes form.

### Q38 [true-false]
Statement: Air has mass and occupies space, which means it is a form of matter.
Answer: true
Feedback: Air is a mixture of real gases; it possesses mass and occupies space, satisfying the scientific definition of matter.

### Q39 [true-false]
Statement: Dissolving table salt in water creates a brand-new chemical compound that can never be separated.
Answer: false
Feedback: Dissolving salt in water forms a physical mixture (solution); boiling the water evaporates it away, leaving the original salt behind.

### Q40 [true-false]
Statement: Invertebrates make up over 90% of all animal species on Earth.
Answer: true
Feedback: Invertebrates (such as insects, molluscs, and worms) constitute more than 90% of all described animal species.

### Q41 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO characteristics of living things found in the MR NIGERCLAD rule:
Correct: Competition, Adaptation
Incorrect: Evaporation, Sublimation
Feedback: Competition and adaptation are biological life processes; evaporation and sublimation are physical changes of matter.

### Q42 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO structural features that are typical of dicotyledonous plants like cowpea beans:
Correct: Taproot system, Net-like (reticulate) leaf veins
Incorrect: Single cotyledon in seed, Parallel leaf veins

### Q43 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO sources of energy that are classified as renewable:
Correct: Solar energy, Wind energy
Incorrect: Crude oil, Coal
Feedback: Solar and wind energy replenish naturally and will never run out; crude oil and coal are non-renewable fossil fuels.

### Q44 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO separation techniques used to separate an insoluble solid from a liquid:
Correct: Filtration, Decantation
Incorrect: Magnetic separation, Simple distillation
Feedback: Filtration and decantation separate insoluble solids from liquids; magnetic separation requires magnetic metals, and distillation separates solutions/liquids.

### Q45 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO physical properties that are typical of metals like copper and iron:
Correct: Good conductors of electricity, Malleable (can be hammered into sheets)
Incorrect: Dull and non-reflective surface, Soft and brittle
Feedback: Metals are shiny, malleable, ductile, and excellent conductors of heat and electricity.

### Q46 [matching]

- Nutrition -> Obtaining and utilizing nutrients for energy and growth

### Q47 [matching]

### Q48 [matching]
Stem: Match each physical change of state with its correct description:
Pairs:
- Melting -> Solid changes into a liquid
- Freezing -> Liquid changes into a solid
- Sublimation -> Solid changes directly into a gas
Feedback: Melting turns solid to liquid, freezing turns liquid to solid, and sublimation turns solid directly into gas.

### Q49 [matching]
Stem: Match each chemical element with its correct chemical symbol:
Pairs:
- Sodium -> Na
- Potassium -> K
- Calcium -> Ca
Feedback: Sodium is $\text{Na}$ (Natrium), Potassium is $\text{K}$ (Kalium), and Calcium is $\text{Ca}$.

### Q50 [matching]
Stem: Match each mixture with the most suitable separation technique:
Pairs:
- Insoluble sand mixed in clean water -> Filtration
- Common salt dissolved in water -> Evaporation to dryness
