## Quiz
### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which celestial body is located at the center of our solar system, providing gravitational pull, light, and heat to all orbiting planets?
A) The Sun (correct)
B) Jupiter
C) The Moon
D) Polaris (The North Star)
Feedback: The Sun is the massive yellow dwarf star situated at the center of the solar system around which all eight planets revolve.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Which planet in the solar system is located closest to the Sun?
A) Mercury (correct)
B) Venus
C) Mars
D) Neptune
Feedback: Mercury is the first and closest planet to the Sun, followed by Venus, Earth, and Mars.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the correct chronological sequence of the first four inner (terrestrial) planets starting nearest to the Sun?
A) Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars (correct)
B) Venus, Mercury, Mars, Earth
C) Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn
D) Mercury, Earth, Venus, Mars
Feedback: The four inner rocky planets in increasing distance from the Sun are Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: Why is Venus known as the hottest planet in the solar system, even though Mercury is closer to the Sun?
A) Venus has a dense carbon dioxide atmosphere that traps intense heat through a runaway greenhouse effect (correct)
B) Venus has hundreds of active radioactive oceans on its surface
C) Venus is made entirely of burning liquefied petroleum gas
D) Venus rotates around the Sun ten times faster than Mercury
Feedback: Venus has an extremely thick atmosphere composed primarily of carbon dioxide and sulfuric acid clouds, producing an intense greenhouse effect that traps solar heat.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: What natural phenomenon on Earth is caused directly by the continuous 24-hour rotation of the Earth on its own tilted axis?
A) The alternate cycle of day and night (correct)
B) The four seasonal weather changes (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter)
C) The high and low oceanic tides caused by the Moon
D) The occurrence of volcanic eruptions in the Pacific Ocean
Feedback: The Earth rotates once on its polar axis every 24 hours, turning one half towards the Sun (daylight) and the other half away (night).

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: How long does it take the Earth to complete one full revolution in its orbit around the Sun?
A) 365¼ days (one solar year) (correct)
B) 24 hours (one day)
C) 28 days (one lunar month)
D) 100 days (one century)
Feedback: The Earth requires approximately 365¼ days (365 days and 6 hours) to complete one orbital revolution around the Sun.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: What major astronomical consequence results from the Earth's annual revolution around the Sun combined with the 23.5-degree tilt of its axis?
A) The change of seasons throughout the year (correct)
B) The regular 24-hour cycle of day and night
C) The generation of Earth's magnetic north and south poles
D) The rotation of the Moon around its own axis
Feedback: Earth's axial tilt causes different hemispheres to receive varying amounts of direct sunlight as Earth revolves around the Sun, resulting in seasonal changes.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: What is a satellite in space science and astronomy?
A) Any celestial or human-made body that orbits around a larger planet in space (correct)
B) A large fiery rock that burns upon entering the Earth's stratosphere
C) A specialized telescope installed permanently on the peak of a high mountain
D) An astronaut who has spent more than six months outside Earth's atmosphere
Feedback: A satellite is any body that orbits a larger astronomical object. The Moon is Earth's natural satellite, while communication satellites are artificial satellites.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: Which artificial satellite application enables television stations to broadcast live international sports matches and allows smartphones to use GPS navigation?
A) Geostationary telecommunication and navigation satellites (correct)
B) Space telescopes looking at distant exploding galaxies
C) Submarine sonars operating in deep Atlantic ocean trenches
D) Underground geological seismographs monitoring earthquakes
Feedback: Artificial satellites placed in Earth's orbit relay high-frequency radio and microwave signals for global telecommunications, television broadcasting, and GPS mapping.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: What is one serious physiological hazard experienced by astronauts during prolonged stays in space stations under microgravity (weightlessness)?
A) Progressive weakening of bones and loss of muscle mass (correct)
B) Sudden doubling of bodily mass and excessive blood pressure
C) Permanent hardening and calcification of leg joints
D) Immediate loss of normal hearing and speech abilities
Feedback: In prolonged microgravity, astronauts' muscles and bones do not support body weight, leading to progressive muscle atrophy and bone mineral density loss.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: Why must astronauts wear pressurized spacesuits with thermal layers and oxygen supplies whenever they venture outside a spacecraft?
A) Space is a vacuum with zero breathable air, extreme temperatures, and harmful cosmic radiation (correct)
B) Spacesuits are designed primarily to make astronauts appear uniform on camera
C) Space is filled with dense carbon monoxide gas that burns skin immediately
D) The high air pressure in outer space would crush human bones without a metal shell
Feedback: Outer space is an airless vacuum devoid of atmospheric pressure and oxygen, featuring extreme temperature swings and solar radiation; spacesuits provide life support.

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: In mechanics, how is a simple machine scientifically defined?
A) A mechanical device that makes work easier, faster, or more convenient by overcoming a load with a smaller effort (correct)
B) A complex electronic computer that runs programmed algorithmic software
C) An electrical motor that operates without consuming any energy
D) A heavy industrial vehicle used exclusively for crushing rocks
Feedback: A simple machine is a mechanical tool or device that alters the magnitude or direction of an applied effort to overcome a resistance (load), making work easier.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: In the study of simple machines, what formula correctly represents Mechanical Advantage (MA)?
A) Mechanical Advantage = Load / Effort (correct)
B) Mechanical Advantage = Effort / Load
C) Mechanical Advantage = Work Done × Time Taken
D) Mechanical Advantage = Distance moved by Effort / Distance moved by Load
Feedback: Mechanical Advantage is defined as the ratio of the resistance force overcome (Load) to the force applied (Effort): MA = Load / Effort.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: In a First-Class lever, which key component is positioned between the other two?
A) The Fulcrum (pivot) is located between the Load and the Effort (correct)
B) The Load is located between the Fulcrum and the Effort
C) The Effort is located between the Fulcrum and the Load
D) The Effort and the Load are located at the exact same point
Feedback: In a First-Class lever, the fulcrum (pivot point) lies between the applied effort and the load (e.g., crowbar, pair of scissors, seesaw).

### Q15 [single-choice]
Stem: A wheelbarrow and a bottle opener are practical everyday examples of which class of lever?
A) Second-Class lever (correct)
B) First-Class lever
C) Third-Class lever
D) Compound hydraulic lever
Feedback: In a Second-Class lever, the Load is positioned in the middle between the Fulcrum and the Effort (e.g., wheelbarrow, nutcracker, bottle opener).

### Q16 [single-choice]
Stem: When a person uses a pair of laboratory tweezers or sugar tongs to pick up a small item, which class of lever is being utilized?
A) Third-Class lever (correct)
B) First-Class lever
C) Second-Class lever
D) Zero-Class lever
Feedback: In a Third-Class lever, the applied Effort is located in the middle between the Fulcrum and the Load (e.g., tongs, tweezers, human forearm).

### Q17 [single-choice]
Stem: What type of simple machine consists of a rigid sloping surface used to raise heavy loads to a higher level using a smaller effort?
A) Inclined plane (ramp) (correct)
B) First-Class lever
C) Fixed single pulley
D) Hydraulic jack
Feedback: An inclined plane is a sloping surface (ramp, staircase) that allows a heavy load to be elevated to a height with less force than lifting it vertically.

### Q18 [single-choice]
Stem: Why is the efficiency of practical machines always less than 100%?
A) Part of the input work is always wasted in overcoming friction between moving parts and air resistance (correct)
B) The effort applied is always greater than the weight of the universe
C) Machines naturally destroy energy according to the law of gravity
D) Machine operators do not apply force in a straight line
Feedback: In real-world machines, friction between interacting surfaces and the weight of machine parts waste energy as heat and sound, keeping efficiency below 100%.

### Q19 [single-choice]
Stem: In physics, work is said to be done when:
A) A force acts on an object and causes it to move a distance in the direction of the force (correct)
B) A person stands completely still holding a heavy carton on their head for two hours
C) A student thinks deeply about a difficult mathematics examination problem
D) An electric fan is turned off and kept inside a locked room
Feedback: Work is done only when an applied force causes a displacement of the object along the direction of the force: Work = Force × Distance.

### Q20 [single-choice]
Stem: If a student exerts a horizontal force of 50 Newtons to push a classroom desk through a distance of 4 metres across the floor, how much work has been done?
A) 200 Joules (correct)
B) 12.5 Joules
C) 54 Joules
D) 46 Joules
Feedback: Work done = Force × Distance = 50 N × 4 m = 200 Joules (J).

### Q21 [single-choice]
Stem: What is power in basic science, and what is its standard SI unit?
A) Power is the rate of doing work or transferring energy, measured in Watts (W) (correct)
B) Power is the total force exerted by an object, measured in Newtons (N)
C) Power is the quantity of matter in a machine, measured in Kilograms (kg)
D) Power is the distance moved by a machine, measured in Metres (m)
Feedback: Power is defined as the work done divided by time taken (Power = Work / Time) and is measured in Watts (1 Watt = 1 Joule per second).

### Q22 [single-choice]
Stem: Which form of energy is stored in the food we eat, the dry battery cells in flashlights, and the firewood burned for cooking?
A) Chemical energy (correct)
B) Nuclear energy
C) Kinetic energy
D) Sound energy
Feedback: Chemical energy is stored in the chemical bonds of substances like food nutrients, electrochemical batteries, and combustible fuels.

### Q23 [single-choice]
Stem: What fundamental law of science states that energy cannot be created or destroyed, but can only be transformed from one form to another?
A) Law of Conservation of Energy (correct)
B) Law of Universal Gravitation
C) Law of Biological Evolution
D) Law of Planetary Motion
Feedback: The First Law of Thermodynamics (Law of Conservation of Energy) establishes that energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only converted from one form to another.

### Q24 [single-choice]
Stem: When an electric fan is plugged into a wall socket and switched on, what main energy conversion takes place?
A) Electrical energy is converted into kinetic (mechanical) energy and sound (correct)
B) Chemical energy is converted directly into gravitational potential energy
C) Light energy is converted into nuclear energy
D) Sound energy is converted into chemical potential energy
Feedback: The electric motor inside the fan draws electrical energy and converts it into rotational kinetic energy of the blades, along with minor sound and heat.

### Q25 [single-choice]
Stem: In the classification of living things, which kingdom contains single-celled eukaryotic microorganisms with defined nuclei, such as Amoeba and Paramecium?
A) Kingdom Protista (correct)
B) Kingdom Monera
C) Kingdom Fungi
D) Kingdom Plantae
Feedback: Kingdom Protista consists of mostly unicellular eukaryotic organisms with distinct nuclei and membrane-bound organelles, including Amoeba and Paramecium.

### Q26 [single-choice]
Stem: Why are mushrooms, yeast, and bread mould classified as Fungi rather than true green plants?
A) They lack chlorophyll and cannot manufacture their own food through photosynthesis (correct)
B) They have green leaves and long taproots that absorb cooked food
C) They reproduce by giving birth to live young like mammals
D) They swim in deep ocean waters using fins and scales
Feedback: Fungi lack chlorophyll and cell walls made of cellulose; they are heterotrophic decomposers that absorb organic nutrients from decaying matter.

### Q27 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following is a clear botanical difference between monocotyledonous and dicotyledonous flowering plants?
A) Monocots have one seed leaf and parallel leaf veins, while dicots have two seed leaves and net-like veins (correct)
B) Monocots produce woody timber, while dicots never produce flowers or seeds
C) Monocots have deep taproots, while dicots only have fibrous roots
D) Monocots reproduce with spores, while dicots reproduce with pine cones
Feedback: Monocots (maize, rice) possess one cotyledon and parallel leaf venation; dicots (beans, mangoes) possess two cotyledons and reticulate (net-like) venation.

### Q28 [single-choice]
Stem: Which vertebrate class consists of animals with dry scaly skin that lay shelled eggs on land and are cold-blooded, such as lizards and crocodiles?
A) Reptilia (Reptiles) (correct)
B) Amphibia (Amphibians)
C) Aves (Birds)
D) Mammalia (Mammals)
Feedback: Reptiles are ectothermic vertebrates with dry keratinous scales or scutes that lay leathery eggs on land (e.g., snakes, lizards, crocodiles, tortoises).

### Q29 [single-choice]
Stem: Which physical separation technique is most suitable for recovering pure common salt crystals from a solution of salt dissolved completely in water?
A) Evaporation to dryness (correct)
B) Filtration through filter paper
C) Magnetic separation using a bar magnet
D) Hand-picking using forceps
Feedback: Since salt is completely dissolved in water forming a homogeneous solution, simple filtration will not separate it; evaporation removes water vapor, leaving salt crystals behind.

### Q30 [single-choice]
Stem: How can a mixture of tiny iron filings and dry beach sand be separated cleanly in a school science laboratory?
A) Passing a strong magnet over the mixture to attract the magnetic iron filings (correct)
B) Boiling the dry mixture in an electric kettle
C) Pouring cold kerosene on the mixture and setting it on fire
D) Centrifuging the dry mixture at high speed in a test tube
Feedback: Iron is a ferromagnetic material attracted to magnets, while sand is non-magnetic, allowing clean separation using magnetic separation.

### Q31 [single-choice]
Stem: What separation method is used in crude oil refineries to separate petrol, kerosene, diesel, and lubricating oil based on differences in their boiling points?
A) Fractional distillation (correct)
B) Paper chromatography
C) Simple decantation
D) Sublimation
Feedback: Fractional distillation separates miscible liquids with different boiling points by repeated vaporization and condensation within a fractionating column.

### Q32 [single-choice]
Stem: A deficiency of Vitamin C in a child's diet over several months leads to bleeding gums, loose teeth, and poor wound healing, a medical condition known as:
A) Scurvy (correct)
B) Rickets
C) Kwashiorkor
D) Night blindness
Feedback: Scurvy is caused by an acute deficiency of ascorbic acid (Vitamin C), leading to impaired collagen synthesis, bleeding gums, and skin lesions.

### Q33 [single-choice]
Stem: Kwashiorkor is a severe nutritional deficiency disease in young children caused primarily by a lack of which class of nutrient in their diet?
A) Proteins (correct)
B) Carbohydrates
C) Fats and oils
D) Dietary roughage
Feedback: Kwashiorkor is a form of severe protein-energy malnutrition characterized by oedema (swollen belly and legs), thinning hair, and muscle wasting.

### Q34 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the primary purpose of constructing sanitary ventilated improved pit (VIP) latrines in rural communities?
A) To isolate human excreta, prevent the spread of waterborne pathogen diseases, and eliminate foul odors (correct)
B) To collect rainwater for drinking during dry Harmattan seasons
C) To store harvest crops like yam and cassava tubers away from sunlight
D) To generate hydroelectric power for running community grain mills
Feedback: VIP latrines safely contain fecal pathogens, preventing groundwater contamination, fly breeding, and the spread of cholera and dysentery.

### Q35 [single-choice]
Stem: In an automobile braking system, why are hydraulic liquids like brake fluid preferred over air or gases to transmit force to the brake pads?
A) Liquids are practically incompressible, transmitting applied pressure instantaneously and uniformly (correct)
B) Liquids compress very easily and reduce the weight of the car
C) Liquids evaporate into steam and push the wheels backwards
D) Liquids turn into solid ice as soon as the driver presses the pedal
Feedback: Liquids cannot be significantly compressed, so pressure applied at the brake pedal is transmitted undiminished through the hydraulic lines to the wheel pistons.

### Q36 [true-false]
Statement: The Moon produces its own visible light by nuclear fusion in the same way that the Sun does.
Answer: false
Feedback: The Moon is a non-luminous body that does not generate its own light; it shines by reflecting sunlight that falls upon its rocky surface.

### Q37 [true-false]
Statement: Earth's rotation on its axis from west to east causes the Sun, Moon, and stars to appear to rise in the east and set in the west.
Answer: true
Feedback: Because Earth rotates counter-clockwise (west to east), celestial objects appear to move across the sky from east to west.

### Q38 [true-false]
Statement: A First-Class lever always has the Load positioned between the Fulcrum and the Effort.
Answer: false
Feedback: A First-Class lever has the Fulcrum in the middle; a Second-Class lever has the Load in the middle.

### Q39 [true-false]
Statement: Energy can neither be created nor destroyed, but can only be changed from one form to another.
Answer: true
Feedback: This is the fundamental Law of Conservation of Energy, which applies across all physical and biological systems.

### Q40 [true-false]
Statement: Distillation is a separation method used to separate an insoluble solid from a liquid using porous filter paper.
Answer: false
Feedback: Separating an insoluble solid from a liquid using filter paper is called filtration; distillation separates liquids based on boiling point differences.

### Q41 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO planets in the solar system that are classified as Gas Giants:
Correct: Jupiter, Saturn
Incorrect: Mercury, Mars
Feedback: Jupiter and Saturn are giant planets composed primarily of hydrogen and helium; Mercury and Mars are small rocky terrestrial planets.

### Q42 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO everyday tools that operate as First-Class levers:
Correct: Crowbar, Pair of scissors
Incorrect: Wheelbarrow, Nutcracker

### Q43 [multi-select]

### Q44 [multi-select]
Stem: Select TWO cold-blooded (ectothermic) vertebrate classes:
Correct: Pisces (Fishes), Reptilia (Reptiles)
Incorrect: Mammalia (Mammals), Aves (Birds)
Feedback: Fishes, amphibians, and reptiles are cold-blooded; their internal temperature fluctuates with the environment. Birds and mammals are warm-blooded (endothermic).

### Q45 [multi-select]
