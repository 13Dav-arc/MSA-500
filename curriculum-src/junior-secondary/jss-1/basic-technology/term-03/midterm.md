## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: How many primary functional classes of woodwork hand tools are recognized in the technical workshop?
A) Six (correct)[cite: 2]
B) Four
C) Eight
D) Two
Feedback: There are six classes of woodwork hand tools: measuring, marking out/setting out, work holding, boring, cutting and paring, and driving tools[cite: 2].
