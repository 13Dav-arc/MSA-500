## Key Terms
- Term: Les Salutations
- Definition: French greetings exchanged when meeting people (*Bonjour, Bonsoir, Salut*).
- Term: Prendre Congé
- Definition: Taking one's leave or saying goodbye (*Au revoir, À bientôt, À demain, Bonne nuit*).
- Term: L'Alphabet et les Accents
- Definition: The 26 letters of the French alphabet and the five orthographic accents (*aigu é, grave è/à/ù, circonflexe ˆ, tréma ¨, cédille ç*).
- Term: L'Élision
- Definition: The contraction where a final vowel is dropped and replaced with an apostrophe before a vowel sound (e.g., *l'ami, l'école, l'homme*).
- Term: Les Nombres Cardinaux (0–30)
- Definition: Cardinal numbers from *zéro* to *trente* used for counting, prices, and stating age.
- Term: Quel âge as-tu? / J'ai ... ans
- Definition: The standard conversational question and response for age in French, using the verb *avoir*.
- Term: S'appeler
- Definition: The reflexive verb meaning "to be called" or "to call oneself" (*Je m'appelle, Tu t'appelles, Il/Elle s'appelle*).
- Term: Voici
- Definition: A presentation word meaning "Here is..." or "This is...".
- Term: Enchanté / Enchantée
- Definition: The courteous expression meaning "Pleased to meet you / Delighted."
- Term: Les Pronoms Sujets
- Definition: Subject pronouns: *Je, Tu, Il, Elle, Nous, Vous, Ils, Elles*.

### 2. Golden Rules for Midterm Exam Success
1. **Age Always Uses *Avoir*:** Never write *"Je suis 12 ans"*. Always write: **J'ai [number] ans**!
2. **Accents Govern Pronunciation & Meaning:**
   - *Aigu (é)* points UP $\rightarrow$ closed "ay" sound (*l'école*).
   - *Grave (è)* points DOWN $\rightarrow$ open "eh" sound (*la mère*).
   - *Cédille (ç)* softens the letter C before A, O, U (*français, garçon*).
3. **Greeting Etiquette:**
   - Arriving at night $\rightarrow$ **Bonsoir**!
   - Going to sleep $\rightarrow$ **Bonne nuit**!
   - Addressing teachers $\rightarrow$ **Bonjour Monsieur / Madame** (never *Salut*!).
4. **Gender Agreement:**
   - Male: *Je suis nigérian*, *Enchanté*, *mon ami*.
   - Female: *Je suis nigériane*, *Enchantée*, *mon amie*.

## Comparative Matrix
| Category | Informal (Familiar) | Formal (Respectful) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Greeting** | *Salut!* (Hi) | *Bonjour, Monsieur / Madame!* |
| **Wellbeing Question** | *Comment ça va? / Comment vas-tu?* | *Comment allez-vous?* |
| **Asking Name** | *Comment tu t'appelles?* | *Comment vous vous appelez?* |
| **Asking Age** | *Quel âge as-tu?* | *Quel âge avez-vous?* |
| **Subject Pronoun** | **Tu** (singular friend/peer) | **Vous** (elder, superior, stranger) |

## Common Mistakes
1. **Translating Directly from English:** Writing *"Mon nom est"* (use *Je m'appelle*) or *"Je suis dix ans"* (use *J'ai dix ans*).
2. **Confusing 'G' and 'J' in the Alphabet:** In French, **G is *zhay*** and **J is *zhee***.
3. **Dropping the Word 'Ans':** Saying *"J'ai douze"* without adding the mandatory noun **ans** (years).
4. **Omitting the Feminine 'E':** A female student forgetting to add the feminine 'e' when writing *nigériane* or *Enchantée*.

## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which French greeting is used during daytime to mean "Good morning" or "Good day"?
A) Bonsoir
B) Bonjour (correct)
C) Bonne nuit
D) Au revoir
Feedback: *Bonjour* is the standard daytime greeting.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: What should you say when arriving at a dinner gathering at 7:00 PM in French?
A) Bonne nuit
B) Bonsoir (correct)
C) À demain
D) Au revoir
Feedback: *Bonsoir* means "Good evening" and is used when arriving at night.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: When is "Bonne nuit" strictly used?
A) When arriving at school in the morning
B) When departing to go to sleep at night (correct)
C) At lunchtime
D) When greeting a stranger
Feedback: *Bonne nuit* means "Good night" and is reserved strictly for bedtime.

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: How is the letter "Y" called in the French alphabet?
A) Epsilon
B) I grec (Greek I) (correct)
C) Double-vé
D) Zed
Feedback: The letter Y is called *i grec* (Greek I) in French.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: Which French accent slants upward from left to right (´) and appears ONLY on the letter E?
A) L'accent grave
B) L'accent aigu (correct)
C) Le tréma
D) La cédille
Feedback: *L'accent aigu* (´) appears exclusively on the letter E ($\mathbf{é}$).

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the purpose of the cedilla (ç) placed beneath the letter C?
A) To make the word silent
B) To soften the hard "K" sound into a soft "S" sound before A, O, or U (correct)
C) To double the length of the word
D) To make the letter sound like a drum
Feedback: The cedilla ($\mathbf{ç}$) changes a hard "K" sound into a soft "S" sound.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: What rule applies to the pronunciation of the letter "H" in standard French?
A) It is pronounced with a harsh cough
B) It is completely silent and never voiced (correct)
C) It is always pronounced like an English "H"
D) It is only voiced by children
Feedback: The French letter H is silent (*le h muet*).

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: Which French verb is grammatically required to ask and state age?
A) Être (To be)
B) Avoir (To have) (correct)
C) S'appeler (To be called)
D) Habiter (To live)
Feedback: Age in French is expressed using the verb *avoir* (to have): *J'ai douze ans*.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: How do you say "I am eleven years old" in French?
A) Je suis onze ans
B) J'ai onze ans (correct)
C) J'ai onze
D) Je m'appelle onze
Feedback: The correct formula is *J'ai onze ans*.

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the French word for the number fifteen (15)?
A) Quatorze
B) Quinze (correct)
C) Seize
D) Treize
Feedback: 15 is *quinze* in French.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: How is the number twenty-one (21) written in French?
A) Vingt-un
B) Vingt et un (correct)
C) Vingt-et-un
D) Trente-un
Feedback: 21 is written with the conjunction *et* without hyphens: *vingt et un*.

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: How do you say "My name is" in standard conversational French?
A) Mon nom est
B) Je m'appelle (correct)
C) Je suis nom
D) J'ai le nom
Feedback: *Je m'appelle* is the idiomatic French expression for "My name is."

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: How do you introduce a female friend by saying "Her name is Fatima"?
A) Il s'appelle Fatima
B) Elle s'appelle Fatima (correct)
C) Tu t'appelles Fatima
D) Je m'appelle Fatima
Feedback: "Her name is" translates to *Elle s'appelle*.

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: What polite courtesy meaning "Pleased to meet you" is spoken when meeting someone?
A) Au revoir
B) Enchanté / Enchantée (correct)
C) Bonne nuit
D) À demain
Feedback: *Enchanté* (or *Enchantée*) means "Pleased to meet you."
