## Hook
Imagine describing your typical school day to a pen pal living in Dakar, Senegal or Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire. At 06h00, your alarm rings and you wake up. You jump out of bed, wash your face, brush your teeth, take a quick shower, put on your freshly ironed school uniform, eat breakfast, and head out the door for morning assembly. In English, we simply say "I wash" or "I dress". But in French, actions that you perform on yourself use a special, elegant grammatical mechanism called **Reflexive Verbs (*Les Verbes Pronominaux*)**! When you say *"Je me réveille"* (I wake myself up) or *"Je m'habille"* (I dress myself), you are using reflexive pronouns that reflect the action directly back onto the subject. Combining these reflexive verbs with chronological connectors like *d'abord, ensuite, puis,* and *enfin* allows you to narrate your entire day with cinematic brilliance. In this lesson, you will master the French daily routine and bring your schedule to life!

## Key Terms
- Term: La Routine Quotidienne
- Definition: The habitual sequence of activities performed regularly each day from morning to night.
- Term: Les Verbes Pronominaux (Reflexive Verbs)
- Definition: Verbs preceded by the reflexive pronoun *se* in the infinitive, indicating that the subject performs the action upon itself.
- Term: Le Pronom Réfléchi
- Definition: The reflexive pronoun (*me, te, se, nous, vous, se*) that matches the subject and precedes the conjugated verb.
- Term: Se Réveiller
- Definition: Reflexive verb meaning "to wake up" from sleep (*Je me réveille à 6h00*).
- Term: Se Lever
- Definition: Reflexive verb meaning "to get out of bed" or "to stand up" (*Je me lève*).
- Term: Se Laver
- Definition: Reflexive verb meaning "to wash oneself" or bathe (*Je me lave*).
- Term: Se Brosser les Dents
- Definition: Reflexive verb phrase meaning "to brush one's teeth".
- Term: S'habiller
- Definition: Reflexive verb meaning "to get dressed"; contracts to *m'habiller, s'habiller* before the silent 'h'.
- Term: Se Coucher
- Definition: Reflexive verb meaning "to go to bed" at night (*Je me couche à 21h30*).
- Term: D'abord
- Definition: Chronological sequencing adverb meaning "first of all" or "at first".
- Term: Ensuite / Puis
- Definition: Chronological transition adverbs meaning "next" and "then".
- Term: Enfin
- Definition: Chronological concluding adverb meaning "finally" or "at last".

## Core Concept
### 1. What is a Reflexive Verb?
[IMAGE: alt="Educational diagram and authentic visual representation of La Routine Quotidienne et Les Verbes Pronominaux" caption="La Routine Quotidienne et Les Verbes Pronominaux: Core Concepts and Pedagogical Illustration"]

In French, when an action is performed by the subject onto the subject itself, the verb is reflexive and takes a **reflexive pronoun**:
- *Je lave la voiture.* (Non-reflexive: I wash the car - the action is on the car).
- *Je **me** lave.* (Reflexive: I wash myself - the action is on me!).

---

### 2. Conjugating Reflexive Verbs in the Present Tense
Here is the complete pattern using **se réveiller** (to wake up) and **s'habiller** (to get dressed):

```
┌─────────────────┬───────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────┐
│ SUBJECT PRONOUN │ REFLEXIVE PRONOUN │ CONJUGATION PATTERN              │
├─────────────────┼───────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┤
│ Je (I)          │ me (m' before v.) │ Je *me réveille* / Je *m'habille*│
├─────────────────┼───────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tu (You sing.)  │ te (t' before v.) │ Tu *te réveilles* / Tu *t'habilles*
├─────────────────┼───────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┤
│ Il / Elle (He/She)│ se (s' before v.)│ Il / Elle *se réveille*          │
├─────────────────┼───────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┤
│ Nous (We)       │ nous              │ Nous *nous réveillons*           │
├─────────────────┼───────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┤
│ Vous (You pl.)  │ vous              │ Vous *vous réveillez*            │
├─────────────────┼───────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┤
│ Ils / Elles (They)│ se (s' before v.)│ Ils / Elles *se réveillent*      │
└─────────────────┴───────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────┘
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Notice Double Pronouns for "Nous" and "Vous":**
> In reflexive sentences with *Nous* and *Vous*, the pronoun appears twice:
> - *Nous **nous** levons.* (Subject pronoun + Reflexive pronoun).
> - *Vous **vous** brossez les dents.*
> Do not drop the second pronoun!

---

### 3. Chronological Connectors for Daily Sequencing
To tell a fluid story of your day, connect your sentences with time transition adverbs:
1. **D'abord:** First of all (*D'abord, je me réveille à six heures.*)
2. **Ensuite:** Next (*Ensuite, je prends un bain et je m'habille.*)
3. **Puis:** Then (*Puis, je mange mon petit-déjeuner avec ma famille.*)
4. **Après cela:** After that (*Après cela, je vais à l'école en bus.*)
5. **Enfin:** Finally (*Enfin, le soir, je me couche à vingt et une heures.*)

## Common Mistakes
- **Forgetting the reflexive pronoun:** Saying *Je réveille à six heures* means you are waking someone else up! To say you wake up yourself, you must say: **Je me réveille**.
- **Omitting the second pronoun with "Nous" or "Vous":** Writing *Nous lavons* instead of **Nous nous lavons**. *Nous lavons* means "We wash (something else)", whereas *Nous nous lavons* means "We wash ourselves".
- **Forgetting elision with "s'habiller":** Writing *Je me habille* or *Il se habille* violates French elision. Because *habiller* begins with a silent 'h', you must contract: **Je m'habille**, **Il s'habille**.

## Worked Examples
### Example 1: Conjugating a Reflexive Sentence
- *Prompt:* Translate into French: "We brush our teeth every morning."
- *French Breakdown:*
  - We = *Nous*
  - reflexive pronoun for *nous* = *nous*
  - brush (verb *brosser*) = *brossons*
  - our teeth = *les dents* (In French, body parts with reflexive verbs take definite articles, not possessives!)
  - every morning = *chaque matin*
- *Result:* **Nous nous brossons les dents chaque matin.**

### Example 2: Negative Reflexive Construction
- *Affirmative:* *Je me couche tard.* (I go to bed late.)
- *Negative Rule:* The negative bracket *ne... pas* surrounds the reflexive pronoun AND the conjugated verb!
- *Negative Sentence:* **Je ne me couche pas tard.** (I do not go to bed late.)

### Example 3: Sequencing Sentences
- *English:* "First, I wake up; then, I eat breakfast."
- *Translation:* **D'abord, je me réveille ; ensuite, je prends mon petit-déjeuner.**

## Guardian Sync
- **Morning Routine Narration:** In the morning, prompt your child to narrate their actions in French: *Je me réveille, je me brosse les dents, je m'habille!*.
- **Sequencing Challenge:** Ask your child to list the 4 key transition words in order: *D'abord* (first), *Ensuite* (next), *Puis* (then), *Enfin* (finally).
- **Bedtime Sign-Off:** At bedtime, say *Bonne nuit, va te coucher!* and have your child reply: *Oui, maman/papa, je vais me coucher!*.
