## Key Terms
- Term: Les Pièces et les Meubles
- Definition: The rooms (*salon, chambre, cuisine*) and furniture (*lit, table, canapé*) forming the domestic environment.
- Term: Les Prépositions Spatiales
- Definition: Words indicating location and spatial relationships (*sur, sous, dans, devant, derrière, entre, à côté de*).
- Term: Les Articles Partitifs
- Definition: The quantity words *du, de la, de l', des* used to denote an unspecified portion of food or drink.
- Term: La Règle de Négation des Partitifs
- Definition: The rule requiring *du, de la, de l', des* to transform into *de* or *d'* in negative sentences.
- Term: Les Métiers et l'Omission de l'Article
- Definition: The grammatical principle that professions directly following *être* do not take indefinite articles (*Il est médecin*).
- Term: Les Pluriels Irréguliers en -aux
- Definition: Masculine nouns ending in *-al* (like *un animal, un cheval*) that take *-aux* in the plural (*des animaux, des chevaux*).

## Core Concept
This Midterm Assessment integrates the four foundational thematic units of the Third Term:
1. **Week 01 (The House & Spatial Prepositions):** Identifying domestic spaces (*la cuisine, le salon, la salle de bains, la chambre*), furniture placement, and positional prepositions (*sur, sous, dans, devant, derrière, à côté du/de la*).
2. **Week 02 (Food, Drinks & Partitives):** Daily meals (*le petit-déjeuner, le déjeuner, le dîner*), food items (*le pain, le riz, le poulet, la viande*), partitives (*du, de la, de l', des*), the negative conversion to *de/d'*, and ordering politely (*Je voudrais...*).
3. **Week 03 (Professions & Ambitions):** Occupational vocabulary, feminine gender alterations (*-ier $\rightarrow$ -ière, -teur $\rightarrow$ -trice*), the omission of *un/une* after *être* (*Elle est avocate*), and expressing future ambitions (*Je veux être...*).
4. **Week 04 (Domestic & Wild Animals):** Household pets (*chien, chat*), farm animals (*vache, chèvre, mouton, cheval*), wild animals (*lion, éléphant, singe*), irregular plurals (*animaux, chevaux*), and ecological conservation.

## Comparative Matrix
| Topic Area | Target Grammar Principle | Exemplar Sentence | Key Rule to Remember |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **House / Prepositions** | Contraction with *de* | *Le fauteuil est **à côté du** lit.* | *de + le* contracts to *du* |
| **Food / Drinks** | Partitive in affirmative vs negative | *Je mange **du** riz* $\rightarrow$ *Je ne mange pas **de** riz.* | Partitives become *de/d'* in negative |
| **Professions** | Zero article after *être* | *Mon oncle est **ingénieur**.* | Never say *est un ingénieur* |
| **Animals** | Plural formation in *-aux* | *Les **chevaux** courent vite.* | *cheval* $\rightarrow$ *chevaux* (never *chevals*) |

## Common Mistakes
- **Confusing "sur" (on) and "sous" (under):** Double-check the vowel sound and meaning before answering spatial questions.
- **Using "du" in negative food sentences:** Remember that in *Je ne mange pas...*, the partitive *du* must change to **de**.
- **Adding "un/une" to professions:** Do not translate word-for-word from English "He is **a** doctor" to *Il est un médecin*. The correct French is **Il est médecin**.
- **Writing "animals" or "chevals":** Always use the irregular plural form ending in *-aux* (**animaux**, **chevaux**).

## Worked Examples
### Example 1: Combining Food and House Vocabulary
- *Prompt:* Translate into French: "We eat rice and chicken in the dining room."
- *Breakdown:*
  - We eat = *Nous mangeons*
  - some rice = *du riz* (partitive)
  - and = *et*
  - some chicken = *du poulet* (partitive)
  - in = *dans*
  - the dining room = *la salle à manger*
- *Result:* **Nous mangeons du riz et du poulet dans la salle à manger.**

### Example 2: Describing a Family Member's Profession
- *Prompt:* Say in French: "My mother is an empathetic nurse."
- *Translation:* **Ma mère est une infirmière empathique.** (Note: article *une* appears because the adjective *empathique* modifies the profession!).
- *Without adjective:* **Ma mère est infirmière.**

## Quiz

### Q1 [single-choice]
Stem: Which room in the house is specifically intended for cooking meals?
A) La cuisine (correct)
B) La chambre
C) Le salon
D) La salle de bains
Feedback: *La cuisine* is the kitchen where meals are prepared.

### Q2 [single-choice]
Stem: Complete the sentence: "Le chien dort paisiblement ______ la table." (under)
A) sous (correct)
B) sur
C) dans
D) devant
Feedback: *Sous* means under or underneath: *sous la table*.

### Q3 [single-choice]
Stem: Complete the sentence with the correct partitive article: "Au petit-déjeuner, Fatima mange ______ pain." (masculine singular)
A) du (correct)
B) de la
C) de l'
D) des
Feedback: *Le pain* is masculine singular, so it takes the partitive *du* (*de + le = du*).

### Q4 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the negative form of the sentence "Je bois du lait"?
A) Je ne bois pas de lait. (correct)
B) Je ne bois pas du lait.
C) Je bois pas de lait.
D) Je ne bois pas un lait.
Feedback: In negative sentences, partitive articles (*du, de la, de l', des*) change to *de*: *Je ne bois pas de lait*.

### Q5 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following sentences correctly states someone's profession in French?
A) Elle est avocate. (correct)
B) Elle est une avocate.
C) Elle a avocate.
D) C'est avocate.
Feedback: In French, the indefinite article (*un/une*) is omitted after the verb *être* when stating a profession: *Elle est avocate*.

### Q6 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the feminine form of the profession "infirmier" (nurse)?
A) infirmière (correct)
B) infirmiresse
C) infirmieure
D) infirmiere
Feedback: Nouns ending in *-ier* form their feminine in *-ière*: *infirmière*.

### Q7 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the plural of "un animal"?
A) des animaux (correct)
B) des animals
C) des animales
D) des animo
Feedback: Nouns ending in *-al* form their plural in *-aux*: *des animaux*.

### Q8 [single-choice]
Stem: Which of the following animals is classified as a domestic pet and guard animal (*le chien*)?
A) The dog (correct)
B) The cat
C) The lion
D) The horse
Feedback: *Le chien* is the dog.

### Q9 [single-choice]
Stem: Where is food kept cold and preserved in a modern kitchen?
A) Dans le réfrigérateur (correct)
B) Sur le lit
C) Derrière la porte
D) Sous la cuisinière
Feedback: Food is kept fresh inside the refrigerator (*dans le réfrigérateur*).

### Q10 [single-choice]
Stem: Complete the sentence: "Monsieur Okafor est ingénieur; il travaille ______ Lagos."
A) à (correct)
B) en
C) au
D) chez
Feedback: Cities take the preposition *à*: *à Lagos*, *à Abuja*, *à Ibadan*.

### Q11 [single-choice]
Stem: Complete the sentence with the correct partitive: "David boit ______ eau fraîche après le sport." (vowel-initial)
A) de l' (correct)
B) du
C) de la
D) des
Feedback: *Eau* begins with a vowel, so it takes the elided partitive *de l'*: *de l'eau*.

### Q12 [single-choice]
Stem: What is the plural of "un cheval" (a horse)?
A) des chevaux (correct)
B) des chevals
C) des cheveaux
D) des chevaus
Feedback: *Un cheval* becomes *des chevaux* in the plural.

### Q13 [single-choice]
Stem: What does "Je veux être médecin" mean in English?
A) I want to be a doctor. (correct)
B) I am a doctor.
C) My father is a doctor.
D) I am visiting the doctor.
Feedback: *Je veux être médecin* means "I want to be a doctor".

### Q14 [single-choice]
Stem: Complete the sentence: "Le chat est assis ______ la chaise." (on)
A) sur (correct)
B) sous
C) dans
D) chez
Feedback: *Sur* means on or on top of: *sur la chaise*.

### Q15 [true-false]
Stem: In French, the phrase "Je ne mange pas de viande" is grammatically correct.
A) True (correct)
B) False
Feedback: In negative sentences, the partitive article transforms into *de*: *Je ne mange pas de viande*.

### Q16 [true-false]
Stem: The French word *le salon* refers to the bedroom.
A) True
B) False (correct)
Feedback: *Le salon* is the living room or parlour. The bedroom is *la chambre*.

### Q17 [multi-select]
Stem: Which of the following are domestic farm animals in French? (Select all that apply)
A) La chèvre (correct)
B) La vache (correct)
C) Le mouton (correct)
D) Le lion
Feedback: *La chèvre* (goat), *la vache* (cow), and *le mouton* (sheep) are farm animals. *Le lion* is a wild animal.

### Q18 [multi-select]
Stem: Which of the following partitive articles are used with singular nouns? (Select all that apply)
A) du (correct)
B) de la (correct)
C) de l' (correct)
D) des
Feedback: *Du* (masc. sing.), *de la* (fem. sing.), and *de l'* (vowel sing.) are singular partitive articles. *Des* is plural.

- La chambre || The bedroom
- Le salon || The living room
- La salle à manger || The dining room
Feedback: *cuisine* = kitchen, *chambre* = bedroom, *salon* = living room, *salle à manger* = dining room.

### Q20 [matching]
Stem: Match each profession with its English meaning:
- L'avocat || The lawyer
- Le médecin || The doctor
- L'enseignant || The teacher
- L'agriculteur || The farmer
Feedback: *avocat* = lawyer, *médecin* = doctor, *enseignant* = teacher, *agriculteur* = farmer.
