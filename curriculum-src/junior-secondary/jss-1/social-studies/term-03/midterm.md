## Key Terms
- Term: Contemporary Social Problems
- Definition: Widespread undesirable conditions, vices, and negative challenges in modern society that disrupt social peace, threaten public health, and hinder national progress.
- Term: Examination Malpractice
- Definition: Any unauthorized, illegal, or unethical act committed before, during, or after an examination to secure unmerited grades.
- Term: Decree 18 of 1984
- Definition: The Nigerian military decree (later codified as the Examination Malpractice Act) imposing up to 21 years of imprisonment on academic cheats and collaborators.
- Term: Cultism
- Definition: Membership, activities, or allegiance to a secret, oath-bound group characterized by nocturnal rituals, weapons, and violent acts.
- Term: Pyrates Confraternity
- Definition: The first student confraternity in Nigeria, founded in 1952 at University College, Ibadan, by Wole Soyinka and six fellow scholars as a peaceful, non-violent intellectual club.
- Term: Blood Oath
- Definition: A terrifying initiation ritual where an individual drinks blood or cuts their flesh to swear absolute secrecy and obedience to a secret cult.
- Term: HIV
- Definition: Human Immunodeficiency Virus, a retrovirus that attacks and destroys the CD4 helper T-lymphocytes of the immune system.
- Term: AIDS
- Definition: Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome, the advanced medical stage of HIV infection characterized by opportunistic infections and low CD4 counts.
- Term: Antiretroviral Therapy (ART)
- Definition: Medical drug therapy that suppresses HIV viral replication, enabling people living with HIV to live long, healthy, and productive lives.
- Term: ABC Prevention Protocol
- Definition: The proven public health model for preventing sexually transmitted HIV: **A**bstinence, **B**e faithful, and **C**ondom use.

1. **The Anatomy of Examination Malpractice (Weeks 01–02):**
   - Driven by academic laziness, truancy, lack of preparation, parental obsession with high-status courses, and corrupt school officials.
   - Takes many forms: smuggled micro-chips, giraffing, mercenary impersonation, and corrupt "miracle centers."
   - Sanctioned under the Examination Malpractice Act (Decree 18 of 1984) with up to 21 years imprisonment without option of fine.
   - Threatens national safety by producing incompetent professionals (collapsing building engineers, quack doctors).

2. **The Menace of Cultism (Week 03):**
   - Founded in 1952 at University College, Ibadan, by "The Magnificent Seven" (including Wole Soyinka) as a peaceful, non-violent intellectual club advocating social justice and African pride.
   - Degenerated in the 1980s into violent, armed criminal gangs using guns, axes, acid, and blood oaths to fight turf wars and serve as political thugs.
   - Attracts insecure youth with false promises of protection, but delivers paranoia, rustication, physical injury, or premature death.
   - Punished with 10 to 21 years of imprisonment under state anti-cultism laws.

3. **The HIV/AIDS Challenge and Public Health (Week 04):**
   - HIV selectively destroys CD4 helper T-lymphocytes, leaving the body vulnerable to opportunistic infections (tuberculosis, wasting, candidiasis).
   - Transmitted via four routes: unprotected sex, contaminated blood transfusions, unsterilized sharp instruments (needles, clippers), and mother-to-child transmission (MTCT).
   - Cannot be transmitted by casual contact: handshakes, hugging, sharing desks/food, toilet seats, or mosquito bites.
   - Effectively managed by Antiretroviral Therapy (ART), which allows normal life expectancy.
   - Protected by the HIV/AIDS Anti-Discrimination Act of 2014, outlawing stigmatization and discrimination.

## Common Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Believing that whisper-helping a classmate on an exam is harmless.
  - *Correction:* Collusion is an examination crime that undermines true learning, devalues academic integrity, and carries severe disciplinary sanctions.
- **Mistake 2:** Assuming that modern cults are just ordinary school social clubs.
  - *Correction:* Modern cults are illegal, armed, violent organizations sworn to blood oaths and criminal conduct. Associating with them leads to prison, rustication, or death.
- **Mistake 3:** Believing that an HIV carrier can be identified by physical appearance.
  - *Correction:* A person carrying HIV can appear completely healthy and energetic for many years. Only a laboratory blood test can confirm infection.
- **Mistake 4:** Thinking that mosquitoes transmit HIV like they transmit malaria.
  - *Correction:* HIV cannot survive, reproduce, or be transmitted through the salivary glands of insects. Mosquito transmission of HIV is a scientific impossibility.

## Worked Examples
- **Example 1: Identifying Academic and Disciplinary Hazards**
  - *Scenario:* Segun is invited by his roommate to an off-campus cyber cafe where a technician promises to hack into an examination database to alter his failing test grades for 50,000 Naira.
  - *Analysis:*
    - *Vice:* Cyber-assisted examination malpractice and forgery.
    - *Risk:* Severe criminal fraud under the Cybercrimes Act and Examination Malpractice Act. Examination databases feature immutable audit trails and backup servers.
    - *Consequence:* Immediate expulsion, blacklisting by examination bodies, and police arrest.
    - *Correct Action:* Segun declines, takes academic responsibility, seeks remedial tutoring from his teachers, and passes his retake exam honestly.
- **Example 2: Overcoming Stigma in a Community Sports Team**
  - *Scenario:* In a local football club, members discover that the goalkeeper takes daily Antiretroviral (ART) pills for HIV. Two players refuse to pass him the ball, demanding his dismissal.
  - *Educational Intervention:* The team coach sits all players down and explains: (1) HIV cannot be transmitted by playing sports, sweating, or touching footballs; (2) Taking ART suppresses the virus, making the goalkeeper healthy and energetic; (3) Discriminating against a teammate is illegal under the Anti-Discrimination Act of 2014. The players shake hands, and the team wins their league championship.
