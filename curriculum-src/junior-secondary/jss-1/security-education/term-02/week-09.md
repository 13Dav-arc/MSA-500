## Hook
Picture a crowded, noisy municipal market on a Friday afternoon. A sudden scream echoes through the vegetable stalls: *"Thief! Ole! Barawo! Catch him!"* Within seconds, a terrified young man is knocked to the ground by an enraged crowd. Without asking a single question or checking if he actually stole anything, people begin stomping on his head, hitting him with iron rods, placing a used rubber car tire over his neck, and dousing him with petrol, ready to strike a match! Five minutes later, the real thief is caught three streets away holding the stolen purse, while the young man lying dead and incinerated on the road was simply an innocent university student running to catch a bus! That horrific, barbaric crime is called **Jungle Justice**! Why is mob violence a deadly crime of murder under Nigerian law, why must citizens cooperate lawfully with the police, and how does your intelligence protect the nation? Welcome to **Citizen Cooperation and the 80% Term 2 Mastery Gate**!

## Key Terms
- Term: Citizen Cooperation
- Definition: The proactive, lawful collaboration between ordinary members of the public and official security agencies through information sharing, reporting, and civic partnership.
- Term: Whistleblowing
- Definition: The courageous act of reporting secret, illicit, dangerous, or corrupt activities within an organization or community to law enforcement authorities.
- Term: Jungle Justice (Mob Lynching)
- Definition: The illegal, extrajudicial execution, torture, or severe beating of a suspected criminal by an enraged crowd without a fair trial in a court of law.
- Term: Extrajudicial Killing
- Definition: The unlawful killing of a person by government agents or private individuals without the sanction of any valid judicial proceeding or legal process.
- Term: Citizen's Arrest
- Definition: The lawful right of an ordinary citizen under the Administration of Criminal Justice Act to temporarily apprehend someone caught in the act of committing a felony and hand them over immediately to the police without violence.
- Term: Anonymous Tip-Off
- Definition: Confidential security intelligence or information delivered to law enforcement agencies without disclosing the identity of the informant.
- Term: National Emergency Number (112)
- Definition: The unified national toll-free telephone emergency line that instantly connects calling citizens to police, fire, medical, and civil defence emergency dispatchers.
- Term: Due Process of Law
- Definition: The constitutional guarantee that every accused person is entitled to a fair hearing, legal representation, and trial before a competent court before any punishment is inflicted.
- Term: Section 33 of the Constitution
- Definition: The constitutional provision enshrining the fundamental right to life, establishing that no person shall be deprived intentionally of their life except in execution of a court sentence.
- Term: 80% Mastery Gate
- Definition: The academic benchmark requiring candidates to demonstrate at least 80% mastery across all Term 2 security agencies before qualifying for the Terminal Examination.

### The Horror and Criminality of "Jungle Justice"
Jungle justice is a barbaric symptom of lawlessness that threatens every civilized person. Under Section 316 and 319 of the Criminal Code:
- **Everyone participating in a mob lynching is guilty of Murder**, which carries a mandatory sentence of **Capital Punishment (Death Penalty)**.
- **Destroying Forensics:** Killing a suspect eliminates vital intelligence. A petty pickpocket may belong to a vast kidnapping syndicate that the police could have dismantled if he were interrogated lawfully.
- **Murdering Innocent People:** History proves that victims of jungle justice are frequently innocent individuals falsely accused due to mistaken identity, personal vendettas, or panic (such as the tragic murder of four innocent university students in Aluu, Rivers State in 2012).
- **Subverting the Constitution:** Section 36(5) of the Nigerian Constitution guarantees that every person charged with a criminal offense is presumed innocent until proven guilty in a court of law.

### The Lawful Citizen's Arrest Protocol
Under Section 20 of the Administration of Criminal Justice Act (ACJA) 2015, any citizen can execute a citizen's arrest under strict legal conditions:
1. The suspect must be caught in the actual commission of a serious criminal offense (e.g., snatching a bag, breaking a lock, assaulting someone).
2. The citizen must use only **reasonable, non-lethal physical restraint** (e.g., holding their arms).
3. Beating, stripping naked, torturing, or burning the suspect is strictly **illegal and criminal**.
4. The suspect must be handed over **immediately** to the nearest police officer or police station without delay.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Believing that Joining a Mob Attack Protects You from Prosecution
Assuming that because hundreds of people were throwing stones at a suspect, the police cannot arrest you.
> **Correction:** Under the legal doctrine of **Common Intention**, every single person who participates in, encourages, or strikes a blow during a mob lynching is charged as a principal offender for murder. Smartphone video recordings and CCTV cameras now make it easy to identify and arrest every mob participant.

### Mistake 2: Fearing that Reporting a Crime Makes You an Automatic Target
Assuming that if you report drug pushers or kidnappers to the police, your name will be announced on the news.
> **Correction:** Official agencies (Police CRU, EFCC, NDLEA, ICPC) maintain strict **informant confidentiality protocols**. Citizens can report anonymously via mobile apps, toll-free lines, or sealed drop-boxes without revealing their names.

### Mistake 3: Filming a Mob Lynching for Social Media Likes Instead of Calling 112
Standing passively and recording a human being being beaten to death to gain followers on TikTok or Instagram.
> **Correction:** Passively filming a murder makes you a moral accomplice to horror. Call the Police immediately (**112**) or alert market elders to intervene and stop the lynching.

## Worked Examples

### Scenario 1: Executing a Lawful Citizen's Arrest
**Situation:** While standing outside a shop, 25-year-old Chijioke watches a man use an iron pry bar to force open the boot of a parked car and remove a laptop bag.
- *Wrong Action:* Chijioke screams "Thief!", gathers a crowd with wooden planks, and beats the man unconscious.
- *Right Action (Lawful Protocol):*
  1. *Restraint:* Chijioke and a fellow shopkeeper swiftly tackle the man, hold his arms behind his back, and take away the pry bar.
  2. *Protection:* They prevent bystanders from throwing stones or hitting the suspect.
  3. *Handover:* Chijioke immediately dials **112** or walks the restrained suspect 100 meters to the Divisional Police Station down the street. The stolen laptop is preserved as legal evidence.

### Scenario 2: Anonymous Whistleblowing
**Situation:** A student notices that an abandoned warehouse behind his estate is visited every night at 2:00 AM by heavily armed men in tinted vans offloading large wooden crates labeled "military surplus."
- **Correct Whistleblowing Action:**
  1. The student does NOT approach the warehouse or attempt to spy closely.
  2. The student informs their parents, who lodge an anonymous intelligence tip on the **DSS / Police State Command hotline** or use the **EFCC/Police mobile portal**.
  3. Tactical security teams conduct surveillance, raid the building legally, and seize an illegal weapons cache, neutralizing a terror cell without endangering the student.
