### Essential Takeaways for Examination Success
1. **The Nigeria Police Force (Week 01):** Established by Section 214 of the Constitution; led by the Inspector-General of Police (IGP). Core mission is crime prevention, apprehension of offenders, and public order. Under the Police Act 2020, suspects must be charged within 24 to 48 hours, torture is banned, and bail is strictly free.
2. **The Armed Forces of Nigeria (Week 02):** Governed by Section 217; headed by the President as Commander-in-Chief and coordinated by the Chief of Defence Staff (CDS). Comprises the Nigerian Army (*"Victory is from God Alone"*), Nigerian Navy (*"Onward Together"*), and Nigerian Air Force (*"Willing... Able... Ready"*). Dedicated to territorial integrity and defense against foreign aggression.
3. **Paramilitary Guardians (Weeks 03–04):**
   - *NSCDC:* Protects Critical National Assets and Infrastructure (pipelines, power lines, railways), regulates Private Guard Companies, and deploys Agro-Rangers.
   - *FRSC:* Headed by the Corps Marshal; reduces highway crashes, enforces speed limiters, issues Driver's Licenses, and operates emergency toll-free line **122**.
   - *NIS:* Manages people and migration; produces Nigerian passports; monitors international airports, seaports, and land borders; issues CERPAC permits.
   - *NCS:* Manages goods and fiscal revenue; collects import duties; intercepts smuggled contraband; protects local manufacturing.
4. **Integrity, Intelligence, & Drug Control (Weeks 06–07):**
   - *EFCC:* Investigates cybercrime ("Yahoo-Yahoo"), money laundering, and advance fee fraud (419); recovers looted assets through court forfeiture.
   - *ICPC:* Eradicates corruption in the public service and government ministries; establishes Anti-Corruption Clubs in schools.
   - *DSS:* Conducts domestic intelligence gathering, counter-espionage, counter-terrorism, and provides VIP close protection.
   - *NDLEA:* Dual strategy of supply reduction (arresting traffickers, K-9 sniffer dogs, farm destruction) and demand reduction (WADA campaigns, rehabilitation).
5. **Corrections, Fire Rescue, & Civic Responsibility (Weeks 08–09):**
   - *NCoS:* Governed by the 2019 Act; custodial and non-custodial streams; enforces the 3 R's: Reformation, Rehabilitation, and Reintegration.
   - *FFS:* Suppresses fires, conducts building collapse and hydraulic rescue ("jaws of life"), operates hydrants, and coordinates with emergency line **112**.
   - *Citizen Cooperation:* Security is everybody's business; reporting intelligence and executing lawful citizen's arrests; absolute rejection of barbaric jungle justice to uphold Section 33 Right to Life and Section 36 Due Process.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Confusing the Statutory Mandates of Specialized Agencies
Attributing the investigation of cybercrime to the Fire Service, or expecting Customs to issue national passports.
> **Correction:** Each agency has clear statutory boundaries defined by the National Assembly. Passports belong to NIS; tariffs belong to NCS; cyber fraud belongs to EFCC; fire rescue belongs to FFS.

### Mistake 2: Thinking Jungle Justice Can Ever Be Justified
Believing that if an eyewitness saw someone steal, burning them alive on the road is acceptable.
> **Correction:** Jungle justice is never justified under any circumstances. It is an act of cold-blooded murder that destroys human lives, eliminates legal evidence, and leads to mandatory death sentences for the mob participants.

### Mistake 3: Paying for Police Bail Under Duress
Believing that bail requires paying cash to an investigating officer.
> **Correction:** Bail is unconditionally **Free**. Any officer demanding bail money is committing criminal extortion. Report such conduct immediately to the Police Complaint Response Unit (CRU).

## Worked Examples

### Problem 1: Multi-Agency Emergency Incident Response
**Scenario:** An oil tanker crashes into an electrical transformer on a federal highway, bursting into flames. Simultaneously, looters attempt to scoop leaking fuel, while a driver with a forged passport attempts to flee through a nearby border crossing.
- **Agency Action Plan:**
  1. *Fire Suppression & Rescue:* The **Federal Fire Service (FFS)** deploys foam fire tankers to extinguish the hydrocarbon blaze and rescues trapped victims.
  2. *Traffic Control & Evacuation:* The **Federal Road Safety Corps (FRSC)** diverts traffic, establishes safety perimeters, and transports casualties.
  3. *Infrastructure Protection:* The **NSCDC** secures the damaged transformer to prevent looting of copper coils and electrocution of civilians.
  4. *Border Interdiction:* The **Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS)** intercepts the fleeing driver with the forged passport at the border crossing.

### Problem 2: Allocating Integrity and Intelligence Cases
**Scenario:** Three distinct national security incidents are detected:
- *Case A:* Foreign spies are caught photographing classified naval defense maps at a coastal military dockyard.
- *Case B:* A criminal syndicate runs a global online romance and business email scam from a cyber cafe.
- *Case C:* A ministry permanent secretary demands a ten-million-naira bribe to approve school construction funds.
- **Correct Investigative Jurisdiction:**
  - *Case A* is foreign espionage; it falls under the **State Security Service (DSS)** and Military Intelligence.
  - *Case B* is international internet cybercrime; it falls under the **EFCC**.
  - *Case C* is public service bribery and procurement graft; it falls under the **ICPC**.
