### Essential Syllabus Takeaways
1. **Security Foundations (Weeks 01–02):** Security is freedom from fear, danger, and anxiety. It operates across four tiers: Personal, Home, Community, and National. In domestic spaces, carbon monoxide from indoor generators is a silent killer; generators must strictly operate outdoors at least 20 feet away from windows and doors.
2. **Common Crimes & The Law (Weeks 03–06):**
   - *Theft:* Taking property without consent.
   - *Burglary:* Breaking and entering a dwelling house at night with felonious intent.
   - *Assault & Battery:* Assault is the threat of violence causing imminent fear; Battery is actual physical contact. Aggravated assault causing permanent disfigurement is Grievous Bodily Harm (GBH).
   - *Vandalism & Arson:* Malicious destruction of property and intentional burning. Pipeline vandalism and infrastructure sabotage carry mandatory life imprisonment under the Miscellaneous Offences Act and Criminal Code Section 443.
3. **Crime Prevention & Target Hardening (Week 07):** Rational Choice Theory dictates that making crimes harder, riskier, and less rewarding stops criminals. Mortise deadbolts, natural surveillance, and neighborhood watch associations form multi-layered defensive rings.
4. **School Safety & Threat Evasion (Weeks 08–09):** Bullying in any form (physical, verbal, relational, cyber) damages learning; students must act as courageous upstanders. When facing predatory strangers, practice the "No, Go, Yell, Tell" protocol, demand the family secret code word, and head directly to verified Safe Havens.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Confusing Night-Time Burglary with Day-Time Housebreaking
Many candidates treat burglary and housebreaking as interchangeable terms.
> **Correction:** Under Nigerian criminal jurisprudence, breaking into a dwelling house at night (between 6:30 PM and 6:30 AM) with felonious intent is strictly **Burglary** (a felony carrying up to life imprisonment). Doing so during daylight hours is classified as **Housebreaking**.

### Mistake 2: Using Matches or Lighters to Locate a Gas Leak
Striking a match or clicking a lighter to see where a cylinder is leaking gas.
> **Correction:** Never use naked flames! LPG vapor ignites instantly upon contact with any spark or flame, causing fatal explosions. Always apply soapy water with a sponge and look for bubbles.

### Mistake 3: Believing Assault Requires Physical Striking
Thinking a person cannot be charged with assault unless they actually lay hands on someone.
> **Correction:** An assault occurs the moment a person intentionally creates an immediate fear of unlawful violence (e.g., raising a heavy iron rod threateningly). Once physical contact occurs, it becomes Battery.

### Mistake 4: Running Indoors or into Lonely Alleys When Pursued
Seeking hiding spots inside deserted alleys or uncompleted buildings when trailed by suspicious persons.
> **Correction:** Isolation leaves a student cornered without witnesses or help. Always sprint towards brightly illuminated, crowded **Safe Havens** (banks, active filling stations, police posts).

## Worked Examples

### Problem 1: Emergency Risk Evaluation
**Scenario:** While studying at home on a stormy evening, 12-year-old Femi smells rotten cabbage near the kitchen. Outside, rain is pouring heavily, and the house inverter battery is running low. What sequence of actions must Femi take?
- **Analysis:** The smell of rotten cabbage indicates that liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) is leaking into the kitchen.
- **Action Sequence:**
  1. *Immediate Gas Isolation:* Femi must immediately enter the kitchen without switching on any electrical lights or wall fans (switching electrical appliances generates internal micro-sparks).
  2. *Shut Valve:* Turn the cylinder regulator valve fully to the "OFF" position.
  3. *Ventilate:* Open all kitchen windows and doors wide to allow heavy gas vapors to disperse into the open air.
  4. *Evacuate & Warn:* Alert all family members to exit the kitchen area and do not strike matches or turn on appliances until the smell completely clears.

### Problem 2: Analyzing School Bullying Intervention
**Scenario:** In an anonymous note, a JSS 1 student writes that an older student demands ₦500 daily behind the sports pavilion and threatens to beat him if he speaks. The victim's grades have dropped from 85% to 45%, and he complains of stomach pains every morning before school.
- **Identification:** This is a clear case of **Extortion** combined with **Physical Intimidation and Bullying**.
- **Correct Institutional Remedy:**
  1. *Guidance Counseling:* Provide immediate emotional assurance and psychological support to the victim.
  2. *Disciplinary Action:* The School Safety Committee must summon the perpetrator, involve their parents, and enforce zero-tolerance disciplinary sanctions.
  3. *Hotspot Patrol:* Station a Teacher Safety Officer at the sports pavilion during recess to permanently eliminate the unsupervised zone.
