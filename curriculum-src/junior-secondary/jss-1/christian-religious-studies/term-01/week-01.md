## Hook
Step outside on a clear, dark night in any part of Nigeria—from the hills of Jos to the Atlantic beaches of Lagos. Look up at the sky: billions of glittering stars sparkle like diamonds across the infinite velvet blackness! Consider our planet Earth: towering mountains, roaring waterfalls like the Gurara Falls in Niger State, vast oceans teeming with millions of fish, and the human body with its beating heart that pumps blood over 100,000 times a day without stopping! How did all this breathtaking wonder come to be? Did a pile of random rocks accidentally explode and assemble themselves into birds that sing melodies, flowers that produce sweet nectar, and human beings who can write poetry and build computers? The Bible declares that behind this magnificent cosmos stands an Almighty, Sovereign, and Loving Architect—**God the Sole Creator**! Who is this Creator, what does it mean to create something out of nothing, and why did God make the universe and place human beings in it? Let us find out!

## Key Terms
- Term: Creation
- Definition: The divine, sovereign act of God bringing the entire universe, all physical matter, energy, time, and living creatures into existence out of nothing (*ex nihilo*).
- Term: Ex Nihilo
- Definition: A Latin theological phrase meaning "out of nothing," describing how God created the universe by His spoken word without using pre-existing materials.
- Term: Sovereignty of God
- Definition: The supreme, absolute, independent, and unlimited power, authority, and rulership of God over all creation, history, and the universe.
- Term: Omnipotence
- Definition: The divine attribute of God meaning all-powerful; having unlimited, infinite power to do all things according to His holy will.
- Term: Omniscience
- Definition: The divine attribute of God meaning all-knowing; possessing complete, perfect knowledge of all things past, present, and future.
- Term: Omnipresence
- Definition: The divine attribute of God meaning all-present; existing everywhere throughout the entire universe simultaneously.
- Term: Purpose of Creation
- Definition: God’s intentional design for creation: to reflect His glory, demonstrate His divine love, and provide a home for humanity to fellowship with Him and steward the earth.
- Term: Genesis
- Definition: The first book of the Holy Bible, whose title means "origin," "beginning," or "birth."
- Term: Stewardship
- Definition: The sacred responsibility and trust given by God to human beings to care for, protect, cultivate, and manage the earth and its resources wisely.
- Term: Fellowship
- Definition: Warm, intimate, loving communion and spiritual relationship between God and human beings.

1. **Creation Out of Nothing (*Creatio Ex Nihilo*):**
   - Human builders, carpenters, and sculptors can only make things by reshaping existing materials: wood, clay, cement, or steel.
   - God alone has the divine power to create *ex nihilo*—calling galaxies, light, water, and life into being where absolutely nothing existed before, solely by the power of His spoken Word (*"Let there be..."*).

2. **The Absolute Sovereignty and Attributes of God:**
   - **Sovereign Authority:** God is before all things, above all things, and owns all things (Psalm 24:1: *"The earth is the Lord’s, and the fullness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein"*).
   - **Omnipotence:** God's power is limitless; no force, creature, or obstacle can thwart His sovereign decrees.
   - **Omniscience:** God’s wisdom is infinite; He understands every law of physics, the number of hairs on each human head, and the secrets of the human heart.
   - **Omnipresence:** God is not confined to a single geographic shrine, mountain, or temple; He fills the entire cosmos with His presence.

3. **Why Did God Create the Universe? (Purpose of Creation):**
   - **To Manifest His Glory and Majesty:** The beauty of sunsets, the majesty of oceans, and the complexity of nature proclaim God’s wisdom (Psalm 19:1: *"The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament showeth his handiwork"*).
   - **To Share His Infinite Love:** God did not create out of loneliness or need. God is complete in Himself. He created the universe out of abundant, overflowing love to share life with conscious beings.
   - **To Provide a Habitation for Humanity:** Earth was meticulously crafted with an atmosphere, water cycle, fertile soil, and plant life to sustain human flourishing.
   - **To Establish Fellowship:** God designed human beings with spiritual capacity to know Him, love Him, worship Him, and communicate with Him in daily relationship.
   - **To Assign Stewardship:** God entrusted the earth to human care, commanding man to tend, nurture, and responsibly govern nature without selfish exploitation.

## Comparative Matrix
| Dimension | Human Making / Crafting | Divine Creation (*Creatio Ex Nihilo*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Source Material** | Requires pre-existing timber, metal, clay, or stone | Out of nothing; brings matter, energy, and life into existence |
| **Mechanism / Tool** | Physical tools, chisels, saws, machinery, physical labor | The authoritative spoken Word of God (*"Let there be..."*) |
| **Scope of Power** | Limited by physical laws, fatigue, and material strength | Infinite, effortless, unlimited omnipotence |
| **Duration of Existence** | Human creations decay, rust, weather, and break down | Divine laws sustain the cosmos continuously across generations |
| **Relationship to Product** | Builder is separate from and subject to natural laws | God is transcendent above creation yet intimately present within it |

## Common Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Believing that God created the universe because He was lonely and needed company.
  - *Correction:* God is eternally complete, self-sufficient, and perfect in the Holy Trinity. He created the cosmos out of pure love, generosity, and grace, not out of emotional deficiency or lack.
- **Mistake 2:** Confusing *Creation* with *Human Manufacturing*.
  - *Correction:* Humans merely assemble or transform pre-existing materials created by God. Only God can create something out of nothing (*ex nihilo*).
- **Mistake 3:** Believing that God created the world and then abandoned it to run on its own like a wound-up clock.
  - *Correction:* The Bible teaches that God actively sustains and upholds all creation every second by His sovereign power (Colossians 1:17; Hebrews 1:3).
- **Mistake 4:** Thinking that human stewardship means humans have the right to pollute and destroy the earth.
  - *Correction:* Biblical stewardship is loving care and responsible management. Ruthlessly destroying forests or polluting rivers violates God's trust and harms fellow humans.

## Worked Examples
- **Example 1: Analyzing the Meaning of Stewardship**
  - *Scenario:* A community youth group in Plateau State observes that residents are cutting down young trees along stream banks to make quick charcoal for sale, causing the local drinking stream to dry up.
  - *Biblical Application:*
    - In Genesis 2:15, God placed man in the garden to *"dress it and to keep it"* (to cultivate and guard it).
    - The youth group organizes a seminar explaining that destroying water catchments violates divine stewardship. They plant 200 bamboo seedlings along the stream banks to protect the water table, demonstrating biblical care for God's creation.
- **Example 2: Reflecting on God’s Sovereignty During Personal Anxiety**
  - *Scenario:* Emeka is anxious about starting JSS 1 in a new city away from his childhood friends. He feels small, helpless, and afraid of the future.
  - *Biblical Comfort:*
    - His mother reminds him of Genesis 1: the same God who spoke the stars into existence and set the boundaries of the oceans is sovereign over every detail of Emeka's life.
    - Because God is omnipotent (all-powerful) and omniscient (all-knowing), Emeka can pray, trust God’s guidance, and enter his new school with peace and courage.

## Guardian Sync
- **Home Discussion Point:** Take a walk with your child at sunset or nightfall. Look at the sky and surrounding trees. Ask: *“When you look at the stars and nature, how does it remind you of the greatness and love of God?”*
- **Scripture Reading Together:** Read Genesis 1:1-5 and Psalm 19:1-4 together as a family. Discuss what it means that the heavens declare the glory of God.
- **Stewardship Habit:** Encourage your child to practice godly stewardship at home by turning off leaking taps, switching off unused electric bulbs, and taking care of family plants and pets.
