## Key Terms
- Term: Creatio Ex Nihilo
- Definition: Creation out of nothing; the unique divine power of God bringing matter, energy, time, and life into existence by His spoken Word without pre-existing materials.
- Term: Sovereignty of God
- Definition: The supreme, absolute, independent power and authority of God over all creation, history, and the universe.
- Term: Six Days of Creation
- Definition: The symmetrical divine work in Genesis 1 comprising three days of forming cosmic realms (Light, Sky, Land/Seas/Plants) and three days of filling them with rulers (Luminaries, Fish/Birds, Animals/Man).
- Term: Sabbath
- Definition: The seventh day of creation week on which God rested from all His work, blessing and sanctifying it as a holy day of rest and worship.
- Term: Imago Dei
- Definition: The Image of God; the unique spiritual, intellectual, moral, and relational likeness of God bestowed upon humanity.
- Term: Breath of Life
- Definition: The divine spiritual breath (*Ruach / Neshamah*) breathed by God directly into Adam's nostrils (Genesis 2:7), transforming him into a living soul.
- Term: Garden of Eden
- Definition: The paradise sanctuary planted by God in the east, provided with fruit trees, four rivers, and the Tree of Life for human communion with God.
- Term: Help Meet (*Ezer Kenegdo*)
- Definition: The biblical description of Eve as an equal, corresponding, and indispensable partner created from Adam's rib to walk beside him in marriage.
- Term: Covenant Marriage
- Definition: The sacred, permanent, lifelong covenant union between one man and one woman ordained by God in creation for companionship, procreation, and mutual support.
- Term: "Leaving and Cleaving"
- Definition: The biblical pattern in Genesis 2:24 where a man establishes an independent family unit ("leaving") and joins in unbreakable loyalty with his wife ("cleaving").

1. **God as the Sole Creator (Week 01):**
   - God is before all things and owns all things (Psalm 24:1). He created the universe out of nothing (*ex nihilo*) by His authoritative spoken Word.
   - His divine nature is revealed through His **Omnipotence** (unlimited power), **Omniscience** (perfect knowledge), **Omnipresence** (present everywhere), and supreme **Sovereignty**.
   - God created the cosmos to manifest His glory, share His infinite love, provide a home for humanity, establish loving fellowship, and appoint man as steward of the earth.

2. **The Six Days of Creation and the Sabbath (Week 02):**
   - Symmetrical progression: Days 1–3 of *Forming* (Day 1: Light/Darkness; Day 2: Firmament/Sky; Day 3: Land, Seas, Vegetation) correspond to Days 4–6 of *Filling* (Day 4: Sun/Moon/Stars; Day 5: Marine life and Birds; Day 6: Land animals and Mankind).
   - Creation was pronounced "very good" on Day 6.
   - On the Seventh Day, God rested—not from exhaustion, but because creation was complete—blessing and sanctifying the Sabbath to institute a rhythm of rest and worship for humanity.

3. **The Creation of Man in Genesis 2 (Week 03):**
   - God formed Adam from the dust of the ground (*Adamah*) and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, making him a living soul with a spiritual dimension.
   - Placed in the Garden of Eden to cultivate and guard it, and given the commandment not to eat from the Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil on pain of death.
   - Eve was formed from Adam's rib to be an equal, complementary partner (*help meet*), demonstrating equal dignity and mutual partnership.

4. **Marriage and Human Relationships (Week 04):**
   - God identified loneliness as the first "not good" state in creation.
   - Ordained marriage for companionship, godly procreation, and mutual support.
   - Established the three-pillar marital blueprint of Genesis 2:24: **Leaving** (establishing independent family loyalty), **Cleaving** (permanent lifelong commitment), and **Becoming One Flesh** (spiritual, emotional, and physical unity).

## Common Mistakes
- **Mistake 1:** Viewing Genesis 1 and Genesis 2 as conflicting, contradictory myths.
  - *Correction:* They are complementary perspectives: Genesis 1 gives the cosmic, panoramic timeline, while Genesis 2 focuses intimately on humanity, Eden, and marriage.
- **Mistake 2:** Assuming that God rested on the seventh day to sleep and recover strength.
  - *Correction:* God is all-powerful and never faints or grows weary (Isaiah 40:28). God rested because His creative work was complete and perfect, setting an example for human rest.
- **Mistake 3:** Believing that human beings are merely advanced animals.
  - *Correction:* Human beings were uniquely created in the image of God (*Imago Dei*) and received the breath of life directly from God's mouth, possessing an eternal soul and moral conscience.
- **Mistake 4:** Thinking that marriage is a modern human invention.
  - *Correction:* Marriage was established by God in the Garden of Eden before any nation, law, or cultural tradition existed.

## Worked Examples
- **Example 1: Demonstrating Environmental Stewardship from Creation Principles**
  - *Scenario:* A community in Benue State is suffering from soil erosion because residents cut down trees along hillsides for firewood and dump trash into local streams.
  - *Biblical Resolution:*
    1. Social Studies and CRS students cite Genesis 2:15, where God commanded man to *"till and keep"* the earth, and Psalm 24:1 (*"The earth is the Lord's"*).
    2. The youth explain that polluting streams and clear-felling trees is a sin against God's creation trust.
    3. The community launches an afforestation project, planting 500 seedlings and creating a designated waste disposal pit. Environmental health is restored.
- **Example 2: Applying the Marital Blueprint to Family Harmony**
  - *Scenario:* A married couple constantly argues because the husband allows his extended family to make decisions regarding his children's schooling without consulting his wife.
  - *Biblical Counseling:*
    - The pastor references Genesis 2:24: *"A man shall leave his father and mother and cleave unto his wife."*
    - The husband learns that while honoring parents is a sacred duty, marital unity requires that decisions regarding his household must be made jointly with his wife as "one flesh."
    - *Outcome:* The couple unites in mutual communication, and domestic peace is restored.
