What is the Difference Between Theft, Burglary, and Robbery?

While ordinary people often use these terms interchangeably, the law makes sharp, critical distinctions based on how the crime was executed:

```

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

│ DISTINGUISHING THEFT, BURGLARY, AND ARMED ROBBERY │

├────┬─────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

│ S/N│ Criminal Offense │ Legal Definition & Modus Operandi (Criminal Code) │

├────┼─────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

│ 1 │ Theft (Stealing)│ Unlawfully taking property *without force and without │

│ │ │ breaking* (e.g., picking a wallet in a bus or stealing a │

│ │ │ phone left on a classroom desk). │

├────┼─────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

│ 2 │ Burglary │ Unlawfully breaking and entering a dwelling house at │

│ │ │ night with intent to steal (e.g., cutting window burglar │

│ │ │ bars and entering a home while residents sleep). │

├────┼─────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

│ 3 │ Housebreaking │ The exact same crime as burglary (breaking and entering), │

│ │ │ but committed during daylight hours. │

├────┼─────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

│ 4 │ Armed Robbery │ Stealing property directly from a victim *by using deadly │

│ │ │ weapons (guns, machetes) or threats of physical violence*. │

└────┴─────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

```

Common Methods Used by Burglars (Modus Operandi)

Criminals rarely attack homes that appear difficult, noisy, or risky to enter. They exploit easy vulnerabilities using specific techniques:

1.
Prying and Forcing Flimsy Doors: Using heavy iron crowbars to force door frames where cheap, hollow wooden doors or flimsy surface locks are installed.
2.
Attacking Louver Windows: Removing exterior glass louvers to reach inside and flip open the window latch, or using small hydraulic car jacks to bend iron burglar bars silently.
3.
Exploiting Spare Keys: Searching under doormats, flowerpots, electrical meter boxes, or above door lintels where careless homeowners hide spare keys.
4.
Insider Information: Gathering intelligence from disgruntled domestic workers, discharged guards, or dishonest artisans (plumbers, painters) about when the family travels or where valuables are kept.
5.
Social Media Reconnaissance: Monitoring social media posts where families announce: "Off to Dubai for two weeks!" Criminals know the house is completely vacant.

---

Home and Personal Security Vulnerabilities

Poor Nighttime Lighting: Dark yards and unlit alleys provide concealment for intruders.
Overgrown Shrubbery: Tall hedges and unpruned trees right next to windows conceal intruders while they work on locks.
Careless Display of Wealth: Discarding luxury appliance packaging (e.g., large 75-inch OLED TV boxes or generator cartons) on the street corner, advertising wealth to passing scouts.
Unsecured Boundary Walls: Fences without razor wire, broken perimeter gates, and weak padlocks.

---

Effective Preventative Measures (Target Hardening)

1.
Install Heavy Deadbolt Locks: Replace simple spring-latch locks on exterior doors with solid steel deadbolts that cannot be slipped with a plastic card or pried with a screwdriver.
2.
Reinforce Window Burglar Proofs: Ensure iron burglar proofs are welded deeply into the concrete blockwork, not merely screwed into wooden window frames. Fasten glass louvers with security clips.
3.
Motion-Sensor Floodlights: Install automated lights that turn on instantly when motion is detected around boundary walls and backyard doors.
4.
Neighborhood Security Integration: Join the local neighborhood watch, establish mutual surveillance with trusted neighbors, and register with the local community vigilante.
5.
Practice Social Media Discretion: Never post live holiday photos or announce family travel dates online. Post vacation photos only after you have safely returned home!

Home Application & Guardian Sync

- Home Perimeter Audit: Walk the perimeter of your compound with your child. Check every window: Are the burglar bars firmly embedded in the concrete? Are any louvers loose or missing? - Lock Verification Routine: Establish a nightly security routine where you or your child verify that all deadbolts, padlocks, and gates are securely fastened by 9:00 PM. - Social Media Safety Rule: Establish a strict family rule against posting real-time vacation travel updates or pictures showing the exterior layout of your home on social media.
