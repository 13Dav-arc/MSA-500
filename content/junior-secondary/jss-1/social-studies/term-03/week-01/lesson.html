Why This Matters to You

Imagine walking through a bustling Nigerian city. You see magnificent glass-walled commercial banks, flyover expressways buzzing with modern cars, and high-tech telecommunication towers beaming fast 5G internet signals into millions of smartphones. Nigeria has made breathtaking strides in technology and urban development! But look a little closer beneath the glittering surface: why are secondary school principals caught sneaking leaked test question sheets to students in exam halls? Why do young university undergraduates join violent secret groups that terrorize campuses in the dead of night? Why are young lives tragically cut short by preventable diseases like HIV/AIDS and substance addiction? These painful challenges that plague modern human coexistence are known as Contemporary Social Problems! What are they, why did they emerge with rapid urbanization and foreign cultural contact, and what role can our generation play in confronting them? Let us find out!

Contemporary Social Problems I: Core Concepts and Pedagogical Illustration

Contemporary social problems are not ancient biological curses; they are modern socio-economic challenges that emerged from rapid urbanization, population explosions, unchecked media influence, breakdown of traditional family values, and the greedy scramble for quick wealth:

1.
Examination Malpractice:
One of the most destructive academic vices in Nigeria. Involves bringing unauthorized notes ("micro-chips" or "expo") into exam halls, impersonation, collusion with corrupt supervisors, and operating illegal "miracle centers."
Destroys academic integrity, devalues Nigerian educational certificates internationally, and produces unqualified professionals (e.g., incompetent doctors or collapsing building engineers).
1.
Cultism:
Originated in 1952 at University College, Ibadan, as a peaceful, non-violent student club (the Pyrates Confraternity) advocating social justice and African cultural pride.
Degenerated in later decades into violent, ritualistic, armed secret gangs that engage in turf wars, extortion, political thuggery, assassinations, and terror on campuses and street corners.
1.
HIV/AIDS Epidemic:
Human Immunodeficiency Virus attacks the body's immune system, specifically CD4 helper T-lymphocytes.
Transmitted through unprotected sexual intercourse with an infected partner, transfusion of unscreened blood, sharing unsterilized sharp objects (needles, razor blades, clippers), and mother-to-child transmission during pregnancy, delivery, or breastfeeding.
Stigmatization and discrimination against carriers remain serious social challenges, hindering testing and treatment.
1.
Drug and Substance Abuse:
Rampant abuse of prescription opioids (Tramadol, codeine cough syrups), cannabis, synthetic chemical mixtures ("Colorado", "Arizona"), and volatile inhalants (glue, fuel).
Leads to acute psychosis, brain damage, kidney and liver failure, family violence, and criminal delinquency.
1.
Terrorism, Militancy, and Human Trafficking:
Insurgencies disrupting peace and displacing millions of innocent farmers into Internally Displaced Persons (IDP) camps.
Human traffickers deceiving vulnerable rural girls with promises of foreign hairdressing jobs, only to trap them in sexual exploitation and forced labor across desert routes.

Home Application & Guardian Sync

- Home Discussion Point: Discuss the dangers of get-rich-quick mindsets with your child. Ask: “Why is honest, patient labor with integrity far better than cheating on an exam or engaging in cyber fraud?” - Health and Hygiene Vigilance: Review personal hygiene rules at home: never share toothbrushes, razor blades, or sharp instruments. Discuss how staying informed about public health protects the family. - Open Communication: Encourage your child to share any strange peer groups or secret invitations they encounter at school, assuring them of parental love and protection.
