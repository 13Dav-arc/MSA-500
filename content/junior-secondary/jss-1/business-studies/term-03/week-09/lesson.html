Punctuation marks are the road signs of language, and mastering their precise spacing rules is the final hallmark of an elite typist! 3. Line Spacing Standards in Corporate Formatting

Line spacing controls the vertical distance between lines of text:

Single Line Spacing (1.0): Standard for routine business letters, memos, and invoices where compactness is required.
One-and-a-Half Line Spacing (1.5): Preferred in academic essays and formal business reports for enhanced reading comfort.
Double Line Spacing (2.0): Mandatory for draft manuscripts, legal documents, and school examination submissions to allow space for handwritten annotations and corrections.
Triple Line Spacing (3.0): Used primarily between major section headings or title display banners.
