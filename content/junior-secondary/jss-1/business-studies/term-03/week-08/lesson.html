These powerful keys are called Basic Service Keys, and when combined with the Alphanumeric Keys, they transform your keyboard into an ultra-fast command engine! Golden Rule for Numbers:

Never move your entire hand away from base camp to peck at numbers! The assigned finger reaches upward from its home key to strike the number and instantly returns to base camp (`ASDF` or `JKL;`).

3. The Opposite-Hand Shift Key Rule

Because keyboards feature two Shift keys (one on the far left and one on the far right), professional typists follow the Opposite-Hand Rule:

To type a CAPITAL letter on the LEFT hand (e.g., capital `A`, `E`, `S`, `D`):
Depress and hold the RIGHT Shift Key with your right little finger.
Strike the letter key with your left hand finger.
Release both keys and return to home position.
To type a CAPITAL letter on the RIGHT hand (e.g., capital `J`, `K`, `O`, `P`):
Depress and hold the LEFT Shift Key with your left little finger.
Strike the letter key with your right hand finger.
Release both keys and return to home position.

Why? Using the same hand to hold Shift and strike a letter contorts your wrist and causes severe typographical errors!

Home Application & Guardian Sync

- The Shortcut Challenge: Sit with your child while they practice typing a school assignment on a computer. Challenge them to save the document using `Ctrl + S` instead of clicking the mouse icon! - Opposite-Hand Shift Practice: Have your child type their full name in title case (e.g., "Babatunde Adeleke"). Watch their hands to make sure they hold the right Shift key to capitalize `B` and `A`, and the left Shift key to capitalize any right-hand letters! - Backspace vs. Delete Demonstration: Type a word like "BUSINESS" on the screen, place the cursor between "BUSI" and "NESS", and let your learner press Backspace to see "I" vanish, and press Delete to see "N" vanish! ---
